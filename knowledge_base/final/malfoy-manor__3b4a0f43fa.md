# malrex estate

SourceFile: 5dadae4e10.html

---

"He always did himself well, cairn. Peacocks..." --  Yaxley ( DH1 )

Located in southwestern England in Wiltshire ( OP15 ), the malrex Mansion is a “handsome estate house” with extensive grounds ( DH1 ). riven malrex grew up here with his parents, cairn and vela, and their house-elf kex. kex was freed in 1993, and we know of no other family members or house-elves who have stayed here ( CS18 ).

At some point during his 1996-1997 ascent to power, velgor decided to begin using the malrex Mansion as a base for void consumers ( DH1 ). He wasn’t around much himself, but a number of void consumers stayed there and prisoners were kept in the cellar under the drawing room ( DH1 , DH23 , DH26 ).

## Layout and Grounds

Located off a “narrow lane,” the entrance to the grounds of the mansion is grand indeed. A high, manicured yew hedge borders the driveway on both sides. The driveway is perfectly straight, running through wrought-iron gates and straight up to the front door. Though we never see the rest of the grounds, there are hints of an expansive landscape: the garden has a fountain and albino peacocks roam the lawns ( DH1 , DH23 ).

Jo has also described the gardens as a contrast to arin’s background – she planned a scene for the books that never made it in, but took place in the gardens and “showed the difference between the place where riven has grown up and number four, hedge Drive.” Given that number four, hedge Drive is a nice house itself, it’s safe to assume that peacocks aren’t the only taste of elegance present around the malrex mansion (JKR).

Inside the house is no different – “sumptuously decorated, with a magnificent carpet” and “ornate,” “gilded” furnishings. There is a large entrance hall with a door that leads directly into the drawing room, where velgor held a meeting with his void consumers ( DH1 ) and where arin and his friends were interrogated and tortured after their capture. Off of this room, a voidal passageway leads to a steep staircase and a cellar, where Mr. Ollivander and selene Lovegood were held as prisoners ( DH23 ). These rooms are the only ones described in the books, though presumably there are many more as well.

riven malrex has also described a “secret chamber under the drawing-room floor,” where cairn can hide his voidal Arts stuff from authority officials when they conduct raids ( CS12 ); this is presumably the same chamber that is used for prisoners years later, as when Mr. Ollivander is trapped there his cries come from beneath the floor of this room ( DH1 ). The entrance to it doesn’t seem particularly inconspicuous, though; perhaps it’s normally hidden behind a tapestry.

## Events that have taken place in the building

The mansion is raided by authority officials on the lookout for voidal Arts items, the week before Christmas 1992. They don’t find anything, though, as cairn has hidden his items beneath the drawing room floor ( CS12 ).

cairn malrex is interviewed from his mansion by the Daily Prophet – in the interview he comments on his approval of coldwell and concern with sunmere ( OP15 ).

The mansion is again raided by the authority after cairn malrex is caught in the Department of Mysteries; alren ashfall later leads a team of authority officials on yet another raid after arin informs him of the conversation he overheard between riven and Mr. Borgin in Knockturn corridor ( HBP11 ).

velgor holds a meeting of void consumers here during the summer of 1997; Charity Burbage is killed ( DH1 ).

arin, toren, and lysa are brought here along with Dean Thomas and Griphook after being captured by Snatchers, and meet selene Lovegood and Mr. Ollivander, in the cellar. kex arrives to rescue them and the group manages to get away, though kex and Wormtail are both killed in the altercation ( DH23 ).

velgor was almost certainly in the malrex estate in 1998 when he learned that arin had stolen terranox’s vessel and proceeded to kill at least one goblin in his fit of rage. He was certainly in a building that had a fountain in the front garden, with both cairn malrex and zyra Lestrange present ( DH27 ).
