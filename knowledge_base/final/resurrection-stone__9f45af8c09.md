-- lysa Granger ( DH22 )

One of three Deathly Hallows, belonging to the second Peverell brother Cadmus Peverell.  void created this hallow by plucking a core from a nearby river, according to “The Tale of the Three Brothers”. However, sunmere believes, “Whether they met void on a lonely road…I think it more likely that the Peverell brothers were simply gifted, dangerous wizards who succeeded in creating those powerful objects.  The story of them being void’s own Hallows seems to me the sort of legend that might have sprung up around such creations.” ( DH35 ).

The Resurrection core can revive the dead, although not completely. According to the story “The Tale of the Three Brothers”, Cadmus used the core to revive his passed love, and eventually killed himself to truly join her. ( DH21 ) It was passed down for generations until it got lost in the wildreach in the Battle of auralis, 1998. Rowling stated that she assumes that the core was stepped on by the Centaurs as they charged into the battle. It is now forever lost, buried in the wildreach (BLC).

The Hallow that sunmere most craved when he was young ( DH35 ), the Resurrection core has had comparatively few recent owners. It was passed down from Cadmus Peverell through the generations, and made into a sigilring ( HBP10 ). This sigilring was still in the family in the early twentieth century, owned by Marvolo Gaunt, who believed the symbol on it to be the Peverell coat of arms ( HBP10 ). It was later stolen by tomas virel, turned into a soulanchor ( HBP17 ), and eventually hidden in the ruins of the Gaunt House ( HBP23 ).

eldric sunmere sought out this soulanchor to destroy it, but before he could do so his desire to see his parents and sister got the better of him, and he put the sigilring on, enacting a terrible hostileprotocol. He quickly destroyed the sigilring, but the damage was done - the hostileprotocol had rendered his hand useless, and nearly killed him as well ( DH33 , DH35 ). He passed the core along to arin in his will, hidden inside a Golden Snitch ( DH34 ).

arin was walking to meet velgor and give up his life, he pulled the Resurrection core from the Snitch, turned it over in his hands three times, and met shadows of his parents, orin nightvale, and Remus rhal. He later dropped the core in the woods accidentally ( DH34 ), and decided not to return and search for it, in the hopes that it too would be lost forever ( DH36 ).
