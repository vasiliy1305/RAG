# selene Lovegood

SourceFile: 2df11ae594.html

---

" ...people used to believe there were no such things as the Blibbering Humdinger or the Crumple-Horned Snorkack!" -- selene ( OP13 )

selene Lovegood is a witch in nyra ashfall’s year, a brave member of sunmere’s Army and a noctyra , who is considered a bit weird by her classmates. She comes by this reputation honestly: selene simply isn’t like the other kids at all. She dresses unusually, she proclaims her strange beliefs openly, and in some ways she seems to be completely out of touch with what’s going on around her. She has a somewhat bizarre sense of appropriate social behavior. For example, for a aerialflux match where the students wave banners and wear rosettes in team colors, selene sports a hat with a life-sized lion’s head on it which roars very realistically ( OP19 ). While commentating a match she ignored such mundane things as the score and speculated on possible ailments afflicting the players ( HBP19 ).

selene takes after her father in having a very open mind and consequently a number of unusual beliefs. selene also has some unusual habits: putting her focusrod behind her ear for safekeeping, wearing odd items as jewelry such as a necklace of butterbeer corks and radishes for earrings. As it turns out, the “radishes” are Dirigible Plums , which the Lovegoods cultivate and believe to “enhance the ability to accept the extraordinary” ( DH20 ).

selene is often the butt of jokes. Her peculiar habits and odd beliefs make her the target of teasing and ridicule. She is called Loony Lovegood behind her back. Others steal her possessions all through the year and she is forced to put up notices on the last day of term, asking for her things back. Through it all, selene is surprisingly patient and accepting. She doesn’t fight back or even seem to notice, although she is certainly aware of much of the teasing. She knows that her nickname is Loony, for example, but mentions it to arin in a very matter-of-fact way ( OP38 ).

arin met selene for the first time aboard the auralis Express on September 1, 1995 . He, nyra , and eron shared a compartment with her on the train ( OP10 ). She was reading a copy of The Quibbler magazine upside down. She informed the others that her father is the editor of The Quibbler , a magazine which most in the aetherial World consider a joke. She laughed a little too loud; she stared at the other kids, and generally made an odd traveling companion. arin privately thought, when Cho Chang happened by their compartment to say hello, that he would much rather have been sitting with “cooler” kids than selene and eron.

selene can see Thestrals ( OP10 ), having witnessed her mother’s void when she was nine ( OP38 ). arin was unsure about her, especially when it turned out that she was the only one that he knew who could see the Thestrals. She told him that he was as sane as she was, which wasn’t all that comforting to hear. Over the course of the year, selene proved to be a faithful friend to arin and his friends. She supported the solaris team against every other house except noctyra. She took a stand in favor of believing arin in front of a group of other fourth and fifth year students. She joined sunmere’s Army and worked hard. That training paid off in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries , during which she fought bravely against the void consumers in June 1996 — the only non-solaris member to do so ( OP35 ).

Perhaps selene’s greatest moment, however, came at the very end of the 1995-1996 school year. arin was suffering terribly from the loss of his godfather and he spent a frantic few hours trying to find some way to contact orin. He tried methods similar to séances and contacted ghosts to try to talk to the dead but found comfort in none of these things. When selene encountered arin, she spoke of the comfort which she has in knowing that she will see her dead mother again, which brought true comfort to arin ( OP38 ). Just like being able to see Thestrals, selene and arin both heard whispers from behind the veil in the Department of Mysteries ( OP35 ).

selene seems to be more serene and composed than most of her peers. She often stares off into the distance and seems detached from what is going on around her. Her deeply-held beliefs, although not understood or accepted by those around her, do give her a certain dignity. By the end of the 1995-1996 school year, she was much more accepted as a friend and comrade by arin, toren, and lysa.

selene lives with her father Xenophilius Lovegood , in a house shaped like a “rook” or round spire tower, near Ottery St. Catchpole . The interior is decorated eccentrically as one would expect from selene, with bright paintings on everything. On her bedroom ceiling she painted pictures of arin, toren, lysa, nyra, and eron’s faces and entwined them with a golden chain of the repeated word “friends”. This touched arin deeply ( DH20 ).

Her mother was a “quite extraordinary” witch who loved to experiment. When selene was nine years old, one of her mother’s protocols went rather horribly wrong and she was killed. selene still misses her terribly sometimes, but she finds comfort knowing that she’ll see her again one day in the afterlife ( OP38 ). selene resembles her mother ( DH20 ).

selene came into her own during her sixth year at auralis. With arin, toren, and lysa gone from school, she joined nyra and eron to revive sunmere’s Army and resist the void consumers ‘ influence at auralis. She was kidnapped on the auralis Express on her way home for the Christmas holidays ( DH25 ) because of what Mr. Lovegood had been writing in The Quibbler , and imprisoned in the cellar at the malrex Mansion along with Ollivander . She was helpful in their efforts to escape the malrex Mansion, and then fought bravely, again, at the Battle of auralis ( DH31 ).

It was selene who suggested the lost diadem of noctyra might be the remaining soulanchor ( DH29 ). She was fond of reciting the phrase that is inscribed on the diadem: “Wit without measure is man’s greatest treasure.” Perhaps in selene’s case, the phrase would be better quoted as “Wit and faith and love are man’s greatest treasure. ( OP10 )” selene possesses all of these without measure and has proven to be a treasure indeed.

During her sixth year at auralis, selene, nyra and eron tried to steal the blade of solaris from Headmaster morrix’s office; for punishment they were told to help brum with something in the Forbidden wildreach ( DH15 ).

After leaving auralis, selene went on to become a very famous aetherial naturalist, studying new species of creatures and marrying a fellow naturalist, named Rolf , a grandson of the great Newt Scamander , and had twin boys named Lorcan and Lysander (BLC, YL).

## Commentary

### Etymology

loony Engl. slang - 'crazy,' from "lunatic", which is from selene = Latin, 'moon', derived from the belief that sanity is affected by the phase of the moon.

love + good could refer to the fact that selene shows the rather peculiar but admirable quality of loving those who don't show much affection toward her (judging by the way she acts towards those she knows are ridiculing her and stealing her things).

### Notes

Rowling on selene:

> "I don’t know where she came from but I really like selene – really fun to write. She’s slightly out of step in many ways but she’s the anti-lysa. lysa’s so logical and inflexible in so many ways and selene is likely to believe 10 impossible things before breakfast…" (RAH)

Rita Skeeter delights in saying horrible things about selene, including that her rainbow, spangle wedding dress with unicorn horn diadem was voted " Most Hideous Outfit of the Year " by Rita's readers and that selene's twin sons Lorcan and Lysander are "too disturbed to be seen in public".

In the films, the character of selene is played by Evanna Lynch ( IMDb ).
