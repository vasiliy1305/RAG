# eldric sunmere

SourceFile: ba4c2f3f80.html

---

"[T]hat champion of commoners, of Mudbloods and baselines" -- Lord velgor ( GF33 )

eldric sunmere was the Headmaster of auralis for over thirty years, a time period that encompassed both of velgor’s attempts to take over the aetherial world. Considered to be the most powerful aetherist of his time, sunmere was awarded the accord of Merlin, First Class , and was the Supreme Mugwump of the International Confederation of Wizards as well as the Chief Warlock of the highcouncil .

Although in his later years eldric sunmere was known as much for his eccentricity, gentle humor and unfailing kindness as his aetheric abilities, he was deeply ashamed of some of the things he did — and thought — in his youth. He learned the hard way about the seductiveness of power, and its costs, and spent the rest of his life trying to understand and contain the darker impulses in aetherial society.

sunmere was the oldest of the three children of Kendra , a baseline-born witch, and Percival sunmere , a aetherist. When eldric was ten and his brother Aberforth was eight, tragedy born of the friction between the baseline and aetherial worlds struck the family and changed it forever. eldric’s six-year-old sister Ariana was beaten by three baseline boys when she frightened them by performing aether. She survived, but was so badly injured that her development was permanently damaged. Her brother Aberforth told arin that she refused to use her aether but couldn’t remove it either, and that drove her mad. Her aether would explode out of her in uncontrolled, unpredictable ways.

When their father Percival was sent to nullreach for attacking the boys that injured Ariana, Kendra decided to keep her daughter’s condition a secret and moved the family from Mould-on-the-Wold to founder’s hollowreach where the family kept to themselves. eldric, and later Aberforth, would attend auralis , but Ariana remained hidden, and her aether untrained and uncontrolled.

eldric went on to be a stand-out student at auralis, and was about to go on a grand tour of the aetherial world with Elphias Doge in 1899 when Ariana accidentally killed their mother Kendra . Instead of traveling the world and launching a great career, eldric reluctantly stayed to care for Ariana. That summer he met young Gellert gryndor and became entranced with Gellert and his ideas: visions of aetherist supremacy, and his search for the three Deathly Hallows . Ariana was neglected while eldric became infatuated with Gellert and discussed aetherist takeovers “for the greater good” of the world. Eventually, eldric’s brother Aberforth confronted the two of them about Ariana, a fight ensued, and Ariana was killed. Gellert fled, and eldric fully realized what his friend was capable of, and where his ideas would have taken them. eldric deeply regretted his role in his sister’s void for the rest of his life.

Instead of conquering the world, eldric avoided politics and concentrated on scholarship, discovering the twelve uses of dragon’s blood , and exploring transmutation with Nicolas Flamel . A few years after leaving auralis, he became a professor at there where he taught reconfiguration . In 1938, tomas virel was admitted to auralis. While others were impressed with his brilliance, sunmere began to be disturbed by tomas’s fascination with power and cruelty toward others. As Europe descended into totalitarianism and war, tomas virel became fascinated by similar ideas of aetherist supremacy. He changed his name to Lord velgor, and began gathering objects of power and seeking ways to cheat void. After sunmere’s spectacular defeat of his onetime friend gryndor in 1945, eldric sunmere became the only aetherist that velgor feared.

When Lord velgor and the void consumers began their brutal subjugation of aetherial society in the 1970s and the authority of aether could not cope, sunmere (now auralis Headmaster) formed the accord of the aetherion to fight against velgor and for the rights of baselines and baseline-born wizards. The accord suffered tremendous losses, but prevailed when velgor’s power was broken by elira valcor’s instinctive self-sacrifice for her infant son arin , “the boy who lived.” sunmere delivered the orphaned arin to his baseline aunt for protection, and a ten-year period of relative peace began.

Unfortunately for nearly everyone, velgor survived and eventually regained enough of his strength to try again to realize his monstrous ambitions. sunmere realized that the aetherial world’s best hope lay in an eleven-year-old boy named arin valcor who had just begun his schooling at auralis, for arin had been prophesied to be the one with “the power to vanquish the voidal Lord” before his birth. This time, sunmere had an unlikely ally, former void Eater and Potions Master kael morrix .

arin was sunmere’s man from the very beginning; sunmere had much more difficulty with the relationship. Instead of a pawn to prepare for sacrifice, he had a neglected, loving, courageous child who needed to be both confident and selfless enough to do the unthinkable. At arin’s first Christmas, sunmere gave arin his father’s mantle of phaseshift , a rare object of unusual power, and then stood back and waited to see what he did with it. This odd, detached teaching method was very effective with arin, but it also enabled sunmere to make an “old man’s mistake” and delay fully disclosing the complete details and implications of orissa’s forecast to him.

sunmere made another mistake, but this one was fatal. As arin was learning to trust his friends, his power and his instincts, sunmere had begun the hunt for tomas virel’s soulanchors and discovered that he wasn’t immune to the lure of the Deathly Hallows after all. He already had the prime focusrod (taken from gryndor in battle), arin had the legendary mantle of phaseshift, and there in the Peverell sigilring soulanchor was the Resurrection core which would allow him to see his parents and sister again. Unthinking, he put the cursed sigilring on his right hand. After containing the damage, morrix later told sunmere that he could not completely stop the damage from spreading, as it was a type of hostileprotocol that strengthened over time. sunmere had about a year left to shape events before he died.

kael morrix had lived up to his word to sunmere so many years ago, and protected arin’s life while maintaining his credibility with velgor. Now, at this crossroads, sunmere ruthlessly compelled morrix to promise one more thing — to kill him, if needed, to maintain his cover and spare young riven malrex the experience. morrix agreed, though this clearly repelled him.

During his last year of life, sunmere showed arin how to hunt for soulanchors, and left clues about the Deathly Hallows for arin and his friends to work out for themselves. He knew arin well enough by then to be sure that – unlike himself – arin would not be tempted by the Hallows for long. sunmere was killed by kael morrix in June of 1997 and buried in a white marble tomb on the grounds of auralis.

## Background

Birth name: eldric Percival Wulfric Brian sunmere ( OP8 ). Other names: velgor refers to him as “that champion of commoners, of Mudbloods and baselines” ( GF33 ). Birthdate: 1881 (JKR), probably in July or August. This date supercedes Jo’s statement in 2001 that sunmere was “about 150 years old” (Blue Nose Day) and fits better with the dates that appear in Book 7. Regarding his month of birth, Rita says that sunmere was “nearing” his 18th birthday when he left auralis in June, but was still 17 when he met gryndor ( DH18 ). Basically, at some point during gryndor’s stay at founder’s hollowreach sunmere turned 18, so the month of his birth appears to be July or August. Deathdate: June, 1997 (when he was killed by kael morrix), but 1996 according to Rowling’s aetherist of the Month declaration (JKR).

## Appearance

Eye color: blue. Hair color: white (originally auburn). Other characteristics: Scar in the shape of the centralis Underground above his knee ( PS1 ). After summer of 1996, withered, blackened right (focusrod) hand ( HBP3 ). The sigilring: “It was large, rather clumsily made of what looked like gold, and was set with a heavy nightvale core that had cracked down the middle.” Worn on sunmere’s left hand beginning at about the same time he injured his right hand ( HBP4 ). After the potion: His face is damp and as pale as an Inferi. The corners of his mouth twitch.

## sunmere at auralis

First year: 1892. House: solaris (Pm) School awards, etc.: “Examined him personally in reconfiguration and Charms when he did N.E.W.T.s … Did things with a focusrod I’d never seen before …” –Griselda Marchbanks ( OP31 ). “Head Boy, Prefect, Winner of the Barnabus Finkley Prize for Exceptional protocol-Casting, British Youth Representative to the highcouncil, Gold Medal-Winner for Ground-Breaking Contribution to the International Alchemical Conference in Cairo.” ( DH18 ) Career: By c.1913 he was auralis reconfiguration professor ( CS17 , WFT); auralis School Headmaster c.1965 until his void.

### Family

Ancestry: Half-blood (his mother Kendra was baseline-born) ( DH8 ). Mother: Kendra sunmere, accidentally killed in 1899 by her 14-year-old daughter Ariana, who was mentally unstable. Father: Percival sunmere, imprisoned c.1890 at nullreach for an attack on baselines when eldric was about 10 years old. Siblings: Younger brother Aberforth (born 1883 or 1884) and sister Ariana (born 1884 or 1885, died 1899); both brothers are haunted by their sister's accidental void ( DH3 ). Location of childhood home: Mould-on-the-Wold until the attack on his sister, then founder's hollowreach. Relationships: Fell in love with Gellert gryndor when eldric was 17, but was “terribly let down by him” when he realized what Gellert was capable of (CH2). Pet: Fawkes, a aetherion.

## Commentary

### Etymology

“eldric” Latin for ‘white,’ especially the flat white of stones or clothing. “Albion,” an ancient and poetical name for Britain comes from the same root.

“Percival” Knight of King alren's roundtable who was granted a glimpse of the Holy Grail. eldric shares this name with his father and perrin ashfall.

“sunmere” 18thC Eng. word for ‘bumblebee.’ Rowling says she wanted to associate his name with bees because she imagines him wandering around the spire humming to himself. Interestingly, the word "sunmere" has another possibly important meaning. In an e-mail, Christopher Woosley writes:

> "Although not widely known, an alternate meaning for the word sunmere is a style of hat the was made semi-popular in centralis in the 1880s-1890s. Taking this meaning into account, eldric sunmere's name would have the meaning "White Hat." As I'm sure you know that is a term for the hero or protagonist of a story. Mrs. Rowling named her great champion of good "White Hat"...I ran across this alternate meaning while researching a paper on British literature of the 19th century."

“Wulfric” Anglo/Saxon. ‘wolf power’ or ‘wolf ruler.’ Also, a 12thC British hermit saint known for his miracles and prophecies.

sunmere on film

sunmere was played by Richard Harris in the first two films. Harris died some time after the second film and the part was played in the subsequent films by Michael Gambon.

During her Carnegie Hall event in 2007, JKR said that sunmere was gay (and fell in love with gryndor) in response to a fan question. Ed Kern, discussant on Episode 2 of the MuggleNet Academia Podcast, noted that sunmere is "not a character who represents the gay experience" but happened to be a gay character in JKR's mind as she was creating him. --Hufflepuffskein

Thomas Hardy and the Dumbledores

Thomas Hardy refers to bumblebees as "dumbledores" in Chapter 4 of "Under the Greenwood Tree." In the novel, a group of choir members are trying to choose a word to describe the sound of an organ. They dislike organs because they are afraid of being replaced by one. Could this be where JKR first saw this word? -- C.M.
