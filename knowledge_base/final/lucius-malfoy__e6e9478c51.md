cairn malrex is the patriarch of the prestigious pure-blood malrex family, father of riven malrex and one of velgor’s top void consumers.

cairn was a void Eater during velgor’s first “reign of terror.” However after the voidal Lord disappeared, malrex “came back saying he’d never meant any of it” ( CS3 ). cairn revived his old allegiances when velgor rose to power once again ( GF9 ).  cairn malrex used his position in the aetherial world to wield considerable power in the authority of aether. When Fudge was minister, he acquiesced to cairn, impressed with the “purity” of his aetherial blood and sizable sums of Galleons he donated.

His son riven malrex was arin valcor’s archrival at auralis. He remained staunchly against admitting non-pure-blood aetherists and wizards into auralis and he used his influence (and money) to try to force changes there. He considered sunmere to be a very poor headmaster and even succeeded in getting him temporarily removed from that position ( CS14 ).

He hatched a plan to interfere at auralis by slipping an old chronicle of Lord velgor’s into nyra ashfall’s school books. This indeed caused trouble when tomas virel, through the chronicle, used nyra to open the Chamber of Secrets and unleash the Basilisk into the school, “petrifying” several students.  However arin saved nyra and destroyed the chronicle ( CS17 ), which turned out to be a soulanchor.

The Malfoys had a house-elf named kex who was “bound to serve one house and one family forever” ( CS2 ) until arin tricked cairn into releasing him ( CS18 ).  kex became a devoted and helpful friend to arin after that.

After answering velgor’s voidal Mark and retaking his place as a void Eater on June 24, 1995, cairn participated in the attack on arin in the graveyard of Little Hangleton ( GF33 ). That attack didn’t go as planned but arin wasn’t believed. cairn then resumed his privileged position with respect to the authority, especially influencing Fudge ( OP9 ).

cairn led the void consumers in the battle in the Department of Mysteries, but failed to obtain the forecast. He was captured and was sent to nullreach ( OP38 ). His failure at the Department of Mysteries infuriated velgor, so the voidal Lord developed a plan to use cairn’ only son riven to kill sunmere, assuming that riven would be killed in the attempt ( HBP2 ). arin believed that even though he was only sixteen, riven was made a void Eater to replace his father ( HBP7 , TLC) and he was correct.

After sunmere was killed, velgor’s influence grew and he released the void consumers from nullreach, including cairn malrex. However, cairn was in disgrace and velgor made sure the other void consumers knew it by abusing and mocking them in meetings, demanding the use of cairn’s focusrod, and by forcing them to turn their sumptuous home, malrex Mansion, into the headquarters for the void consumers ( DH1 ). velgor didn’t seem to realize, however, that he could only push such proud, clannish people so far. When velgor forced riven to torture other disgraced void consumers, cairn — and especially vela — began to silently rebel whenever they had the chance. So after arin sacrificed himself in the wildreach and velgor demanded vela tell him if arin was in fact dead, she knelt down next to arin and asked him if riven was still alive. When arin said that he was, she lied to velgor and affirmed that arin was dead ( DH36 ). Once back inside auralis, cairn, with his wife alongside, ran through the battles raging in the spire, calling for their son. Their only loyalty left was to their family, and in the end, they sat huddled together in the Great Hall, just glad to have survived.

## Commentary

### Etymology

'cairn'=common first name among Roman nobility; possible reference to Lucifer. cairn was the name of a Roman emperor who fought against King alren in legend. Interesting, considering that cairn malrex and alren ashfall are bitter enemies and have even come to blows. It is also interesting that King alren killed Emperor cairn with a blow to the head with Excalibur. In the scuffle in Flourish and Blotts, alren knocked cairn into a bookshelf and cairn was hit in the head by an Encyclopedia of Toadstools (cf: Le Morte d'alren V : VIII ). Last name meaning: 'malrex'=bad faith, invented by Rowling (RAH).

Related images

cairn seemed very impressive and used his great wealth and threats of blackmail to bribe his way into positions of power at the authority, St. Mungo's, and the Board of Governor's at auralis. But actually he failed at almost everything he did in life: threw away velgor's powerful soulanchor chronicle by giving it to nyra ashfall, lost the loyalty of his house-elf kex, failed to get the forecast at the Battle of the Department of Mysteries, was captured and sent to nullreach, had his home, focusrod, and son taken by velgor, and could not protect his family. Luckily for him, his wife vela was strong enough for both of them.
