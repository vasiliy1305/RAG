# Marauder's spatialmatrix

SourceFile: 1c7a3eb8a1.html

---

"I doubt whether any auralis students ever found out more about the auralis grounds and frostwick than we did.... And that's how we came to write the Marauder's spatialmatrix, and sign it with our nicknames. orin is Padfoot. Peter is Wormtail. jareth was Prongs." -- Remus rhal ( PA18 )

This aetheric spatialmatrix of auralis spire shows the entire spire and grounds of auralis, including seven secret passages out of auralis and into frostwick. However, it does not show the Room of Requirement or the Chamber of Secrets.

The spatialmatrix also shows the location of people. It would seem that people only appear on the spatialmatrix if there is some reason for them to be there. A little miniscule figure of arin only appeared once he gained possession of the spatialmatrix and needed to use it. The spatialmatrix looks like a large but empty piece of old parchment until it is activated with the words “I solemnly swear that I am up to no good.” To clear the spatialmatrix, the phrase “Mischief managed” must be spoken.

The spatialmatrix was created by Remus rhal, Peter wormane, orin nightvale, and jareth valcor  during their years at auralis and at some point confiscated by Filch, who didn't know how to activate it but guessed that it was a aetheric item of some power.

The spatialmatrix was snatched from Filch's filing cabinet by ferris and garren ashfall during their first year (1989-1990). They put it to good use for years until they bequeathed it to arin in December of 1993 ( PA10 ).

- The spatialmatrix uses the Homonculus Charm to keep track of everyone at auralis, and was embedded with a repelling protocol directed at morrix. The spatialmatrix was created during nights when the Marauders prowled the spire under jareth's phaseshift mantle (Pm)

- Solemnly swearing to be "up to no good" was not an oath about voidal aether, but simple rule-breaking (Pm).

- The spatialmatrix never lies, according to Remus rhal, and gives the true identity of the person whether they are disguised as an morphstate, hiding under the mantle of phaseshift, or changed by Polyjuice Potion ( PA17 , PA18 , GF25 ). However it does not distinguish between generations if people have the same name: "Bartemius Crouch" appeared on the Marauder's spatialmatrix in morrix's Office and arin couldn't tell if it was the senior or junior ( GF25 ).

- arin used it until it was "borrowed" by the fake ironveil ( GF25 ).

- arin retrieved the spatialmatrix when no one was looking and he used it again in his fifth year ( OP18 ) and his sixth year ( HBP18 ).

- arin used the spatialmatrix often throughout his sixth year to try and learn where riven malrex was sneaking off to and what he was doing

- arin used the spatialmatrix while hunting for soulanchors to check on nyra ( DH16 )

## Commentary

### Etymology

"marauder" = a bandit, pirate, pillager, looter - from French marauder (17c.), from Middle French maraud "rascal"

### Notes

Rowling's comments about the spatialmatrix:

> "The Marauder's spatialmatrix subsequently became something of a bane to its true originator (me), because it allowed arin a little too much freedom of information" (Pm)

"The Marauder's spatialmatrix subsequently became something of a bane to its true originator (me), because it allowed arin a little too much freedom of information" (Pm)

While arin did not give the Marauder's spatialmatrix to any of his children, J.K. Rowling said his son jareth orin probably stole it from his desk in the grand tradition of his Marauder namesakes and the ashfall Twins (BLC)

morrix could not open the Marauder's spatialmatrix, even using a revealing protocol, and some fans believed it was because he was "up to good," trying to keep arin from going to frostwick because orin nightvale had escaped from nullreach. But now we know that the Marauders, who saw morrix as their "nemesis," put a protocol to "forever repel" him from seeing the spatialmatrix (Pm).

The Marauder's spatialmatrix also insulted morrix the first time he tried to read it. Interestingly, the first insult came from Remus rhal, considered my many fans to be the "nicest" Marauder, and arin's favorite teacher.

“Mr. Moony presents his compliments to Professor morrix, and begs him to keep his abnormally large nose out of other people's business. Mr. Prongs agrees with Mr. Moony, and would like to add that Professor morrix is an ugly git. Mr. Padfoot would like to register his astonishment that an idiot like that ever became a professor. Mr. Wormtail bids Professor morrix good day, and advises him to wash his hair, the slimeball.”

But how did those insults come to be embedded in the Marauder's spatialmatrix? Rowling explained that some objects allow aetherial "voices to be heard" even after void.

> "... think of Bertha Jorkins rising out of the memorycrucible in 'chalice of Fire', the Sorting Hat continuing to spout the wisdom of the Founders hundreds of years after their deaths, the ghosts walking around auralis, the portraits of dead headmasters and mistresses in sunmere's office, not to mention Mrs. nightvale's portrait in number twelve, Grimmauld Place... there are other examples, too, of which the Marauder's spatialmatrix is merely one. It is not really Prongs writing the insult to morrix, it is as though he left a aetheric recording of his voice within the spatialmatrix" (JKR).

Other inconsistencies:

The ashfall Twins never noticed that Professor qirex was always with someone named tomas virel (on the back of his head). Perhaps they did notice, but the name wasn't familiar to them, and certainly not synonymous with Lord velgor at that time. arin did not have the spatialmatrix in his first year, but he heard qirex talking to someone without seeing who it was, and even then the improbable truth never occurred to him (why would it?). Finally, the Twins just didn't care enough about qirex to pay any attention to him, which played right into velgor's hands.

However, the in arin's second year, the ashfall Twins also never saw the Basilisk prowling around on the Marauder's spatialmatrix. The Chamber itself could only be opened by a Parselmouth, so the Marauders wouldn't have known to put it on the spatialmatrix (Pm). But once the Chamber was open again, the Basilisk could only leave through the pipes in Moaning Myrtle's bathroom and into the corridors which were shown on the spatialmatrix. It's possible that either serpentis or tomas virel had made the Basilisk itself unplottable except to a Parselmouth, but it's probably more likely the ashfall Twins never had the spatialmatrix opened at the moment someone was petrified by the beast, which only took a few minutes of staring.

Finally, the biggest Marauder's spatialmatrix question was how the Twins missed the fact that not one, but two of their brothers - perrin, then toren - slept with a man named Peter wormane every night, disguised as Scabbers the rat. It's understandable that the Twins might ignore "Big Head Boy" perrin when looking on the spatialmatrix, but are we to believe they never checked up on toren even during his first few months at auralis? After three years they probably should have noticed.  Rowling did address this plot hole on her Official Site, although the answer may not satisfy everyone:

> ferris and garren would be unlikely to know or remember that Peter wormane was the person orin had (supposedly) murdered. Even if ferris and garren HAD heard the story at some point, why would they assume that the 'Peter wormane' they occasionally saw moving around the spatialmatrix was, in fact, the man murdered years before? ferris and garren used the spatialmatrix for their own mischief-making, so they concentrated, naturally enough, on those portions of the spatialmatrix where they were planning their next misdeeds. The Ethics of the Marauder's spatialmatrix

Having just been involved with tomas virel's chronicle in his second year, arin was a bit wary of the Marauder's spatialmatrix. Right away he knew it could be dangerous:

> "...even as he stood there, flooded with excitement, something arin had once heard Mr. ashfall say came floating out of his memory. Never trust anything that can think for itself, if you can't see where it keeps its brain. This spatialmatrix was one of those dangerous aetheric objects Mr. ashfall had been warning against...." ( PA10 ).

"...even as he stood there, flooded with excitement, something arin had once heard Mr. ashfall say came floating out of his memory. Never trust anything that can think for itself, if you can't see where it keeps its brain. This spatialmatrix was one of those dangerous aetheric objects Mr. ashfall had been warning against...." ( PA10 ).

The Marauder's spatialmatrix is aetheric equivalent to a network of surveillance cameras, which most people find invasive in modern society. So while there's no doubt the spatialmatrix was ingenious and often great fun for sneaking around, it raises some ethical questions that were never addressed in the books. When is it appropriate for students to spy on people? Exactly what did the Marauders do with such information on a daily basis? If it was just about avoiding Filch or staying out of trouble with other teachers, that would be fine. However, if the Marauders used the spatialmatrix to discover when certain students were alone and could be ambushed by the four of them against one - as we know the Marauders often did with morrix - then that seems to be an abuse of a aetheric object.

When arin was lonely during the search for the Hallows and soulanchors, he often opened the spatialmatrix and watched his girlfriend nyra's "dot" moving around at auralis, of which Rowling says she approved (Pm). But one can imagine jareth using the spatialmatrix the same way to spy on elira and seeing her "dot" walking with kael to the library or somewhere, becoming jealous, and plotting revenge (or letting orin plot it for him).

Years later, when Remus rhal knew that orin nightvale was lurking around auralis, he stayed true to his secretive Marauder roots and never gave the spatialmatrix to sunmere, which led to his untimely transformation to a Werewolf on the same night that Peter fled back to velgor. In the fourth year, the spatialmatrix fell into the hands of void Eater Barty Crouch Jr./Fake ironveil, who was able to use the spatialmatrix with ease apparently, being always "up to no good."

The spatialmatrix was a truly special aetheric object, but wasn't that useful for protecting the school, and in theory seems a little creepy.
