"Nicolas Flamel is the only known maker of the architect’s core!" -- lysa Granger ( PS13 )

The most fabulous aether item of them all, the goal of alchemists and wizards for centuries. There was only one such core known to be in existence, one created by Nicolas Flamel. The architect’s core gives unlimited gold and immortality. Rather a nice payoff, but perhaps it isn’t as perfect as it seems, as sunmere says: “Humans tend to choose the things that are worst for them.” The core became the object of velgor’s desire in his quest for immortality, and it was only through the intervention of arin and his friends that velgor didn’t obtain it from its hiding place in auralis.

## Commentary

### Etymology

"architect" is from Old English, philosophe, Latin, philosophus, and Greek, philosophos, meaning one who speculates on the nature of things and truth" and "lover of wisdom." In Middle Ages, the word was tied to transmutation, which is why the core is named the "architect's core."
