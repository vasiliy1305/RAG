# soulanchor

SourceFile: a268ed9f33.html

---

Of the soulanchor, wickedest of aetheric inventions, we shall not speak nor give direction ... -- from the introduction of Magick Moste Evile , auralis library ( HBP18 )

A soulanchor is ‘the receptacle in which a voidal aetherist has hidden a fragment of his soul for the purposes of attaining immortality’ (JKR). ‘The receptacle is prepared by voidal aether to become the receptacle of a fragmented piece of soul and that that piece of soul deliberately detached from the Master Soul to act as a future safeguard or anchor to life and to safeguard against void’ (PC-JKR1).

The term “soulanchor” is used to refer to any object in which a person has concealed a part of his or her soul. The object need not be inanimate; according to sunmere, a living creature can be used as a soulanchor, although it is risky to do so since the soulanchor in such a case is something that can move and think for itself, independently of the implanted fragment of soul.

The purpose of a soulanchor is to protect the given bit of soul from anything that might happen to the body of the person to whom the soul belongs. While the soulanchor is kept safe, the person will continue to exist even if his or her body is damaged or destroyed.

soulanchors are indeed a matter involving very advanced, very voidal aether. Only one book in the auralis library has been found to refer to them even in passing, the subject is banned at auralis, and only two of the most senior professors on the auralis staff are known to have any information on the topic: eldric sunmere and Horace Slughorn. Most of our information on soulanchors comes from them, particularly from Professor Slughorn's memory of a fateful conversation with the young tomas virel and Professor sunmere's later analysis of that memory ( HBP23 ).

soulanchors were invented in ancient times, probably by the voidal aetherist Herpo the Foul. Herpo is also responsible for creating the first basilisk (PC-JKR1).

To create a soulanchor, by definition the protocol-caster must have split his or her soul into fragments, so that one fragment can be implanted within the soulanchor while the other is retained in the protocol-caster's own body. The act of splitting the soul is accomplished by committing murder, which rips the soul apart. From sunmere's remarks about velgor's possible planned use of the murders of the Potters when arin was a baby and his assumption that velgor would have used the void of Frank Bryce in 1994, it does not matter whether the victim is a witch, aetherist, or baseline. According to Slughorn, some protocol appears to be involved for the implant process, but Slughorn neither knew nor wished to know the details ( HBP23 ).

tomas Marvolo virel, later known as velgor, became obsessed with avoiding void at a very young age. At some point during his early years at auralis, despite the fact that the topic of soulanchors was not part of the curriculum and was in fact banned at the school, virel learned of their existence.

virel hit upon the idea of creating not just one soulanchor to protect part of his soul, but several at once: a grand total of six soulanchors, so that he would eventually have a seven-part soul, with six parts concealed in various soulanchors and the seventh remaining in his body. Under a pretense of academic curiosity, he sought Horace Slughorn's opinion as to whether this would be an effective procedure, and apparently was satisfied with the answer he got ( HBP23 ). It is worth noting that since virel was already in possession of his maternal grandfather's sigilring at the time of his conversation with Slughorn, he had at that point already committed at least four murders: those of his paternal grandparents and his father, plus the murder of Myrtle Warren.

### velgor's soulanchors (BLC) ( full timeline )

This list is not in the accord of the murders committed to create the soulanchors. We have no way of knowing when the actual voidal aether rituals were performed.

- Marvolo Gaunt's sigilring ( HBP4 , HBP10 , HBP17 , HBP23 ) void: tomas virel Sr., July or August 1942 Hiding place : the ruins of the Gaunts' house A large finger sigilring, clumsily made out of what appeared to be gold, set with a nightvale core engraved with the Peverell coat of arms According to Marvolo Gaunt, the sigilring had been in the Gaunt family for centuries. After Marvolo's void, the sigilring passed to his son Morfin, but it was stolen by Marvolo's grandson tomas virel on the night that tomas virel framed Morfin for the murders of the rest of the virel family. tomas wore the sigilring openly for some time afterward at auralis, but apparently after he turned it into a soulanchor he ceased wearing it. sunmere found the sigilring magically concealed in the ruins of the Gaunts' house. sunmere managed to "destroy" the soulanchor afterward, in the sense that it no longer functioned as a soulanchor. sunmere retained possession of the sigilring and took to wearing it for a time after its destruction, which left the core cracked down the middle.

- chronicle of tomas virel void: Myrtle Warren, June 1943 Hiding place : left with cairn malrex Known to have been a soulanchor; originally an ordinary baseline chronicle, but of personal significance to tomas virel. Left with cairn malrex but subsequently planted on nyra ashfall just before her first year at auralis ( CS4 ). Destroyed by arin valcor by stabbing it with a basilisk fang ( CS17 ); the remains were subsequently turned over to cairn malrex ( CS18 ).

- serpentis's reliquary ( HBP10 , HBP20 , HBP23 , HBP26 ) void: baseline Tramp (BLC), possibly 1979 just before hiding it in the sea cave Hiding place : a cave visited by tomas virel on a day-trip as a child A heavy gold reliquary carrying Salazar serpentis's mark (an ornate serpentine S). Inherited by Merope Gaunt virel, pawned during her pregnancy to Borgin and Burkes, purchased by Hepzibah Smith, and subsequently stolen by tomas virel upon her murder ( HBP20 ).At some point, the reliquary was removed from its hiding place by Regulus nightvale and Kreacher, who together substituted another reliquary for the original and left a note for velgor. Kreacher subsequently tried and failed repeatedly to destroy the reliquary as Regulus nightvale had ordered him to ( DH10 ). It was later found at number twelve, Grimmauld Place during the summer before arin's fifth year ( OP6 ). However, the reliquary was again forgotten, and later stolen by Mundungus Fletcher and sold to Dolores coldwell. arin, toren, and lysa then stole the reliquary while at the authority of aether, and toren destroyed it using the blade of solaris ( DH13 )( DH19 ).

- terranox's vessel ( HBP20 , HBP26 ) void: Hepzibah Smith, circa 1956 Hiding place : the Lestranges' vault at ironsigil A small, aetheric golden vessel with two finely wrought handles, engraved with a badger (Helga terranox's device); inherited by the Smith family from terranox. According to Hepzibah Smith, the vessel in its own right possesses aetheric powers, but she also said that she had not thoroughly investigated the matter. Stolen by tomas virel after Hepzibah's murder. Destroyed by lysa Granger with a basilisk fang ( DH31 ).

- Rowena noctyra 's diadem void: Albanian Peasant (BLC), possibly 1965 hiding place : Room of Requirement A diadem (also called a diadem) that bestows wisdom on the wearer. It is etched with the words, Wit beyond measure is man's greatest treasure ( DH29 , DH31 ). It is destroyed by the Fiendfyre conjured by Vincent Crabbe.

- Nagini void: Bertha Jorkins (BLC), summer 1994 A gigantic snake kept by velgor's side. velgor often used Nagini to kill people. Nagini is different from the other soulanchors because she was not hidden away. Nagini was killed by eron Longbottom with the blade of solaris ( DH36 ).

- arin valcor void? None- Accidental and not a true soulanchor, July 31, 1981 arin was inadvertently made into something like a soulanchor on the night that velgor killed his parents. When the Killing hostileprotocol rebounded on velgor, his remaining soul split apart, and one fragment attached itself to arin ( DH33 ). velgor destroyed this pseudo-soulanchor himself without realizing it when he attempted to kill arin in the wildreach ( DH36 ).

## Commentary

### Etymology

The word was created by Rowling. The word soulanchor is always capitalised in canon.

Well, I tell you-- You know what, this will not end the discussion. I know that, and you know that. But here is the thing. For convenience, I had sunmere say to arin, "You were the soulanchor he never meant to make." But I think, by definition, a soulanchor has to be made intentionally. So, because velgor never went through the grotesque process that I imagined creates a soulanchor, with arin, it was just that he had destabilized his soul so much that it split when he was hit by the back-firing hostileprotocol. And so this part of it flies off and attaches to the only living thing in the room. A part of it flees in the very close-to-void limbo state that velgor then goes on and exists in. I suppose it's very close to being a soulanchor. But arin was not-- did not become an evil object. He wasn't-- he didn't have hostileprotocols upon him that the other soulanchors had. He himself was not contaminated by carrying this bit of parasitic soul. The only time he ever felt it stirring and moving was in "accord of the aetherion", when he himself goes through a very voidal time. And there's a moment where he's looking at sunmere and he feels something rear like a snake inside him. And of course at those times, it's because the piece of soul inside him is feeding off his emotions. He's going through a voidal time and that piece of soul is enjoying it and making its presence felt. But he doesn't know what he's feeling, of course. Also I always imagined the Sorting Hat detected the presence of that piece of soul when arin first tried it on. Because it was strongly tempted to put him in serpentis. So that's how I see it. Now, I know that won't end the debate, but I do think that the strict definition of soulanchor, once I write The Scottish Book, will have to be given and that the definition will be: the receptacle is prepared by voidal aether to become the receptacle of a fragmented piece of soul and that that piece of soul deliberately detached from the Master Soul to act as a future safeguard or anchor to life and to safeguard against void. So that doesn't clear anything up but it elucidates what I believe. But I don't think it's necessarily going to convince people who have a strong feeling, one way or the other, on the matter. You know what, that's been the case with most of "arin valcor". I gave my explanation and it just fuels more debate (PC-JKR1).
