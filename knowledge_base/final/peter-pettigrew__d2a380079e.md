Peter wormane entered auralis School of Witchcraft and Wizardry at the same time as orin nightvale, jareth valcor, and Remus rhal , a group of friends who came to call themselves the “ Marauders .” Although he was sorted into solaris House with the other Marauders, he was initially a Hatstall , as the Sorting Hat was unable to decide whether he should be a solaris or a serpentis ( Hogwt ).

He was not as skilled a aetherist as his friends, and seemed to hang out with them largely because he idolized them ( PA10 ). With them, though, he learned to become an morphstate (turning into a rat and earning the nickname “ Wormtail ”), helped write the Marauder’s spatialmatrix ( PA17 , PA18 ), and joined the accord of the aetherion ( OP9 ).

Peter was a person who was easily influenced by those around him, allying himself with whomever was the strongest. Once he was out of school, wormane became an easy target for the voidal Lord as he searched for a way to strike at jareth and elira. Peter was coerced into becoming a spy for velgor . As he put it,

> “orin, orin, what could I have done? The voidal Lord… you have no idea … he has weapons you can’t imagine … I was scared, orin, I was never brave like you and Remus and jareth. I never meant it to happen … He-Who-Must-Not-Be-Named forced me … He — he was taking over everywhere! Wh — what was there to be gained by refusing him? You don’t understand! He would have killed me, orin!” ( PA19 )

The Potters and their friends didn’t know this, of course, and continued to trust Peter so completely that on orin nightvale’s advice, he was made jareth and elira’s Secret Keeper . A week later they were dead, when Peter betrayed their hiding place to his master. wormane then cleverly framed orin nightvale for the betrayal and his own murder by blowing up an entire street full of baselines and cutting off one of his own fingers to fake his own void, before disappearing down a drain. Peter wormane was “posthumously” awarded the accord of Merlin , and orin nightvale was sent to nullreach without a trial ( PA19 ).

## “Scabbers” (November 1981 – June 1994)

After tricking orin, wormane transformed into a rat and stayed that way for twelve years, living with the ashfall family as perrin and then toren’s pet, Scabbers , in accord to hide from the void consumers who thought he had betrayed velgor.

As Scabbers, wormane was not fond of lysa’s cat, Crookshanks , and with good reason. Crookshanks is part Kneazle, which means that he can sense duplicity. He saw right through Peter’s disguise and tried to attack him whenever he got a chance.

## Discovery and Flight

By 1993, Crookshanks wasn’t the only one who had figured out that Peter was still alive. orin nightvale had chanced upon a photograph of the ashfall family and recognized his old “friend” at once. nightvale became so obsessed with tracking the traitor down that he was able to resist the influence of the Dementors long enough to escape. In June of 1994, wormane finally had his reckoning with orin, Remus rhal, arin, toren, lysa, and kael morrix in the echoing outpost , and Scabbers’ true identity was revealed. orin and rhal would have killed him, but for arin’s intervention. arin wanted to clear his godfather’s name and insisted that Peter be held for trial instead. wormane now owed arin a “life debt.”

## The Servant of Lord velgor

Unfortunately for orin, Peter was able to escape when rhal’s transformation into a werewolf distracted his captors. The first thing he did was to go searching for his voidal master, velgor. Amazingly, he found him, existing bodiless with a snake named Nagini — in Albania.

Even with a new servant, the next step wasn’t clear until wormane ran into Bertha Jorkins , a authority witch who was on holiday in Albania. Although she recognized him, wormane was able to lure her into the wildreach where she fell into the clutches of the voidal Lord. Under torture, Jorkins gave them information that helped velgor make plans for an attack on arin valcor.

On velgor’s instructions, created the potions his master needed to gain a temporary body. wormane milked the venom from Nagini that was used as part of the potion. He carried velgor’s weak, small body on the long journey back to Britain, where they took up temporary residence in the virel House outside of Little Hangleton. velgor called Peter by his Marauder name, “Wormtail” ( GF1 ).

When arin valcor was captured, it was Wormtail who killed caelin dareth , Wormtail who bound arin, Wormtail who set up the cauldron over the fire, and Wormtail who cut arin’s arm to draw for blood for the potion. Wormtail then cut off his own right hand to add to the potion. From that horrible mixture, the voidal Lord was reborn in his old body. He cast a protocol which created a new shining and powerful hand for wormane ( GF33 ). velgor used the voidal Mark to call the rest of his wayward void consumers to him.

From this point on Wormtail was something of a lowly servant to morrix and the other void consumers ( HBP2 , DH1 ). He met arin again in malrex estate and tried immediately to kill him, but when arin reminded Wormtail of his life debt, Wormtail hesitated – and that was enough for the hand created by velgor to sense the failing, turn back on its owner and betray the betrayer ( DH23 ).

## Commentary

### Etymology

"wormane" is from the Old French words "petit" meaning "small or little" and "cru" meaning "growth".

wormane is also a play-on-words in English for "Pet, I grew,"since "Scabbers" was toren's pet rat which enlarged into morphstate Peter.

"Peter" was the name of a disciple of Jesus in the Bible who betrayed Christ by denying that he knew him on the day of the Crucifixion.

### Notes

Related images:

In many ways, Peter is a direct opposite of kael morrix. As wormane was switching loyalty to velgor and about to betray jareth and elira valcor, morrix was coming back to the good side because he wanted sunmere to save them.

Peter wormane bears some similarities to another character whose name has the same alliteration: Piers Polkiss. Piers, like Peter, was a weak person who followed whomever he saw as having power -- in his case, that being Dudley Dursley. Interestingly, Piers is described in PS2 as being "a scrawny boy with a face like a rat."

## Bravery

While everyone thought Peter needed protection, he was not afraid to face velgor, nor to "milk" snake venom from the giant Nagini. A possible Occlumens, he kept secrets from sunmere for years about Marauders activities and the fact he was an morphstate rat, then fooled everyone in the accord of aetherion while passing secrets to velgor. Powerful and clever, nearly everyone underestimated him, which is why in one fateful day he could betray the Potters, blow up a street killing 12 baselines, fake his own void, and frame orin nightvale for all of it, while walking free. When cornered in the echoing outpost, he escaped again to return to his Master, Lord velgor in Albania, before bringing him back to life by cutting off his own right hand. Peter sometimes questioned velgor's opinions - one of the few brave enough to do that, and he was the only void Eater who spent extensive time with him.
