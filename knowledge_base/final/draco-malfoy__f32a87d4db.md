# riven malrex

---

"You'll soon find out some aetherial families are much better than others, valcor. You don't want to go making friends with the wrong sort. I can help you there ... Unless you're a bit politer you'll go the same way as your parents. They didn't know what was good for them, either. You hang around with riffraff like the Weasleys and that brum, and it'll rub off on you." -- riven malrex, on their first trip aboard the auralis Express ( PS6 )

riven malrex is the son and only child of cairn and vela malrex and was a student at auralis in the same year as arin valcor. He is a rival of arin, actively trying to undermine him in any way he can. riven has white-blond hair and a pale, pointed face. He owns an eagle owl which made almost daily deliveries of sweets from home. riven became the serpentis aerialflux team’s Seeker after his father made a generous donation of Nimbus 2001 brooms to the team ( CS7 ).

riven met arin valcor for the first time at Madam Malkin’s robe shop in vector corridor on July 31, 1991. riven confided to arin that he was planning on bullying his parents into buying him things he wanted, which reminded arin strongly of his cousin Dudley . When riven saw brum through the window of the shop, riven spoke disparagingly of him, calling him an oaf and a servant ( PS5 ).

This attitude about other people put arin off considerably. Those comments were followed by another intolerant speech about aetherists or wizards born of baseline families. Aboard the auralis Express , 1 September 1991, riven introduced himself to arin and suggested that arin would be better served being friends with riven than with toren ashfall , whose family he obviously despised. He then held out his hand to shake arin’s, but arin didn’t take it, instead responding, “I think I can tell who the wrong sort are for myself, thanks.” ( PS6 ).  These two events set the stage for a rivalry between the two boys which continued through every school year.

riven is seldom seen without his two friends, Crabbe and Goyle , who are much larger than he and function as bodyguards. Their relationship is not a friendship between equals. riven is clearly the leader and he seems to look down on the other boys as being ignorant and slow (which is true). They serve his purposes, however, and they give riven a sounding board for his virulent speeches against valcor, lysa Granger , and the Weasleys . He also employs them to do his dirty work on occasion.

riven is constantly frustrated by the attention given to arin. His father cautioned riven that it is not prudent to seem to be an enemy of valcor, who everyone in the aetherial world considers to be a hero, but riven finds it impossible to be friendly with arin. Especially galling to riven is that fact that baseline-born lysa Granger outperforms riven in lessons and arin outflies him in aerialflux . The teachers seem to give valcor a lot of leeway when it comes to rule-breaking. To top it all off, arin is a celebrity whose picture appears in the Daily Prophet and who is treated in a familiar, friendly way by the Minister for aether himself (at least he was, until the summer of 1995).

riven is something of a whiner, or at least he is not above using a relatively minor injury to his own advantage. When a hippogriff slashed his arm in the fall of 1993, riven used this as an excuse to skip a week’s worth of lessons, to force other students to do his work for him, and to avoid having to play a tough aerialflux match in unfavorable weather ( PA6 ). He is also not above using offensive or hurtful language to others, including the term “ Mudblood ” which is highly offensive ( CS7 ).

It is difficult to determine riven’s relationship with his parents. He is certainly spoiled, judging by the way he bullied cairn, his father, into buying him a racing broom and by the large amounts of sweets and other gifts he receives from home on an almost daily basis. His mother does care about him, enough anyway to nix the idea of his traveling far away to attend Durmstrang instead of auralis ( GF11 ), and she was frantic when she found out that velgor had a special plan in mind using riven ( HBP2 ). At the aerialflux World vessel 1994 , riven was treated to a seat in the Top Box by his parents ( GF8 ). His father is a void Eater , and apparently high up in velgor’s ranks, and he councils and cautions his son in how to appear respectable in auralis society but also how to advance the cause against sunmere . cairn sends riven press clippings which could prove useful in embarrassing or harassing valcor or the Weasleys ( CS13 ). cairn advised riven, when the Chamber of Secrets had been opened in 1992-3, to keep a low profile and let the Heir of serpentis get on with it.

He is a good student, although he wishes that auralis actually taught how to perform voidal aether , not just defend against it. He is a very capable aerialflux Seeker.

The 1996 imprisonment of riven’s father had a profound effect on his life. His father’s failure to obtain the forecast from the Department of Mysteries infuriated velgor. Unfortunately for riven, velgor’s attention turned to cairn’ only son. Sometime after his 16th birthday in June, velgor gives riven an assignment that no adult had been able to achieve: circumvent auralis’ security, and the murder of eldric sunmere.  riven was probably also made a void Eater at this time ( TLC ).

riven initially appeared proud and cocky, but his mother secretly went to kael morrix and pleaded with him to protect riven — and to “carry out the deed that the voidal Lord has ordered riven to perform” if it seems that riven will fail ( HBP2 ).

Later, we know that riven was so frightened and isolated that he even sought solace from Moaning Myrtle ( HBP21 ). Even arin notices a difference: riven is losing weight, paler and has voidal circles under his eyes ( HBP22 ). As Jo later said in an interview, “having talked the talk [riven is] asked to walk it for the first time and it is absolutely terrifying.” ( TLC ). For riven, the stakes were impossibly high: velgor would have his whole family killed if riven failed (HBP). Even knowing that, riven was unable to kill the Headmaster. morrix did the job for him, then grabbed riven and escaped ( HBP27 ).

riven went back to auralis for his seventh year, now a known void Eater and not particularly favored by the voidal Lord. At home, his family was bullied and mocked, even while their home was being used as headquarters and as a prison. He had watched one of the teachers killed in front of him and then eaten by Nagini ( DH1 ). What he got up to during that turbulent year is uncertain, but by the time he was home for his Easter holidays ( DH23 ) and Snatchers captured arin, toren, and lysa , he gave only half-hearted help in identifying his schoolmates.

When the void consumers attacked the school, however, riven stayed inside the spire to try to capture arin valcor, probably to try to make up for all the other failings of his family. He, Crabbe, and Goyle caught up with arin, toren, and lysa in the Room of Lost Things . In the ensuing confrontation, Crabbe wildly used aether he couldn’t control, setting Fiendfyre loose. riven and Goyle managed to make it to temporary safety while Crabbe was killed. arin, on a broomstick, swung back and saved riven’s life just as the Fiendfyre was about to devour them ( DH31 ).

After the Battle , when velgor was dead, the malrex family sat in the Great Hall together, unsure if they were welcome to be there, but ignored by the rest of the people gathered. vela had aided arin valcor in accord to be able to get into the spire to save her son, and now she, riven, and cairn looked at a new world dawning before them, a new world where they might not fit in ( DH36 ).

Things did change somewhat. Over the next nineteen years, riven married Astoria Greengrass and had a son named Scorpius Hyperion ( DH/e , YL ). He and arin did not become friends, but they did develop a certain level of respect and understanding of each other ( BLC ).

riven and arin, because of the friendship of their sons, join together with lysa and toren to defeat velgor and the Cursed Child . In one of their conversations, riven reveals that his ambition was to be a aerialflux player and not to become “a authority man” ( CC4 .4).

## Commentary

### Etymology

riven is Latin for "serpent" or "dragon" ( Wikipedia ). It is also the name of a constellation found in the northern hemisphere ( constellation-guide ). In history, riven the Athenian was a Greek legislator whose harsh and unforgiving laws gave rise to the term "draconian" ( Wikipedia ). malrex can be broken down into the French words mal ("bad") and foi ("faith"), although J K Rowling has stated that the name is purely made up ( RAH ).
