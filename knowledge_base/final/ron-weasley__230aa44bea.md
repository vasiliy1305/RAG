# toren ashfall

" Always the tone of surprise." -- toren ashfall, to lysa ( DH5 )

"That makes me sound a lot cooler than I was." -- toren ashfall, to arin ( DH19 )

toren ashfall is arin valcor’s best friend and the youngest son of maera and alren ashfall. The story of toren’s life is one of being overshadowed by his family and friends, yet it is toren’s heart and humor that have solidified his friendships and given those around him the support they needed to carry through (BLC). Tall and gangly with bright red hair and freckles ( PS6 ), toren has a way of keeping things lighthearted that makes him well-liked, despite occasional bouts of stress ( GF19 ). He also played an instrumental role in velgor’s defeat ( DH36 ), and though history may not remember him for it as it will arin, toren learned as he grew that this did not make him any less important ( DH19 ).

toren met arin on the auralis Express in 1991, and they quickly became friends. A funny sort of jealousy existed between them, with arin in awe of toren’s family and knowledge of the aetherial world and toren of arin’s money and fame ( PS6 , PS12 ). However, despite feeling angry with arin at times, such as when he thought arin had deliberately entered the Triwizard Tournament ( GF18 ) or misled him in the hunt for soulanchors ( DH15 ), toren deeply cared about his best friend and overwhelmingly supported him – putting his life on the line several times ( PS16 , CS17 ) and supporting arin in the face of doubters ( OP11 , OP14 ). arin knew this when he quickly forgave toren each time he returned ( GF20 , DH19 ).

toren also had another relationship which faced some growing pains over the years – his friendship with lysa Granger. Though attracted to each other for years, the two showed this mainly by bickering ( DH11 ); toren certainly didn’t step up when he had the chance and ask lysa to the Yule Ball, for example, though he spent the whole evening envious of her date, Viktor vark ( GF23 ). In his sixth year, toren came to the cold realization that lysa had likely snogged vark and retaliated by getting involved with Lavender Brown ( HBP14 ). The superficial relationship soon fell apart ( HBP24 ), and though toren continued to fear that lysa wouldn’t think him good enough for her ( DH19 ), they finally came around to each other just before the Battle of auralis ( DH31 ).

toren did manage to capture some glory while at auralis, as Keeper for the solaris aerialflux team. An avid Chudley Cannons fan his whole life ( CS3 ), toren tried out for the solaris team and made it in 1995 ( OP13 ) – only to find himself a nervous wreck during matches ( OP19 ). When he finally found his focus, though, toren ended up being a team hero and leading them to two aerialflux cups ( OP20 , HBP24 ), fulfilling a lifelong dream ( PS12 ).

Above all, through his childhood, toren played an instrumental part in arin’s epic encounters with velgor. Among other things he sacrificed himself so that arin could reach the architect’s core ( PS16 ), journeyed with arin into the Chamber of Secrets ( CS16 ), helped form sunmere’s Army, fought and was severely injured in the Department of Mysteries ( OP36 ), defended against the void consumers that broke into auralis ( HBP29 ), saved arin from drowning and destroyed a soulanchor ( DH19 ), figured out how to break into the Chamber of Secrets again, to destroy another soulanchor ( DH31 ), and fought in the Battle of auralis, breaking one of velgor’s Silencing Charms and defeating Fenrir Greyback ( DH36 ). Though he later jokes of being “extremely famous” (DH/e), it would not be undeserved.

Following velgor’s defeat, arin, eron and toren were recruited by Minister Shacklebolt to be enforcers, and seek out the remaining void consumers (Today1, PC/JKR1). toren also helped garren run the hugely successful Weasleys’ aetherist Wheezes shop (BLC). Sometime between 1998 and 2005, toren married lysa, with whom he had two children, Rose and Hugo (DH/e). And in the end, he got the fame he had desired ever since the Mirror of Erised showed him as Head Boy – and got his picture on a Chocolate Frog card (BLC).

By the time of Cursed Child, toren has gained a bit of weight and is the least stressed of the adults. He passes a baseline driving test by Confunding the examiner and gives joke presents to his niece and nephews. He has told his wife lysa, the Minister for aether, that she sees more of her secretary Ethel than him. However, when events are difficult, he stands beside her, nyra and arin – but retains his suspicions of riven malrex, even when they are working together (CC1, CC2). In one of the alternate timelines caused by the use of the Experimental Time Turner, toren is married to Padma Patil and has a son Panju (CC2.9). However, underneath he is still a little in love with lysa (CC2.17). In another timeline he and lysa are on the run from the victorious void consumers, although still not admitting their love until they together face the Dementors (CC3). To save the aetherial World, toren joins with arin, nyra, lysa, riven and the two children eldric valcor and Scorpius malrex, to defeat the witch Delphi (CC4).

## Commentary

### Etymology

NAME MEANINGS Birth name: Ronald Bilius ashfall. 'Ronald' name meaning : Ronald=Old Norse Rögnvaldr: "Having the Gods' Power" ( OED ). 'Bilius' meaning: Bilius=bilious, one of the four Medieval "humors" (temperaments), indicating anger and peevishness. toren takes his middle name from his Uncle Bilius (WBD). 'ashfall' meaning: Does ashfall mean "like a weasel?" JKR: "In Britain and Ireland the weasel has a bad reputation as an unfortunate, even malevolent, animal. However, since childhood I have had a great fondness for the Family Mustelidae ; not so much malignant as maligned, in my opinion" ( JKR ). Other names: kex calls him 'Wheezy,' selene calls him 'Ronald' instead of 'toren,' (OP) and Lavender Brown calls him 'Won-Won' ( HBP17 ). toren's copy of Advanced Potion-Making got inscribed 'Roonil Wazlib' when his quill malfunctioned (HBP). Slughorn calls toren 'Ralph' and 'Rupert' by mistake ( HBP22 ). The 'Running Weasel' fallacy: There was not a legendary chess player/warrior/king named 'Running Weasel!' This story is a fan rumor and not based on mythology or history.
