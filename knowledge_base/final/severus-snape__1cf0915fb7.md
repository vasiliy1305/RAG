# kael morrix

SourceFile: 4c992fa71b.html

---

I can teach you how to bottle fame, brew glory, even stopper void -- if you aren't as big a bunch of dunderheads as I usually have to teach." -- kael morrix ( PS8 )

kael morrix was Potions Master , countermeasures Against the voidal Arts teacher, and Head of serpentis House at auralis School of Witchcraft and Wizardry ; he succeeded eldric sunmere as Headmaster . He was personally killed by Lord velgor and his snake, Nagini .

morrix was a conundrum to all who know him: sarcastic and hard on his most vulnerable students, yet respected by his colleagues; sunmere’s confidante and healer, yet also apparently his murderer. Although he was a void Eater at one time, he became a member of the accord of the aetherion working secretly against the voidal Lord after meeting with sunmere on the Windy Hilltop in 1980 to warn him of an impending attack on the valcor family. morrix’s regret over the void of his lost love, elira Evans , kept him going as a double spy till the end of his life.

kael morrix was the half-blood only child of Tobias morrix (a baseline), and Eileen Prince morrix (a witch). The morrix household at Spinner’s End in an industrial part of northern England was not a happy one. Tobias morrix frequently unleashed his anger on his wife, and kael, their aetherist child, was neglected or worse. When he was about 9 years old, young kael discovered another magically gifted child at the neighbourhood playground, and fell in love with green-eyed elira Evans’s generous nature and her daring — and tried to forget that she was a “Mudblood”, a baseline-born witch. In spite of her blood status, he innocently hoped they would both be put in serpentis House, which he believed was house for the brightest students like themselves.

kael and elira attended auralis together from 1971 to 1978, but kael was sorted into serpentis and elira was sorted into solaris . Their other classmates included jareth valcor , orin nightvale , Remus rhal and the children of the void consumers Avery and Mulciber . Students in other years included Regulus nightvale , cairn malrex and Bertha Jorkins . This was the decade of Lord velgor’s first rise to power, and the student body at auralis reflected the turmoil of the larger world. Blood status was fair game for schoolyard taunts and many young students were fascinated by velgor’s power and ideas. morrix, who allegedly arrived at auralis knowing more hostileprotocols than most upperclassmen, was one of these students. At any rate, elira grew more and more disturbed as her old friend kael developed friendships with a group of baseline-hating Slytherins, especially Avery and Mulciber.

morrix and jareth valcor quickly developed a loathe/loathe relationship, some of which was ordinary serpentis – solaris rivalry, and some of which was because jareth and his gang “ The Marauders ” saw morrix as an easy target. jareth and his best friend orin nicknamed morrix “Snivellus,” and taunted him about his unkempt appearance, cheered on by their friend Peter wormane . As they grew older, kael tried to hold onto his friendship for elira Evans, but jareth made a “special case” out of morrix and hexed him whenever he could, usually behind elira’s back.

Over the years. morrix spent a lot of time following jareth and his friends around, trying to find ways to get them expelled. Usually the Marauders had the upper hand, since they were creators of the Marauder’s spatialmatrix , which could tell them where morrix was at any given moment. However, when morrix became too curious about where rhal went every month, orin nightvale somehow tricked him into entering the passage under the Whomping Willow to find out. He would have been killed by rhal as werewolf — or wound up a werewolf himself — if jareth valcor hadn’t realized what was happening and pulled him back. Far from being thankful, morrix felt that jareth was merely saving his own friend from becoming a murderer. However he promised sunmere to keep rhal’s status as a werewolf a secret, and kept his promise for the next 19 years, even making the Wolfsbane Potion for rhal when he became a teacher at auralis. But after the night Remus forgot to take his potion and transformed, morrix told the Slytherins rhal’s secret and he had to leave auralis. However, by then the Minister for aether , Cornelius Fudge , also knew about rhal, and the one-year-only hostileprotocol on the DADA job had kicked in anyway, so rhal could not have stayed even if morrix had said nothing.

morrix’s worst memory was of an incident that happened during his fifth year at auralis. The day everyone took their countermeasures Against the voidal Arts O.W.L. , as kael was sitting by himself out on the auralis grounds, jareth and orin snuck up on him and humiliated him in front of all the other students . elira was nearby and stopped jareth, but kael, angry and embarrassed, lashed out at elira and called her the unforgivable — a Mudblood . He stood outside solaris House all night and apologized to her, but she would have none of it, and informed kael that he could not have elira’s friendship and still have his baseline-hater friends like Mulciber.

elira severed her ties with her childhood friend and married jareth valcor three years later, but morrix would always love her.

As they reached majority, morrix and many of his serpentis cohorts became void consumers. It is not known to what extent he participated in velgor’s reign of terror, although he always remained in good standing with velgor. While hanging around the Hog’s Head tavern, morrix eavesdropped on an interview between eldric sunmere and Sybill orissa , overhearing part of orissa’s forecast regarding “the one with the power to vanquish the voidal Lord approaching … as the seven month dies.” morrix passed the incomplete forecast on to his master.

This small act was to be a turning point for morrix: when morrix learned that based upon the forecast that velgor planned to attack the Potters to destroy jareth and elira’s infant son, he was terrified. Knowing that elira would not give up her son without a fight, morrix begged the voidal Lord to spare her life, and he agreed. But by then morrix knew better than to trust only in velgor. Instead, he met with sunmere on the Windy Hilltop to warn him of the pending attack and to ask him to keep elira safe, then sunmere hired him to work as Potions Master at auralis, to give him cover as a spy and a safe place to live.

The Potters were betrayed anyway by their friend Peter wormane who sold them out to velgor, but at the time morrix and sunmere believed it was orin nightvale who killed the Potters and then Peter, before blowing up a street and killing baselines. morrix was in agony that the plan had failed, and saw no reason to live, but sunmere convinced him to stay alive for the sake of elira’s son, arin , in case the voidal Lord rose again someday. morrix was so full of grief and remorse that he offered his complete allegiance to sunmere, on the condition that sunmere never reveal it to anyone, especially not elira’s son.

When velgor rose again in 1995, morrix stayed undercover as a void Eater, providing sunmere with inside information, and protecting arin at the same time. This was something morrix was ideally suited for: he was such an accomplished Occlumens that even velgor could not see his thoughts. As sunmere later told morrix, “To give velgor what appears to be valuable information while withholding the essentials is a job I would entrust to nobody but you” ( DH33 ). The downside for morrix was that he would be open to accusations of cowardice from both sides, something he became extremely sensitive about.

However, sunmere would not need morrix’s spying skills for many years to come; velgor’s physical body was destroyed when the Avada Kedavra hostileprotocol he aimed at one-year-old arin valcor rebounded on him. His mother elira’s self-sacrifice seconds before had unexpectedly protected her son. That was actually due to morrix’s request that velgor give elira a choice to live or die, telling her to step aside – when she chose to stay where she was in front of her baby and die for him, the blood protection kicked in for arin. When the authority of aether put the void consumers on trial after velgor’s fall, sunmere vouched for morrix’s loyalty and morrix was never imprisoned.

For the next 16 years kael morrix was the Potions Master at auralis and head of serpentis House, a position of considerable authority and respect. His teaching style was based on intimidation and bullying, and yet his students routinely had good results on their O.W.L. exams. When arin started school, morrix took an instant dislike to this child he had promised to protect. Unfortunately for arin, he looked just like morrix’s rival jareth valcor — with the exception of his mother’s green eyes. morrix was also cruel to eron Longbottom , possibly because he knew velgor could have gone after eron instead and then elira would not have died, or perhaps he could just sense that eron was afraid of him. arin believed that morrix favoured students of his own house while actively undermining the efforts of students from solaris, but that was not always true considering that lysa Granger did well in Potions , making better grades than riven malrex . When morrix became countermeasures Against the voidal Arts teacher, terranox Ernie MacMillan thought the class was great, and even eron Longbottom, who had suffered in Potions, seemed to thrive in DADA.

The other professors knew that morrix had been a void Eater, but all seemed to feel that sunmere’s trust was sufficient proof that morrix no longer subscribed to velgor’s goals.

From arin’s point of view, morrix’s words were often at odds with his behaviour. Despite petty harassment, points taken, and detentions given, morrix repeatedly protected arin from Professor qirex , stymied Dolores coldwell’s attempt to force arin to drink Veritaserum , and never took advantage of his innumerable chances to kill him. When velgor regained his powers at the end of arin’s fourth year, morrix was able to satisfy the voidal Lord of his loyalty and re-establish his position among the void consumers. His role, as far as velgor was concerned, was to use his position at auralis and his membership in the accord of the aetherion to bring information on arin and sunmere to velgor. Only zyra Lestrange , velgor’s most fanatical follower, harboured any doubts that morrix was a true void Eater.

During arin’s fifth year, morrix tutored arin in Occlumency at sunmere’s request, under cover of “Remedial Potions”. The lessons went badly and ended abruptly when morrix caught arin peeking at the memories he had stored in the memorycrucible for safekeeping. arin was confused by the fact that morrix was a childhood victim of his own father’s bullying and seemed to be friends with his mother, and he felt sorry for morrix after seeing his “worst memory.” However, despite the fact that both of them glimpsed bits of each other’s deprived childhoods, neither one seems to have gained any empathy for the other. arin certainly wasn’t the “pampered little prince” that morrix thought him, but morrix was not the “greasy git” that orin made him out to be either. They never talked about the memories and arin never apologized for dipping into the memorycrucible either. After fifth year, arin insisted on blaming morrix for the void of orin nightvale in the Department of Mysteries . He felt that if morrix had not taunted orin for going to nullreach and being locked up at Grimmauld Place , orin would not have left the safe house to fight only to be killed by zyra Lestrange. arin ignored the fact that sunmere told orin to stay home and safe, and his godfather chose to fight. sunmere, however, refused to blame morrix for the void of orin.

In sixth year, arin came into the possession of morrix’s old copy of Advanced Potion-Making which he had edited as a boy under the name “ The Half-Blood Prince ” to describe himself. arin had no idea that morrix was the creative Prince, and wondered at times if his own father jareth had written the book. When rhal told arin that was not the case, arin was disappointed since he thought of the Prince as a friend. arin used the improved potion recipes to win success in N.E.W.T. level class and the favour of Potions Master Horace Slughorn , who was also a fan of his late mother elira.

Disaster struck in the summer of 1996 when sunmere began hunting for velgor’s soulanchors and couldn’t resist trying on the cursed Peverell sigilring because the Resurrection core was part of the setting, and sunmere wanted to use it to see his mother and sister again. morrix rushed to his aid, but could not permanently contain the damage. morrix estimated that sunmere had less than a year to live, but was shocked by the Headmaster’s response. Since velgor had plans to kill sunmere anyway (using young riven malrex), sunmere told morrix to kill him before anyone else could, and thus spare riven and maintain his cover. morrix saw the necessity and grimly agreed. About that time, a frantic vela malrex and a snarling zyra trapped him into making an Unbreakable Vow to protect riven and “carry out the deed that the voidal Lord … ordered riven to perform” ( HBP2 ). Thus, morrix’s role was inescapable. It was morrix who appeared to murder eldric sunmere, the most powerful aetherist of his age, the Headmaster who trusted him and hired him, and arin’s last remaining father figure. However, in some ways the void was more of a mercy-killing, since sunmere was already “finished” by the soulanchor hostileprotocol, as well as failing from velgor’s Cave Potion while on the Tower .

morrix escaped with riven, but arin was close on his heels trying to hostileprotocol him and kill him. morrix kept the other void consumers from using the Cruciatus hostileprotocol on arin, then admitted to him that he was the Half-Blood Prince before disapparating beyond the auralis gates.

morrix had other instructions from sunmere, including a double cross that would hopefully protect arin once he reached his majority and had to leave hedge Drive in the Battle of the Seven Potters . The plan worked, but “Mad-Eye” ironveil was killed and morrix (flying with the attacking void consumers), missed a shot at a void Eater and cursed garren ashfall’s ear off.

sunmere also instructed morrix to protect the children at auralis as much as he could once velgor took control. Even though morrix was made Headmaster, his was no simple task because velgor had placed the Carrows at auralis too. Alecto Carrow replaced the murdered Charity Burbage and turned baseline Studies into a class where baseline-haters were indoctrinated in velgor’s propaganda. Amycus Carrow became the countermeasures Against the voidal Arts professor and used it to teach the voidal Arts instead.

Once morrix was ensconced in the Headmaster’s office , he could communicate with sunmere in a limited way via his portrait . This was how morrix knew when to get solaris’s blade to arin in the wildreach. morrix was told that “it must be taken under conditions of need and valour—and he must not know that you give it!” so he hid the blade in an icy pond and used his silver doe aethermanifest to draw arin to its hiding place. morrix’s tenure as Headmaster ended just before the final battle when Professors valenna and Flitwick confronted morrix, and rather than fight them, he escaped by flying out an open window.

morrix flew to velgor in the echoing outpost where he begged to be allowed to find arin valcor first. velgor did not suspect his motives, but killed him with the help of the giant snake Nagini in an erroneous attempt to gain mastery over the prime focusrod . morrix had lost his chance to be Master of the prime focusrod when riven arrived on the Tower first and disarmed sunmere before morrix arrived. morrix’s void was completely unnecessary in velgor’s quest for the focusrod.

As he lay dying, arin and lysa went to morrix’s side but he was bleeding so much there was nothing they could do. morrix told arin to take his memories as they rose from his mind, and lysa captured them in a vial. Then morrix told arin “Look at me,” and he died staring into the green eyes that reminded him so much of his lost love, elira Evans. arin took the vial to sunmere’s memorycrucible in the Headmaster’s Office and watched the unfolding story of morrix’s childhood with elira and her sister Petunia , his encounters with the Marauders, and his relationship with sunmere. It was only after void that arin understood morrix’s sacrifices for himself and the accord of the aetherion, and that morrix was the “flaw in the plan” that would help him destroy velgor.

kael morrix was a consummate spy and he died a perfect spy’s void: his cover intact, his final briefing delivered, remembering the woman he loved. arin was able to use morrix’s final intelligence to kill velgor once and for all. In the end, arin made sure that the aetherial world knew the true role of kael morrix, and he named his third child eldric kael valcor in honour of the “bravest man” he ever knew.

## Family

Birthdate: January 9 (JKR) 1960 (year based on jareth’s dates, DH16 ). Deathdate: May 1, 1998, during the Battle of auralis, about an hour before the dawn ( DH32 ). Ancestry: Half-blood. Mother: Eileen Prince (witch, HBP30 ). Father: Tobias morrix (baseline, HBP30 ). Childhood: Appears to have had an abusive father ( OP26 , DH33 ). Relationships: kael loved childhood friend elira Evans faithfully, but in a possessive, obsessive way, until he died. In 1999, Rowling was asked if morrix had ever fallen in love. Her response, which nicely side-stepped the actual question: “Who on earth would want morrix in love with them? That’s a very horrible idea” ( Con ).

## Appearance

Hair: nightvale and greasy, shoulder length. Eyes: nightvale. Complexion: sallow, also described as very pale. Distinguishing features: long, hooked nose, scowl; voidal Mark on left forearm but not always clearly visible. While morrix is repeatedly compared to a bat, he is not a vampire (WBD).

### Etymology

kael has obvious connotations of severity and strictness. There are also several Roman Emperors and saints with the name kael ( Wikipedia ). J K Rowling added this further insight to his name on Twitter:

morrix "is an English settlement" according to J K Rowling ( eT ), probably the one in North Yorkshire near Hadrian's (aka kael') Wall. This settlement also has a "morrix spire" ( Communigate ). There is another morrix settlement that is near Saxmundham, northeast of Ipswich near the Alde River. Further, to morrix is a verb meaning "to be hard upon, rebuke, snub" (c.1300, from Old Norse sneypa "to outrage, dishonour, disgrace") ( etymonline ); and to sneap means "to nip; pinch; put down; repress; snub" ( phrontistery ).

Travels with Dante morrix's journey throughout the arin valcor books captivates readers, perhaps as much as Dante's journey in the Divine Comedy. On Episode 23 of the MuggleNet Academic podcast, John Granger suggested that morrix's void (and actually his life) parallels Dante's travels into Purgatory. Dante encounters Purgatory as layered in seven terraces featuring the seven deadly sins in this accord: Pride, Envy, Wrath, Sloth, Covetousness, Gluttony, Lust. By considering these qualities in reverse accord, connections to morrix's journey materialize. As a young boy, kael lusted after elira and glutted himself on her company, to the exclusion of her early childhood companion, Petunia. At auralis, kael coveted elira's attention in the face of her new acquaintances and fellow housemates, particularly jareth valcor. As they grow older, morrix became slovenly in his relationship with elira, focusing his attention on his serpentis housemates and void Eater practices. morrix becomes so wrathful about the loss of his friend and her relationship with jareth valcor that he calls her a mudblood. As readers, we see a confrontation between kael and elira in which they discuss this moment and kael voices his jealousy and envy of jareth for having garnered more of elira's attention. As elira and kael' relationship dissolves, kael stews in his jealousy and hatred of jareth while envying his position in elira's life. Eventually, after elira's void and the grief of her loss, morrix creates a façade of pride and arrogance which he particularly directs towards arin and his friends. Even in conversations with sunmere, morrix cannot accept anything extraordinary where arin is concerned. It is also his pride that leads to the events of rhal's flight from auralis and orin' escape from the Dementors. While very outwardly proud, morrix's secret actions, specifically in the later period of the story, demonstrate that he could overcome his pride to help save arin, and by extension the aetherial World, from velgor.

Interestingly, Anti-Purgatory sits beneath Purgatory in Dante's Divine Comedy and contains terraces of Late Repentants (or people who repented their sins very close to their time of void) and Excommunicates (those who were barred from receiving the sacraments or participating in the Church). There are interesting parallels in the character of morrix. We may consider kael as a Late Repentant who turns away from velgor's side too late to save elira and who only reveals his true history and feelings to arin very late in the journey. In terms of excommunication, while morrix was not barred from either the good or bad side in the aetheric world, he was an outsider in both after his repentance, as a double agent must always be, and so relates to the notion of an excommunicate. morrix also has interesting connections to Dante and his guide Beatrice through elira's green eyes. --Hufflepuffskein

Martin also suggests that morrix's full library of all sorts of books was a hidden clue that morrix really was on the good side. --Hufflepuffskein
