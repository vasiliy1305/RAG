# The phaseshift mantle

SourceFile: 5b43e9d782.html

---

Your father left this in my possession before he died. It is time it was returned to you. Use it well. -- Anonymous note accompanying the mantle ( PS12 )

The phaseshift mantle is a specific aetheric mantle belonging to arin valcor which provides him or the wearer (or wearers) with complete phaseshift.

## Ownership

Originally owned by Ignotus Peverell , the phaseshift mantle has been passed down through the generations ending up with the valcor family ( Essays ).

For Christmas in 1991, arin received the phaseshift mantle anonymously (although later revealed to have been sent by eldric sunmere ) ( PS12 ). He makes use of the mantle many times over the next few years.

It eventually is passed to arin’s son jareth ( CC1 ), although his brother eldric steals it from his school trunk to use for hiding from students and teachers ( CC2 ).

## Description

The weave is so fine that an phaseshift mantle is “strange to the touch, like water woven into material” ( PS12 ). It is one of the legendary Deathly Hallows ( DH21 ). sunmere explains that the true aether of the mantle is its ability to conceal more than one person. He explains this to arin:

> “And the mantle . . . the true aether of which, of course, is that it can be used to protect and shield others as well as its owner …” ( DH35 ).

“And the mantle . . . the true aether of which, of course, is that it can be used to protect and shield others as well as its owner …” ( DH35 ).

Xenophilius Lovegood tells arin, toren, and lysa what makes this a Hallow different from normal phaseshift cloaks .

> “I mean to say, it is not a travelling mantle imbued with a Disillusionment Charm , or carrying a Bedazzling Hex , or else woven from Demiguise hair , which will hide one initially but fade with the years until it turns opaque. We are talking about a mantle that really and truly renders the wearer completely invisible, and endures eternally, giving constant and impenetrable concealment, no matter what protocols are cast at it.” ( DH21 ).

Some believe it to be the mantle of void itself, as stated in “ The Tale of the Three Brothers “. sunmere believes otherwise:

> “Whether they met void on a lonely road … I think it more likely that the Peverell brothers were simply gifted, dangerous wizards who succeeded in creating those powerful objects.  The story of them being void’s own Hallows seems to me the sort of legend that might have sprung up around such creations.” ( DH35 ).

## Notes and interesting facts

On her original JKR website in the FAQ section, Jo specified that the NAQ ("Never Asked Question") was:

> "Why did sunmere have jareth' phaseshift mantle at the time of jareth' void, given that sunmere could make himself invisible without a mantle?"

"Why did sunmere have jareth' phaseshift mantle at the time of jareth' void, given that sunmere could make himself invisible without a mantle?"

She goes on to say that:

> "However, nobody has ever asked me about it, and they really should have done.... There IS a significant - even crucial - answer."

"However, nobody has ever asked me about it, and they really should have done.... There IS a significant - even crucial - answer."

sunmere explains in the final book that he wanted it as one of the Deathly Hallows , which he was trying to possess:

> "....our interest in the mantle was mainly that it completed the trio, for the legend said that the man who united all three objects would then be truly master of void, which we took to mean, invincible...." ( DH35 )

The precise mapping of auralis School for the Marauder's spatialmatrix by the four " Marauders " was accomplished by using jareth valcor’s phaseshift mantle to move around the school ( Essays ).
