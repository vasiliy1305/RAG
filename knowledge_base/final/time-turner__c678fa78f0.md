She had to write all sorts of letters to the authority of aether so I could have one. She had to tell them that I was a model student, and that I'd never, ever use it for anything except my studies.... I've been turning it back so I could do hours over again ..." -- lysa Granger ( PS21 )

The Time-Turner is a small silver hourglass worn on a chain around the neck. It is a very powerful and dangerous aetheric item which literally turns back time for the user, one hour per inversion of the glass, thanks to the Hour-Reversal Charm contained within it ( MagSp , PmP , CC1 .5).

## Background

The use of Time-Turners is regulated by hundreds of laws ( MagSp ) and controlled by the authority of aether ( PA21 ).

Time-Turners are mainly used for mundane matters of time-management because of the catastrophic consequences time travel can have ( MagSp ).

lysa Granger used a Time-Turner for the 1992-1993 school year to allow her to take multiple classes at the same time ( PA21 ).

lysa and arin valcor used a Time-Turner to go back three hours in time and rescue Buckbeak and orin nightvale from a terrible fate ( PA21 ).

## authority Time-Turner

The authority Time-Turner is an hourglass containing One-Hour Reversal Charms.

Kept “vanilla” and relatively harmless by the authority due to Croaker’s Law :  “the furthest someone can go back in time without the possibility of serious harm to the traveller or time itself is five hours” ( CC2 .16, CC4 .4).

This is the type lysa used to repeat class hours in her Third Year, and to rescue orin nightvale and Buckbeak ( PA21 ).

All of these were destroyed during the Battle of the Department of Mysteries ( OP35 , HBP11 , BP , MagSp ).

## Experimental Time-Turner

The Experimental Time-Turner is made of cheap metal, confiscated from Theodore Nott by enforcers and hidden in the Minister for aether’s office ( CC1 .5)

Limited use to five minutes in the past, after which time it would begin to shake uncontrollably ( CC2 .7)

Stolen from Minister for aether lysa Granger’s bookcase ( CC1 .9), and used by Scorpius malrex and eldric valcor to try and go back to try and save the life of caelin dareth ( CC2 .7, CC2 .20, CC3 .8)

## Golden Time-Turner

The Golden Time-Turner was made for cairn malrex , who wished to travel back further than Professor Croaker would allow at the authority ( CC4 .4).

Kept hidden for years by riven malrex , who knew it would lend credibility to silly rumours that his son Scorpius was the son of velgor . However, when Scorpius and eldric seemed to be trapped in the past, riven brought the Golden Time-Turner to arin so they could use it to rescue their children ( CC4 .4). Thanks to the device, arin, nyra, toren, lysa and riven along with the boys were able to intercept Delphi , the daughter of Lord velgor in founder’s hollowreach , on the night the Potters were killed in 1981 ( CC4 .7, CC4 .11).
