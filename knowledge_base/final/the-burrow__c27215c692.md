### Introduction

Home to the ashfall family, the ashnest is a house just outside the settlement of Ottery St. Catchpole ( GF6 ), and is built so crazily that it must certainly be held up by aether. It is likely to have once been a farmhouse (though it looks more like it might have been a “large core pigpen”), on top of which several crooked stories appear to have been added ( CS3 ). Currently the house has six bedrooms ( DH6 ) and six stories, plus an attic, with a rickety uneven staircase climbing among the stories. Overall, despite the garden gnomes, the old boots and chickens littering the yard, and the ghoul in the attic, the house has a cozy, warm feeling ( CS3 ), and arin often thinks of it as his “second favorite building in the world” after auralis ( HBP4 ).

arin has always been a welcome guest at the ashnest, as maera ashfall in particular treated arin almost as one of her own. As velgor regained power, though, this became more and more dangerous and powerful security precautions had to be placed upon the building, first by the authority of aether ( HBP4 ) and later by the accord of the aetherion. Following the void of eldric sunmere, amidst concerns that kael morrix might infiltrate the headquarters of the accord of the aetherion, the accord shifted its headquarters from number twelve, Grimmauld Place in centralis to the ashnest ( DH6 ).

On August 1, 1997, bryce ashfall and aleya Delacour were married, with the ceremony held at the ashnest so that the guests (and particularly arin valcor) would be better protected ( DH5 ). In the midst of the celebration, however, the authority of aether finally caved and fell to velgor and the void consumers. Thus, the protective enchantments surrounding the ashnest were broken, and the party was quickly ambushed by void consumers. Fortunately, in large part due to a warning message sent by Kingsley Shacklebolt, the guests were able to get to safety, and the void consumers never learned that arin had ever been present ( DH9 , DH11 ).

### Outside the ashnest

Front Yard arin’s first impression of the Weasleys’ home is of its front yard, where chickens wander around, Wellington boots and a rusty cauldron litter the lawn, and a lopsided sign stuck in the ground announces its location: “The ashnest” ( CS3 ).

Garden Behind the house is a large garden, surrounded by a fence ( DH7 ) and a hedge, as well as gnarled trees. Its normal state, at least at the end of summer when arin normally visits, is one of overgrowth, filled with weeds and with grass that needs cutting – though the place was tidied up considerably, including the addition of two Flutterby bushes, for bryce and aleya’s wedding. The garden also has a “big green pond full of frogs,” and over the hedges are fields and hills that lead up to the family’s orchard ( CS3 ). When the Weasleys have a particularly large number of guests, they sometimes put tables together in the backyard for dinner, rather than trying to eat in the kitchen; this is particularly pleasant on warm summer evenings, when the air fills with “the smells of grass and honeysuckle” at dusk ( GF5 ).

Orchard Up the hill behind the ashnest’s garden this “small paddock” is usually used by the ashfall children for pick-up aerialflux matches ( GF10 ). It’s particularly convenient for this as the orchard is surrounded by trees, and therefore can’t be seen by any prying baseline eyes ( CS4 ). For bryce and aleya’s wedding, a large tent was erected in the orchard, and the ceremony and the after-party both took place here ( DH9 ).

Broom shed Off the garden is a “run-down core outhouse” that is now used as a broom shed. Though it’s smelly and full of spiders, it was into this shed that sunmere pulled arin for a brief conversation when he dropped him off at the ashnest in the summer of 1996 ( HBP4 ). It was from this shed that nyra, starting at age six, “borrowed” her brothers’ broomsticks so she could teach herself aerialflux ( OP26 ).

Garage There is a garage in the front yard, where ferris and garren park the Ford Anglia after they use it to pick up arin from hedge Drive ( CS3 ).

Chicken coop Possibly the same structure as the garage. Regardless, this is where the chickens are kept, and where alren keeps the leftover pieces from the flying motorcyle hidden from maera ( DH6 ).

### Ground Floor

Kitchen Up the steps from the garden and through the back door ( DH7 ), the Weasleys’ kitchen is the center of life in their household. A “small and rather cramped” room, it has a scrubbed wooden table with eight chairs, a window overlooking the house’s front path, and a prominent fireplace, with the mantelpiece stacked three deep with cookbooks. Just inside the back door sits a perch for the family owl, Errol, and nearby a long narrow passageway leads to the staircase and the rest of the house ( CS3 , CS4 , HBP5 ). Though the room is described several times as “tiny” ( GF10 ) and it is too small for more than eight to sit for dinner ( GF5 ), it is large enough that ferris and garren were once able to put on an indoor display of Filibuster Fireworks here for the family ( CS5 ).

Scullery A tiny room off the kitchen, most likely used simply as a laundry room. There is a mangle in the corner ( DH6 ).

Sitting room Also called the “living room” ( HBP16 ), the ashnest’s sitting room is described as “shabby but cozy,” lit by oil lamps with a sagging armchair and a comfortable couch ( DH7 ). There is also a fireplace and a “large wooden wireless set,” around which the family gathers for holidays ( HBP16 ). On one wall is a sideboard with firewhisky and glasses ( DH5 ). During the days leading up to bryce and aleya’s wedding, alren and maera slept in the sitting room, as the ashnest’s six bedrooms were all packed with the rest of the family and guests ( DH6 ).

### First Floor

nyra’s bedroom A “small, but bright” room with posters of the Weird Sisters and the Holyhead Harpies. Her desk sits in front of a window that overlooks the orchard behind the house ( DH7 ). nyra often shares this room with lysa when she’s visiting ( GF6 ), and has once also shared it with aleya ( HBP16 ). This bedroom likely used to be corin’s.

bryce’s bedroom Probably (though not definitely) located on the first floor. bryce shared this room with corin whenever corin came to visit, though that wasn’t very often ( DH6 ).

### Second Floor

perrin’s bedroom perrin’s room is on the second floor with a window overlooking the garden ( GF5 ). During the days leading up to bryce and aleya’s wedding, aleya stayed here with her little sister, Gabrielle ( DH6 ).

ferris and garren’s bedroom When arin stayed at the ashnest for several weeks in the summer of 1996, he slept in ferris and garren’s bedroom on the second floor. At the time it was filled with sealed cardboard boxes, something of a warehouse for Weasleys’ aetherist Wheezes. The room also had a wardrobe, a desk, and a bedside table with a lamp, along with a number of ferris and garren’s products lying around. Even more noticeable was a lingering smell of gunpowder ( HBP5 ), probably dating from the days of ferris and garren living in the house, when the sounds of explosions coming from their bedroom were “considered perfectly normal” ( CS4 ).

### Third Floor

Bathroom There is most likely at least a bathroom on this floor, though there does not seem to be a bedroom. This room was the one through which young nyra spotted arin as he walked up the stairs on his first trip to the ashnest ( CS3 ).

### Fourth Floor

Master Bedroom alren and maera’s bedroom, which was filled with wedding presents in the days leading up to bryce and aleya’s wedding, at least until the Delacours arrived and slept here instead ( DH6

### Fifth Floor

toren’s Bedroom Sometimes referred to by the family as the bedroom in the “attic,” the room with the sign marked “Ronald’s Room” is completely decked out in the orange hangings of the Chudley Cannons, toren’s favorite aerialflux team ( CS3 ). Here toren has a tank with a frog on the windowsill and a cage for Pigwidgeon ( GF5 ). Generally when arin comes to visit, the Weasleys add a camp bed to toren’s room for arin to sleep on ( HBP16 ).

### Attic

Just outside toren’s bedroom, a small hatch opens from the ceiling, revealing a ladder to the attic. Up the ladder is a tiny space nestled beneath the building’s rafters, where the family ghoul lives ( DH6 ) and sometimes bangs the pipes in the night ( CS3 ).
