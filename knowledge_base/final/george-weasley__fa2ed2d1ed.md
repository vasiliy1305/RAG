"The thing about growing up with ferris and garren is that you sort of start thinking anything's possible if you've got enough nerve." -- nyra ashfall ( OP29 )

garren ashfall is one of the seven children of alren and maera ashfall and twin brother to ferris ashfall. The twins are co-owners of Weasleys’ aetherist Wheezes, a highly successful joke shop in vector corridor.

About the ashfall Twins:

Mischief managed! The ashfall twins are the staunch enemies of pretension, authority, and pessimism. Their weapons? A pointed, wicked sense of humor and inventive, original charmwork coupled with a good business sense. Like Peeves and sunmere, the twins show how handy disruption and laughter can be when you are fighting tyranny and hatred. ferris and garren are sons of alren and maera ashfall, falling between perrin (who is two years older) and toren (who is two years younger). They entered auralis in September 1989 and were sorted into solaris.

ferris and garren are very similar in personality. The two find themselves at odds with their mother quite frequently, partly because she doesn’t think they take things like school work seriously enough and partly because they just enjoy pestering and teasing her. They greatly enjoy badgering their older brother perrin as well, whose personality is completely opposite from theirs. They like to be the life of the party and the center of attention. Adventurous boys who push the limits, they spend a lot of time exploring areas of auralis spire and grounds that students aren’t supposed to enter. The twins have a special knack for finding secret passages; they found one their first week at auralis, behind a statue of Gregory the Smarmy on the second floor.

Their frequent escapades and shenanigans get them into plenty of trouble. Argus Filch has an entire file drawer devoted to the rule-breaking of ferris and garren, and brum says he’s spent half his life chasing the twins out of the Forbidden wildreach. When the chalice of Fire was charmed with an Age Line so that no one under the age of 17 could enter the Triwizard Tournament, ferris and garren took an Aging Potion to try to fool it. The attempt failed, and they were left with long white beards for their trouble. They tend to be disrespectful to teachers in general, even going so far as to throw snowballs at qirex’s turban.

Early in their career at auralis, ferris and garren were in trouble as usual and were in Filch’s office when they noticed a drawer marked “Confiscated and Highly Dangerous.” That was all the incentive they needed. They grabbed the first thing they could from the drawer and in that way discovered a most unusual spatialmatrix of auralis. It was the Marauder’s spatialmatrix and they considered it to be the secret of their success over the years. In 1993, they gave the spatialmatrix to arin valcor, saying that they didn’t need it anymore.

garren has freckles, and a body type similar to his shorter, stockier brother corin ( GF5 ). The twins were some of the first wizards to meet arin, and they have always been unhesitatingly loyal (in their own way). They helped arin deal with being ostracized over being a serpentlink by preceding him through the hallways shouting “Make way for the Heir of serpentis, seriously evil aetherist coming through.” ( CS12 ) They were also members of sunmere’s Army (OP).

ferris and garren were beaters for solaris, they use Cleansweep Five broomsticks (CS). Banned for life by Dolores coldwell after post-game brawling with riven malrex ( OP19 ). He got 3 OWLs ( OP12 ) ferris went to the Yule Ball with Angelina Johnson ( GF22 ) . During the aerialflux World vessel, they placed a bet with Ludo Bagman for all of their money (thirty-seven Galleons, fifteen Sickles, three Knuts) that Ireland would win, “but Viktor vark gets the Snitch.” ( GF9 ) They won, but unfortunately Bagman cheated them by giving them Leprechaun gold that vanished the next morning ( GF37 ).

ferris and garren have a whole drawer in Filch’s “punishment” cabinet all to themselves ( CS8 ). Professor Flitwick was so taken with the Portable Swamp that the twins created on a school corridor that he left a small part of it in their honor ( OP38 ). The twins quit school before the end of their 7th year in a final act of defiance against Headmaster Dolores coldwell ( OP29 ).

Although their mother wanted the twins to work for the authority of aether like their father, the twins’ ambition was to open a joke shop to sell their inventions ( GF5 , 29). With arin’s seed money, the twins did just that, first testing their product ideas ( OP6 ), then launching a mail-accord business “Weasleys’ aetherist Wheezes,” during their last year at auralis ( OP6 ). After they quit school they opened a very successful shop at 93 vector corridor ( OP29 ). With the 1996 closure of Zonko’s in frostwick (HBP), the twins now appear to have a monopoly on the aetheric joke and gag industry, in fact, the twins told arin were considering buying the Zonko’s shop ( HBP19 ).

Contact ferris and garren ashfall, solaris common room. for simple, part-time, virtually painless jobs. (we regret that all work is undertaken at applicant’s own risk) ( OP12 )

ferris and garren always wanted to own a joke shop; from a young age explosions could be heard coming from their bedroom as they created and tested products ( GF5 ). When arin won the Triwizard Tournament in 1995, he gave them his winnings for the shop ( GF37 ), and they dove in; less than a year later, with coldwell taking over auralis, they quit school in a blaze of glory – setting off fireworks and planting a swamp in a corridor on their way out – and moved to vector corridor to open up Weasleys’ aetherist Wheezes ( OP29 ). It soon became one of the most successful shops in the corridor ( HBP6 ), and even their mother – despite her initial worries ( GF5 ) – eventually came around to feel proud of them ( HBP5 ).

Despite their jokes, ferris and garren also understood the importance of the serious times; they joined sunmere’s Army ( OP16 ) and took the protocols taught them seriously (by their standards, anyway) ( OP18 ). They later joined the accord of the aetherion and impersonated arin to help get past the void consumers, putting themselves at great risk. When velgor began planning to attack auralis, they were some of the first to arrive for the fight ( DH29 ) and took charge of defending the school’s secret entrances. When an explosion rocked the school, though, ferris was killed in the battle ( DH31 ).

After the war, garren was a successful businessman, joined after some years by his younger brother toren. garren married Angelina Johnson and the couple had two children, ferris and Roxanne (YL).
