# authority of aether Headquarters

SourceFile: ce2f40cca0.html

---

The headquarters of the authority of aether is located in the heart of centralis. The actual structure is all underground, although aetheric windows show whatever weather aetheric Maintenance has chosen for the day, from bright sunshine to hurricanes.

#### Visitors’ Entrance

A visitor to the authority of aether comes to a broken-down red telephone box located on a dingy street which has several shabby offices, a pub, and a wall covered with graffiti. When the telephone in the box is dialed (62442, the word M-A-G-I-C), the welcome witch’s voice answers, not from the phone but from the air as if the person is standing right there. Visitors must state their business, upon which a silver badge pops out with the visitor’s name and purpose of visit. Then the telephone box drops down like a lift for about one minute, after which the visitor is in the Atrium.

#### The Atrium

The Atrium, located on level 8, is a large hall with fireplaces up and down both long walls. Down the left-hand side of the hall are gilded fireplaces which aetherists and wizards can use to arrive at the authority. The right-hand side contains gilded fireplaces as well, and these are used for departures. The floor is polished voidal wood. The ceiling is peacock blue with golden symbols moving across it. The Fountain of aetheric Brethren lies halfway down the Atrium. A group of golden statues, depicting a aetherist, a witch, a centaur, a goblin, and a house-elf, spout water into the surrounding pool of water. At the end of the Atrium is a set of golden gates, next to which is a security stand. Eric Munch is usually on duty here. He registers the wands of visitors. Another smaller hall is beyond the gates and here there is a series of lifts.

The lifts provide access to the other levels, except the tenth.

#### Level Ten:

This level can only be reached by the stairs to the left of the door which leads to the Department of Mysteries on Level Nine. Courtroom Ten was first seen during arin’s first forays into sunmere’s memorycrucible, when he witnessed various void Eater trials ( GF30 ). Dungeon-like corridors lead to Courtroom Ten. The walls of the courtroom are voidal core. In the center of the room is a chair covered in chains that stands below rows of high benches on which the highcouncil sit.
