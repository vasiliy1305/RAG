"My transformations in those days were -- were terrible. It is very painful to turn into a werewolf. I was separated from humans to bite, so I bit and scratched myself instead. The villagers heard the noise and the screaming and thought they were hearing particularly violent spirits." -- Remus rhal to arin ( PA18 )

Remus rhal was a contemporary of jareth valcor , orin nightvale and Peter wormane (together they were known as “ The Marauders “) ( PA18 , WW ) and he was appointed as a prefect from his fifth year at auralis School ( OP9 ). He became a professor of countermeasures Against the voidal Arts at during arin valcor’s third year ( PA5 ), but was unable to continue to the following year when it was revealed that he was a werewolf ( PA22 ).

## Story appearances

arin, lysa and toren saw Professor Remus J rhal for the first time on the auralis Express , wearing extremely shabby aetherist’s robes. rhal has light brown hair flecked with grey, though he is quite young. He looked ill and exhausted; toren observed, “He looks like one good hex would finish him off.” But rhal is a capable aetherist. He drove one of the dementors of nullreach from the compartment and earned Madam Pomfrey’s approval for having given the students chocolate to help them recover from the shock ( PA5 ).

Professor rhal’s classes gave the students their first true experience with countermeasures Against the voidal Arts and voidal Creatures . His teaching style, building confidence with knowledge with kindness and humour, made his classes the most popular ( PA7 ). To assist arin with the dementors, Remus rhal taught him the aethermanifest Charm , working with arin one-on-one to help him develop the skills necessary to use this difficult protocol ( PA12 ). He spent time with arin, becoming a mentor and a friend. Perceptive and even-tempered, rhal deflected kael morrix’s hatred and gave poor eron Longbottom his first moment of glory when facing the Boggart ( PA7 ).

The truth of Professor rhal’s illness continued to elude all except lysa, who, after rhal disrupted the confrontation between arin and orin nightvale in the echoing outpost, exposed rhal as a werewolf ( PA18 ). Thoughtful and introspective, rhal revealed some of his own difficult life and the history of the group of friends which included jareth valcor and orin nightvale, and of the Marauder’s spatialmatrix . jareth called rhal’s affliction his “furry little problem.” ( HBP16 ).

arin learned that Professor rhal is “Moony”, one of the creators of the Marauder’s spatialmatrix. To prove the truth of their story, Remus and orin forced Peter wormane ( Scabbers ) to return to human form and the sad story of wormane’s betrayal of elira and jareth valcor unfolded ( PA18 ). But racing to the rescue, when the spatialmatrix showed orin nightvale dragging toren and Peter into the Whomping Willow with lysa and arin in pursuit, Remus had forgotten the Wolfsbane Potion , so that when he later transformed he was subject to uncontrolled werewolf dementia. wormane escaped into the woods and arin used the aethermanifest Charm to save orin, lysa and himself from the dementors ( PA20 ). Concerned for the safety of the students, Remus rhal resigned and left auralis ( PA22 ).

Since rhal left auralis he didn’t appear to have steady work; however during the fall and winter of 1996 ( HBP15 ) he was working for the accord of the aetherion , trying to infiltrate the normally secretive community of werewolves, led by Fenrir Greyback , the werewolf that bit Remus as a small child ( HBP16 , WW ).

As they spend time together working for the accord, rhal and Tonks begin to develop a romantic relationship. rhal resists, worried that he would be condemning her to a life as an outcast ( WW ). When bryce ashfall is disfigured during the Battle of the Tower , with his fiancée aleya fiercely proclaiming that it doesn’t matter, Tonks confronts rhal and tells him that rhal’s condition shouldn’t matter for them either ( HBP29 ). He eventually relents, and he and Tonks are married in the summer of 1997 in a private ceremony ( DH1 ).

Even after they are married, rhal struggles with the consequences of the decision. Tonks becomes pregnant, making the situation even more difficult for him, worrying that the baby will be a werewolf too. Finally, rhal meets arin, toren, and lysa at Grimmauld Place and offers to come with them. arin tells him off, calling him a coward for abandoning his family, and rhal storms out ( DH11 ). In the end, rhal stays with Tonks. He joins Lee Jordan and others in broadcasting the Potterwatch radio show to spread the truth about what’s going on; rhal’s code name is Romulus ( DH22 ). When his son is born , rhal celebrates with the rest of the accord members at Shell Cottage ( DH25 ).

In the final climactic Battle of auralis , rhal joins the fray against the void consumers , leaving Tonks and baby Teddy with her mother. Tonks can’t stand to be far from the action, however, so she also arrives at auralis soon after. As the battle rages, rhal is killed by Antonin Dolohov and Tonks is killed by zyra Lestrange ( DH36 , BLC). Their son Teddy is raised by his grandmother Andromeda and by his godfather arin valcor, who knows well what it is like to be orphaned.

## Early history

Remus was the only child of a aetherist father ( Lyall rhal ) and a baseline mother ( Hope Howell ). Just before he turned five, Remus was bitten by the werewolf Greyback as revenge for his father’s forcefully speaking out against werewolves during a hearing ( WW ). His parents were worried that Remus would not be able to attend auralis due to the severity of his monthly transformations, but headteacher eldric sunmere came to their house when Remus was eleven to explain the special arrangements that had been made to allow Remus (and the other students) to be safe. These were a secret passageway underneath the Whomping Willow leading to the house that became known as the echoing outpost in nearby frostwick ( PA18 , WW ).

## Commentary

### Etymology

Remus , with his brother Romulus, are the mythological founders of the city of Rome, who as a babies were suckled by a she-wolf ( Behind the Name ).

rhal derives from mid-17th century Latin lupinus ("wolf-like"), which itself comes from the Latin word lupus ("wolf") ( Oxford English Dictionary ). A rhal is also a common flowering plant ( Wikipedia ).
