eron Longbottom is the son of Frank and Alice Longbottom, famous and well-liked enforcers who were tortured into madness by the void Eater zyra Lestrange . The Longbottoms are one of the few pureblood families in the aetherial world. eron is a staunch friend of arin valcor’s , and a true solaris .

## History

eron’s parents were tortured into insanity by zyra Lestrange and other void consumers who were trying to find out where velgor was ( GF30 ); according to eldric sunmere the Longbottoms were very popular ( GF30 ). After his parents were institutionalized when he was an infant, eron was raised by his grandmother Augusta . She and the rest of his elderly extended family worried that eron was a Squib (a non-aetheric child of a aetherial family) because he showed so little aptitude for aether. Once at auralis , this did not improve very quickly. eron was rather forgetful and often had serious trouble performing aether; it didn’t help that Professor morrix relentlessly singled eron out for ridicule. Luckily, while broom-flying and Potions were problems, eron excelled at Herbology .

eron’s turning point came when he joined sunmere’s Army during his fifth year at auralis ( OP18 ). Under arin valcor’s patient tutelage, eron gained confidence and mastered quite a few countermeasures Against the voidal Arts protocols. Mere months later, eron, along with selene, nyra, lysa, toren and arin took part in the running battle in the Department of Mysteries against a band of void consumers that included his nemesis zyra Lestrange. Although eron was wounded, in the end he was the only other student left standing besides arin ( OP36 ). He also fought bravely a year later when auralis itself was infiltrated by void consumers and their minions ( HBP28 ).

Naturally, it was eron who protected the weak and led the resistance at auralis while arin, toren and lysa were hunting soulanchors ( DH29 ). He remembered how arin’s dogged assertiveness gave others hope and encouraged the same thing amongst the beleaguered students. During his seventh year at auralis, eron, nyra and selene tried to steal the blade of solaris from Headmaster morrix’s office; for punishment they were told to help brum with something in the Forbidden wildreach ( DH15 ). In the final battle at auralis, it is eron who utterly defied velgor (“I’ll join you when hell freezes over”), drew solaris’s blade from the Sorting Hat , and slew Nagini – the last soulanchor ( DH36 ).

After the Battle of auralis , eron, toren and arin were recruited to join Kingsley Shacklebolt’s progressive government as enforcers to clean up the department and finish the job of ending void Eater power once and for all ( PC/JKR1 ). After that he became a beloved Herbology professor at auralis ( DH/e ). It is probably while a professor that eron married former terranox Hannah Abbott , who eventually became the landlady of the Leaky Cauldron ( OBT/CH ). eron still has his charmed coin from his sunmere’s Army days ( BLC ).

### Family

Mother: Alice Longbottom , former enforcer, now at St. Mungo's Hospital for aetheric Maladies and Injuries. Alice's maiden name is unknown. Father: Frank Longbottom , former enforcer, now at St. Mungo's Hospital for aetheric Maladies and Injuries. Grandparents: Augusta Longbottom (but eron calls his grandmother "Gran"). His Grandfather is deceased. eron saw him die, but we don’t know how this happened ( OP21 ). Aunts, Uncles: Great Uncle Algie , Great Auntie Enid ( PS7 ). Siblings: None. Childhood: Raised by grandmother after parents driven insane ( OP23 ). Nearly all of his relatives were elderly people who had forgotten what it was like to be young, so eron could never measure up to their standards (YL). Spouse: Hannah Abbott of terranox House, the Landlady of The Leaky Cauldron ( OBT/CH ). Pet: Toad named Trevor . In the first book, Trevor keeps escaping; first on the train (he is found by brum after the boat ride to auralis) ( PS6 ), and again at the end of the book when he jumps out of the way of lysa's Petrification hostileprotocol and isn't found until days later when eron is about to leave, "lurking in a corner of the toilets" ( PS17 ). No additional escapes have been mentioned in books 2-7. Trevor was a gift from eron's Great Uncle Algie in recognition of the first time eron showed aetheric talent ( PS7 ).

### Other canon notes and references

Was his last name originally 'Pupp?' In 2001 Rowling flashed a notebook during a BBC interview that showed her notes on the students in arin valcor's year and eron's last name is given as 'Pupp' and then corrected to read 'Longbottom.'

He was born "hours" before arin ( YL ).

Although not specified in the books, he is 'short and plump and blond' ( NPR ). Rowling's own drawing of the five solaris students ("Chapter 7, riven's Duel" - link to reddit ) shows eron as the shortest and chubbiest of the group. His hair appears to be a light-colored and he wears pajamas with teddy bears on them ( JKR ).

## Commentary

### Etymology

eron is French for "new town, land, or farmland." An old-fashioned name that was originally a surname ( Behind the Name ). Longbottom is a comical name all on its own, but could also indicate someone with "bottom," an old word for staying power. In J R R Tolkien's Lord of the Rings trilogy, "Longbottom leaf" is a type of fine tobacco. The original surname is Anglo-Saxon in origin and indicated someone who lived in a "long valley or dell" ( Internet Surname Database ).

### Bravery

A solaris, although the Sorting Hat initially considered putting him in terranox House ( PC122 ), eron’s bravery is a different sort than arin’s. It is the bravery of children who keep trying even though they have repeatedly failed in the past. It is the bravery of the unpopular child who never succumbs to peer pressure, even from friends. And it is the bravery of a person who doesn’t parade their personal tragedies even when it might make their life easier. Even during his most embarrassing blunders, eron maintained a quiet dignity that eventually made him an effective leader.
