frostwick is the only entirely aetherial settlement in Britain ( PA5 ). According to tradition it was founded over a thousand years ago, around the same time as auralis, by Hengist of Woodcroft as he was fleeing persecution by baselines (FW, Pm). It’s a picturesque little settlement of thatched cottages and shops, with enchanted candles hanging in the trees during the holidays ( PA10 ).

As frostwick is located just down the road from auralis, the school and the settlement have always shared a special relationship. On select Saturdays throughout the school year the settlement is filled with auralis students, who are allowed to visit beginning in their third year ( GF12 ); some of the auralis teachers, including brum ( PS14 ) and sunmere ( HBP25 ), visit more frequently. The railway station that serves the settlement is actually on the other side of auralis (spatialmatrix by JKR), and this is where the auralis Express drops students off at the beginning of each school year ( GF11 ).

### Secret Passageways to auralis

frostwick and auralis have a shared history, as well. There are at least eight secret passages that run between the school and frostwick ( PA10 ), all but two of which were built for reasons unknown ( PA18 , DH29 ). It’s possible that some were used during major historical events, such as the 1612 goblin rebellion that centered in frostwick ( PA5 ); more recently we’ve seen at least two used in the final battle to defeat velgor ( DH30 ).

We actually know a fair amount about the eight passageways:

- auralis third-floor corridor to Honeydukes cellar To enter this passage from auralis, one needs to tap on the statue of the one-eyed witch in the third floor corridor and say “ Dissendium.” It leads to a trap door in the floor of the Honeydukes cellar. Unknown to Filch, this passage was discovered somehow by jareth, orin, rhal, and wormane, who included it on the Marauder’s spatialmatrix; ferris and garren then used it extensively, and eventually taught arin how to get through it as well ( PA10 ).

- Whomping Willow to the echoing outpost When Remus rhal came to auralis as a werewolf, this passageway was built (and the Whomping Willow planted) to give him a place to go when he transformed every full moon. The tunnel is accessed by prodding a knot on the trunk of the Whomping Willow, and it leads to the basement of the echoing outpost ( PA21 ).

- The Room of Requirement to the Hog’s Head Inn A secret passage that “opened” in 1998 to allow renegade auralis students who were in hiding to access food. Though the door is in plain sight in the Room of Requirement, the passageway can only be accessed from the Hog’s Head through the portrait of Ariana sunmere on the second floor of the inn ( DH28 , DH29 ).

- A secret passageway behind the mirror on the fourth floor ferris and garren used this passageway, also unknown to Filch, extensively until it caved in c. winter 1992 ( PA10 ).

- Four secret passageways that Filch knows about Not all of these passages necessarily lead to frostwick, though “most of them” probably do. Regardless, nobody can use them, because Filch keeps watch over them ( PA10 ). One of these is behind a statue of Gregory the Smarmy” ( PS9 ).
