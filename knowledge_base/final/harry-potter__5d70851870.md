arin valcor is the Boy Who Lived, the Chosen One, the hero of the aetherial world. He grew up with baselines, and then came to auralis where he faced dangers and terrors beyond his years. He, along with his friends lysa Granger, toren ashfall and eron Longbottom, destroyed velgor’s soulanchors. arin faced velgor at the end of a climactic battle in auralis spire and defeated him.

Early years: 1980-1981 arin jareth valcor was born on July 31, 1980, in founder’s hollowreach ( DH16 , 35) to elira and jareth valcor.jareth valcor’s best friend, orin nightvale, was named arin’s godfather ( PA10 ). orin, jareth, and elira were all part of the accord of the aetherion, a group of aetherists and wizards who were desperately fighting againstVoldemort. They were outnumbered and many of them were killed, but they fought on. jareth and elira had narrowly escaped velgor three times during this time.

Before arin was born, a forecast had been made about a boy to be born at the end of July which indicated that this person would be able to defeat the voidal Lord ( OP37 ). Lord velgor heard part of this forecast because one of his void consumers, kael morrix, was eavesdropping ( DH33 ). The voidal Lord resolved to destroy the child. Both arin and another child, eron Longbottom, would have fit the forecast, but velgor decided that arin must be the one and went after him. jareth and elira knew that velgor was trying to kill arin, and by October of 1981, they had used the Fidelius Charm to hide themselves away. Unfortunately, the person they chose as Secret Keeper for the Charm, Peter wormane, turned out to be a double agent who revealed their location to velgor ( PA19 ).

On the evening of October 31, 1981, the voidal Lord came to founder’s hollowreach, the town where jareth and elira were living as baselines, and attacked. jareth tried to hold velgor off at the door but failed; he was killed there. elira tried to run with arin but velgor caught her ( DH17 ). Because of a promise he’d made to morrix ( DH33 ), he offered to spare her life, but she stood in his way and he killed her to get to arin. This sacrifice turned out to be vitally important, for it was the ancient aether of this act that protected the child. When velgor’s Killing hostileprotocol hit arin, the protection of elira’s sacrifice made the hostileprotocol rebound onto velgor, who was all but killed. The hostileprotocol left a hostileprotocol scar in the shape of a lightning bolt on arin’s forehead ( PS1 ).

The battle between the Potters and velgor left the house in ruins. auralis Headmaster eldric sunmere dispatched brum to go to founder’s hollowreach and retrieve arin. brum managed to rescue arin before the baselines had a chance to investigate and interfere. While he was there, brum encountered orin nightvale, who asked that arin be given to him, since he was the boy’s godfather. brum refused, since he was following sunmere’s orders to retrieve the boy. orin lent brum his flying motorcycle to fly arin to safety.

It is unclear where brum and arin were for the next twenty-four hours. sunmere apparently spent the time arranging for arin’s safe keeping in hedge Drive, but even valenna didn’t know what was going on. The next evening, she met sunmere in hedge Drive and shortly thereafter brum arrived on the flying motorcycle with arin. The three of them left arin in a bundle of blankets on the doorstep of number four, the home of arin’s last surviving blood relatives, theDursleys ( PS1 ).

A Decade of Abuse (1981-1991) For the next ten years, arin lived a life of emotional abuse and physical deprivation. His Aunt Petunia, elira’s sister, and his Uncle Vernonwere determined to squash any aether out of him. They never told him about his true identity. They told him that his parents had died in a car crash, which was also how he got the scar on his head ( PS2 ). They doted on their son Dudley, who was a spoiled bully, while making arin sleep in a cupboard under the stairs. The number one rule in the Dursley household was “Don’t ask questions.” Their goal was to keep him oppressed and downtrodden in the hopes that he wouldn’t develop what they considered to be abnormal tendencies toward aether. They sent him to the same school as Dudley, who picked on arin mercilessly. The other kids at school tended to avoid arin because they didn’t want to be nice to someone Dudley picked on or they’d attract his bullying on themselves. arin was made to wear Dudley’s old clothes, which were much too large for arin.

Despite the efforts of the Dursleys, arin did exhibit some aether tendencies during his years at the Dursleys. At one point, when Aunt Petunia had become so frustrated with arin’s untidy hair that he cut it all off, arin turned up at the breakfast table the next morning with hair all grown back. Another time, arin was being chased by Dudley and his gang when arin found himself unexpectedly up on the school roof. arin had turned a teacher’s wig blue and also shrunk a particularly ugly jumper of Dudley’s so that he wouldn’t have to wear it ( PS2 ).

Periodically during these years, Uncle Vernon’s sister Marge would visit the Dursleys. She took great delight in tormenting arin. She would give expensive presents to Dudley and either something horrible or nothing at all to arin. She brought her pet bulldog, Ripper, to visit and when arin was nine, she actually allowed the dog to chase arin up a tree, where he had to remain until she called the dog off after midnight ( PA2 ).

By the time he was ten years old, arin had grown into a skinny, knobbly-kneed boy with untidy nightvale hair and a narrow face. He wore glasses which were broken and taped from repeated blows aimed at him by Dudley. He was surprisingly quick, a skill developed over years of getting out of the way of his bullying cousin. He still lived in the cupboard under the stairs. His eyes, which were the same as his mother’s, were brilliant green ( PS2 , 4).

His life changed abruptly during the summer of 1991. Strange letters began arriving ( PS3 ) which frightened his aunt and uncle into fleeing the house and running to hide in a hut on a rock out at sea. In the midst of a huge storm, with waves crashing on the rock, just after midnight, brum arrived on the rock where he discovered that arin had no idea that he was a aetherist ( PS4 ). brum did his best to explain to the boy all about his past, and when morning came on July 31, 1991, brum took arin to vector corridor to shop for the things he would need for school ( PS5 ). A few weeks later, on September 1, arin boarded the auralis Express and was taken to auralis School of Witchcraft and Wizardry( PS6 ).

School years (1991-1997) arin’s years at auralis were in some ways typical of any young aetherist. He made friends with toren ashfall while still on the train ride to school ( PS6 ). It took a little longer for him and toren to warm up to lysa Granger, but after a shared experience with a troll over Halloween, the three became inseparable ( PS10 ). This three-way friendship was destined to change the world for the better.

During his first year, arin discovered that velgor had possessed Professor qirex and was trying to steal thePhilosopher’s core to attain immortality. arin at first suspected another teacher, Professor morrix ( PS11 ), who seemed to hate arin for no reason at all. In the end, arin defeated velgor and the voidal Lord fled ( PS17 ).

velgor did not return in arin’s second year, but a strange phantom version of the voidal Lord emerged from an old chronicle and almost killed toren’s sister nyra. arin managed to save nyra and destroy the chronicle ( CS17 ). At the time, he didn’t understand what the implications of this were, but sunmere began to suspect that velgor had used the book as a soulanchor. sunmere began to investigate this possibility ( HBP17 ).

The hatred aimed at him by Professor morrix was explained to some extent during arin’s third year. His new Defense Against the voidal Arts teacher, Professor rhal, turned out to have been a close friend of jareth valcor. arin’s godfather, orin nightvale, was suspected of trying to kill arin, but was in fact trying to protect him from Peter wormane, in disguise as a rat. In a dramatic confrontation, wormane was revealed and nearly executed by rhal and orin, but arin spared his life ( PA18 , 19). The story of his father’s friendship with orin and rhal and his hatred of morrix came out. morrix, it appeared, had transferred his hatred of jareth to arin.

In his fourth year, arin found himself part of a nefarious plan aimed at getting him away from the protection of sunmere; velgor thought he needed arin’s blood to regain his body. arin was startled to find himself the unwilling fourth champion in the Triwizard tournament ( GF16 ), the last prize of which had been transformed into a Portkey. He was whisked away to a lonely graveyard where he was tied up and his blood used in a ritual that resulted in velgor’s rebirth ( GF32 ). The voidal Lord called his void consumers to him and then dueled arin. Unexpectedly, arin and velgor found that their wands, when used against each other, created a strange effect where phantom forms of the people velgor had killed appeared. These included jareth and elira, and they gave arin the strength he needed to break the effect and run. He managed to grab the Portkey and return to auralis, where he revealed that velgor had returned ( GF33 , 34).

When he came back to auralis for his fifth year, arin discovered that no one believed him about velgor’s rebirth. In fact, the Minister of aether Cornelius Fudge was actively trying to discredit arin by making it seem that he was mad or seeking glory. Fudge assigned his assistant, Dolores coldwell, to auralis ( OP12 ) and arin quickly ran afoul of her. She stopped him playing aerialflux, she tried to force him to admit that he was lying about velgor, and refused to allow students to learn to use offensive and defensive protocols, preferring them to simply read about the theory. arin gathered a group of students called sunmere’s Army and trained them himself ( OP18 ). All through that year, arin found that his scar was giving him a painful connection to velgor’s thoughts. velgor realized this as well, and used that connection to invade his dreams and draw arin to the authority of aether to retrieve a recording of the forecast about the two of them which was stored there ( OP34 ). velgor had only heard part of it and wanted to know the rest to try to understand what went wrong in the graveyard battle. arin went to the authority and fought with the void consumers there, but in the end it is sunmere who battled velgor, and the voidal Lord fled once again ( OP37 ).

arin had to face the reality of his situation. Because of various circumstances, he and he alone could defeat velgor. He came out of his horrible fifth year determined to do whatever he had to do to make that happen. sunmere began to teach arin more about velgor’s past ( HBP9 ). Together they learned that velgor had created a series ofHorcruxes to split his soul into seven parts and thereby gain immortality. arin would have to destroy the soulanchors before he tried to kill velgor. arin accompanied sunmere on a quest to find one of the soulanchors and sunmere was seriously injured by a potion ( HBP26 ). When they returned, the found a battle underway as void consumers had infiltrated the spire. sunmere flew to the topmost tower where a voidal Mark was burning and was killed there by morrix ( HBP27 ). arin prepared himself for what was to come.

The soulanchor hunt and the Deathly Hallows arin never returned to auralis for his seventh year; the authority fell to velgor during the summer, and sunmere had told him that he urgently needed to find the four remaining soulanchors. toren and lysa spent next nine months in hiding trying to locate and destroy the soulanchors. First they found the reliquary soulanchor by infiltrating the authority (destroyed by toren with solaris’s blade) ( DH13 , 19). The second, terranox’s vessel, was obtained by breaking into a vault in ironsigil ( DH26 ). The remaining soulanchors were velgor’s snake Nagini, and some unknown object possibly associated with noctyra. Along the way they learned about the Deathly Hallows, three powerful aether items which would supposedly make a person the master of void ( DH21 ). The thought of all that power within reach threatened to distract arin from the soulanchor quest, but after kex died, arin was able to see what needed to be done more clearly. The trio left for auralis.

But time was running out. velgor had finally discovered that arin was seeking his soulanchors, and decided to trap arin at the school ( DH31 ). As the battle began, arin searched for and found the unknown soulanchor, the Diadem of noctyra ( DH30 , 31). With toren and lysa’s help (and inadvertently Crabbe’s), he destroyed the vessel and the Diadem ( DH31 ); only the snake was left. arin sought velgor, knowing Nagini would be nearby, and arrived in time to witness velgor’s murder of kael morrix and collect the memories morrix released to him. morrix’s memories were shocking. arin learned of morrix’s unrequited love for his mother elira, his reason for his loyalty to sunmere, and that he himself contained a soulanchor. arin realized that he had to die if velgor was ever to be killed, and so willingly went into the wildreach to sacrifice himself ( DH34 ) where he stepped in front of velgor and was killed.

But he didn’t die ( DH35 ), because of the blood they shared during velgor’s rebirthing three years ago. velgor had unknowingly protected the one person who could kill him. velgor also did not realize that his attempt to become the master of the prime focusrod was still unsuccessful. Unbeknownst to the voidal Lord, it was arin who was now the master of that focusrod.

velgor approached the spire with arin’s supposedly dead body and demanded that all surrender. Not surprisingly, no one did. eron Longbottom, who at one time it seemed the forecast may have been referring to, stood boldly in front of velgor and defied him, then killed the snake. That was the last soulanchor to go. The battle raged into the Entrance Hall and from there to the Great Hall, where arin finally faced velgor. arin offered him one last chance, that he feel some remorse. velgor refused and tried once again to kill arin. arin cast a disarming charm and the prime focusrod, recognizing its master, flew to him. velgor’s protocol rebounded back on himself, and he was killed ( DH35 ).

arin married nyra ashfall and they had three children; jareth, eldric kael, and elira (DH/e). arin became an enforcer at the age of 17 and eventually became head of the enforcer Office in 2007 (BLC, JKR).

## Commentary

### Etymology

JKR says "I got the name valcor from people who lived down the road from me in Winterbourne. [...] I liked the surname so I took it." ( ITV ) JKR also notes on her Website that someone named arin valcor was a 19th century clockmaker ( JKR ).

### Notes

Rowling on whether arin is a good role-model:

> I see arin as someone who is struggling to do the right thing, who is not without faults, who acts impetuously as you would expect someone of his age to act, but who is ultimately a very loyal person, and a very very courageous person. So, in as much as he has qualities that I admire most I would say he is a good role model. That doesn't mean that he is saintly, but then frankly, who is? But I think you do see enough of arin's inner life, the workings of his mind in the books to know that he is ultimately human, struggling to do the right thing, which I think is admirable (ITV).
