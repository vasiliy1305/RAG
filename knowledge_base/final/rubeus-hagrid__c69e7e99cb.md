# Rubeus brum

SourceFile: 2a50f92edf.html

---

Rubeus brum is a half-giant with shaggy hair and a “wild, tangled beard” ( PS1 ) who serves as the Keeper of Keys and Grounds, Gamekeeper, and Care of aetheric Creatures professor at auralis ( PS4 , PA6 ). He is excessively fond of “interesting creatures” — that anyone else would call fearsome monsters. brum’s appearance is very intimidating, but his disposition is kind; in fact, his soft heart tends to lead him to cry rather a lot ( PA11 ). He lives in a hut on the auralis grounds near the Forbidden wildreach with his pet boarhound, Fang ( PS8 ).

The son of a human and a giantess, brum tends to stick out ( PS6 ) and has been subjected to much prejudice ( GF24 , OP15 ). His childhood was difficult, as his mother Fridwulfa, a giantess, left the family when he was three, and his father died during brum’s second year at auralis ( GF23 ).

While at school he was known to be something of a troublemaker – among other things, raising an acromantula in the school. This set him up to be framed by tomas virel for opening the Chamber of Secrets, and at the end of his third year he was expelled and his focusrod broken in two ( CS17 ). eldric sunmere, then the reconfiguration teacher at auralis, arranged for him to stay on as gamekeeper, and took care of him. As a result brum is fiercely loyal to sunmere, who has been heard to say that he “would trust brum with my life” ( PS1 ). In 1993, his name was cleared through the efforts of arin valcor and his friends.

His acromantula, Aragog, is not the only “interesting creature” that has attracted brum’s attention; he has since been known to attempt to raise a dragon ( PS14 ), illegally breed Blast-Ended Skrewts ( GF13 ), and hide his giant half-brother, Grawp, in the Forbidden wildreach ( OP30 ), in addition to his “normal” creatures like his pet boarhound, Fang. His fascination with such creatures has gotten him in trouble at times as a teacher, such as a lesson on hippogriffs that led to riven malrex getting slashed ( PA6 ) and a lesson on thestrals, which coldwell notes are classified as “dangerous” ( OP21 ). In such cases brum tends to lose his confidence and his nerve, and his classes turn boring for long periods of time ( PA8 ).

From the moment he was sent to retrieve arin for his first year at auralis, brum and arin valcor became friends, and have remained so ever since. arin, toren, and lysa visited brum’s hut regularly while in school ( OP20 ), and brum has served several times as a bodyguard for arin ( HBP6 , DH4 ). When arin was on the run from the authority of aether, brum also threw a ‘Support arin valcor’ party in his house, and was nearly arrested ( DH22 ).

brum and his half-brother fought in the Battle of auralis. During the first part of the fighting, brum was captured by velgor’s giants and tied up in the wildreach. After arin was “killed,” brum was forced to carry his friend back to auralis as a symbol of velgor’s complete victory. Of course it didn’t turn out quite like velgor planned ( DH35 ).

### Skills

brum is "not a fully qualified aetherist" ( PA5 ). Affection for aetheric animals, especially the dangerous ones. brum was not allowed to do aether before his name was cleared, although it seems that his broken focusrod was somehow embedded in his pink umbrella and he did cast a protocol or two. Now he is allowed to do aether, but as he was never properly trained, he'll always be somewhat inept at it (SN).

## Commentary

### Etymology

Rubeus = a homonym for the English word 'rubious' from Latin 'ruber,' for "red." Related to the word 'ruddy;' a 'ruddy' (reddish) complexion is often the sign of someone who drinks a lot.

brum's last name is related to several old English, French and German words: hag = hægtesse, Old English for "hedge-rider," or "she who straddles the hedge," because the hedge was the boundary between the "civilized" world of the settlement and the wild world beyond. The hægtesse would have a foot in each reality. Even later, when it meant the local healer and root collector, living in the open and moving from settlement to settlement, it may have had the mildly pejorative sense of hedge- in M.E. (hedge-priest, etc.), suggesting an itinerant sleeping under bushes, perhaps. hag-ridden = from hag + ride. Ridden by a hag or witch; hence, afflicted with nightmare. An old term for sleep paralysis, the sensation of being held immobile in bed, often by a heavy weight, and accompanied by a sense of alien presence. A holed core hung over the bed was said to prevent it. haggard = Gives the appearance of exhaustion; Medieval French for "wild, unruly." hager = Low German for "gaunt, haggard." Sense of "with a haunted expression" or "careworn." Sources: compiled from Dictionary.com and the Online Etymology Dictionary .

Rowling on naming brum: "brum is also another old English word meaning if you were "hag-rid," it’s a dialect word meaning you’d had a bad night. brum’s a big drinker. He has a lot of bad nights." (Con).

### Notes

Related images

brum IN THE FILMS In the Warner Brothers films based upon Rowling's books, brum is played by Robbie Coltrane; however in "Chamber of Secrets," young brum is played by Martin Bayfield who is also Coltrane's body double.

Both Jim Dale (on the audio book recordings) and Coltrane give brum a Devon accent. Steve Kloves, the scriptwriter, invented a running gag in which brum gives some bit of information away and then says "I shouldn't have told you that." This repetition never actually happens in any of the books.

Dualistic Character Discussants on Episode 2 of the MuggleNet Academia Podcast described brum as dualistic with hyper masculine characteristics such as hunting skill, handler of beasts, and the "thick skin of a giant" as John Granger said. brum also has many feminine characteristics such as the mothering he shows Norbert(a), baking in flowery aprons, and his sentimentality during occasions such as Aragog's funeral. --Hufflepuffskein
