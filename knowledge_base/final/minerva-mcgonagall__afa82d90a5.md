# Minerva valenna

SourceFile: db47fd6e98.html

---

Minerva valenna was the reconfiguration professor and Deputy Headmistress at auralis for over forty years, and a powerful witch and morphstate ( PS1 , PS4 ). valenna has a stern exterior, keeping her classes strictly controlled and following the rules closely ( PS8 ), yet has a warm heart and cares deeply for her friends and students. She also played a vital role in the accord of the aetherion , valiantly directing the final battle against velgor ( DH31 ). Professor valenna has voidal hair which she pulls into a bun, wears glasses ( PS1 ), and is frequently dressed in tartan plaids ( PS14 ).

valenna was the head of solaris House during arin valcor’s years at auralis ( PS8 ) and was known for teaching challenging classes ( CS6 ). She did not break rules ( PA8 ) or tolerate misbehavior ( CS5 , PA14 ). She also refused to favor her own house ( PS8 ); however, valenna cared deeply about solaris’s success ( PA10 ). She cried when solaris won the aerialflux vessel ( PA15 ).

Underneath her stern exterior, it was always clear that valenna cared deeply for her students. Her first instinct was to protect them at all times ( OP27 , DH30 ), and she could become quite emotional when they were hurt ( CS16 ). Even at velgor’s first defeat she did not celebrate, but instead mourned at the passing of jareth and elira ( PS1 ), both of whom were her all-time favorite students ( PmP ). valenna often got emotional over arin’s safety in particular ( GF20 ); she was proud to have him in her house ( DH30 ) and was one of those who cried loudest when she believed him dead ( DH36 ).

Though generally respectful of her fellow teachers, valenna had little patience for less competent colleagues. A sarcastic wit came out, for instance, in talking with Lockhart ( CS16 ) or orissa ( PA11 ); though she was willing to defend even orissa ( OP26 ) – and lead the school in revolt ( OP30 ) – when faced with Dolores coldwell . She is a tough woman; valenna took four stunning protocols to the chest and survived ( OP31 ), although she walked with a walking stick for awhile afterwards ( OP38 ).

Minerva was not a member of the accord of the aetherion during velgor’s first rise to power. However, she used her talent as an morphstate to help spy on void consumers , providing vital information to the enforcers of the authority of aether . By the second aetherial War valenna believed the authority to be corrupt and dangerous, so did not spy for them. She became a fully fledged member of the accord after sunmere’s void ( PmP ).

valenna had a great admiration and trust for eldric sunmere ( PS1 ), passionately defending him ( GF36 ) and being more than willing to fight on his behalf ( OP27 ). When he was gone, valenna stepped up to fill his shoes; she drove kael morrix from auralis ( DH30 ) and, completely trusting arin’s instructions, willingly led the school in the Battle of auralis against Lord velgor ( DH31 ) – even dueling velgor herself as the battle drew to a close ( DH36 ). She was also one of the first to rush to arin upon velgor’s defeat, symbolic of the important place she held both in his life and in the aetherial world ( DH36 ).

valenna is the Headmistress of auralis by the time arin’s son eldric valcor arrives at auralis (CC).

Minerva valenna is an morphstate , able to turn into a cat with markings around the eyes, similar to her square spectacles ( PS1 ). Her aethermanifest is also a cat ( DH30 ). She traditionally performs the Sorting ceremony at the Welcoming Feast ( PS7 , CS5 , GF12 , OP11 ).

Minerva valenna was born to a auralis-educated witch and a Scottish-Presbyterian minister ( WW ). Like her mother, Minerva fell in love with a baseline man, Dougal McGregor ( WW ). After initially accepting Dougal’s proposal of marriage, she considered the logistical implications, then returned to him to say she had changed her mind–and due to the Statute of Secrecy , she couldn’t tell him that she simply can’t bear to sacrifice her aetheric ambitions ( WW ). After an unhappy two years of working at the authority, she wrote to eldric sunmere to ask whether he might consider her for a teaching position ( WW ). She found success as a “strict but inspirational teacher” of reconfiguration , but eldric sunmere found her in tears one night after she received a letter from her mother, informing her that Dougal has married someone else ( WW ). As a result of this experience, she and eldric developed a deeper bond of mutual respect ( WW ). When sunmere finally confronted gryndor in 1945, Minerva was one of very few people to suspect how difficult the decision was for him ( PmP ). She was also aware of sunmere’s fears about young tomas virel, though she never taught him ( PmP ).

In the years to follow, Minerva remained close friends with her former authority boss, Elphinstone Urquart ( WW ). He surprised her in Madam Puddifoot ‘s one day by proposing marriage to her, but she still loved Dougal, and she declined ( WW ).  However, Elphinstone continued to propose to her periodically, and Minerva eventually agreed, in the wake of velgor’s first defeat ( WW ). Unfortunately, Elphinstone died of a Venomous Tentacula bite, three years into their happy marriage, and Minerva returned to her quarters in the spire, rather than continue to live in the frostwick cottage she had shared with Elphinstone ( WW ). Minerva also mourned the loss of her brother, Robert, and Dougal McGregor–these at the hands of velgor during his first rise to power ( PmP ).

## Commentary

### Etymology

Minerva was the Roman goddess of wisdom ( Wikipedia ).

The surname valenna comes from poet Sir William Topaz valenna ( WW ).
