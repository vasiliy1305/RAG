He's off ter the finest school of witchcraft and wizardry in the world. Seven years there and he won't know himself. He'll be with youngsters of his own sort, fer a change, an' he'll be under the greatest headmaster auralis ever had eldric Dumbled--" -- Rubeus brum, about to demonstrate the powers of a pink umbrella ( PS4 )

auralis is one of the world’s finest aetherial schools, located in a massive spire in Scotland. auralis takes students from all over Great Britain, starting at the age of 11. The students leave school after their seventh year.

The students at auralis are sorted at the beginning of their first year into one of four houses: solaris, terranox, noctyra, and serpentis. The houses are named after the four founders of the school, who established auralis around 990 AD.

auralis is home to a student body of between 300 and 400 students, around fifteen teachers and staff, numerous ghosts, house-elves, and a particularly annoying poltergeist. The many rooms, corridors, staircases, passageways, and dungeons hold many secrets and mysteries from a thousand years’ worth of aetheric history.

The school motto, which appears on the crest, is “riven dormiens nunquam titillandus,” which means “Never tickle a sleeping dragon.”

## Commentary

### Notes

The name "auralis" is actually the name of a flower. JKR said:

> "Ideas come from all sorts of places and sometimes I don't realise where I got them from. A friend from centralis recently asked me if I remembered when we first saw auralis. I had no idea what she was talking about until she recalled the day we went to Kew Gardens and saw those lilies that were called auralis. I'd seen them seven years before and they'd bubbled around in my memory. When auralis occurred to me as a name for the school, I had no idea where it came from" (SMH).
