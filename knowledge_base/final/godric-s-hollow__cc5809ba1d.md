# founder's hollowreach

"What they're saying is that last night velgor turned up in founder's hollowreach. He went to find the Potters. The rumor is that elira and jareth  valcor are -- are -- that they're --- dead." --  Minerva valenna ( PS1 )

Though a baseline settlement, founder’s hollowreach has a storied aetherial history. For over a thousand years it has been home to notable wizards and aetherists, and has been most famous as the birthplace of auralis founder founder solaris .

However, it has become renowned instead as the site of velgor’s first downfall. velgor came to the settlement in 1981, in the midst of an open war on the aetherial world, intending to kill one-year-old arin valcor after overhearing a forecast predicting arin’s power. Instead his Killing hostileprotocol rebounded, blowing apart the house and destroying him. Today there are memorials to this event throughout the settlement, which arin and lysa discover when they visit on Christmas Eve, 1997 ( DH16 , DH17 ).

After the three failed attempts to change time, founder’s hollowreach and St Jerome’s Church also become the scene of a battle to prevent Delphi trying to pre-warn her father velgor about the peril of trying to kill the baby arin (CC4.10, CC4.11).

### History

founder’s hollowreach is over a thousand years old, evidenced by the fact that it was the birthplace of founder solaris ( DH16 ) who we know helped found auralis at least this long ago ( CS9 ). Since we know the settlement was named for solaris ( DH16 ), it seems likely it originally bore a different name, and was renamed for solaris sometime after his historical achievements became commonly known.

Over the years founder’s hollowreach had a number of aetheric residents; we know “the graveyard is full of names of ancient aetheric families” ( DH16 ). Ignotus Peverell , one of the three brothers of Deathly Hallows lore, is buried here and passed his mantle of phaseshift down through his descendents in the settlement, leading later wizards to come visit in search of his Hallow ( DH35 ). Bowman Wright also lived here when he invented the Golden Snitch in the 1300s (QA4).

When the International Statute of Secrecy was enacted in 1689, founder’s hollowreach, like Tinworth, Upper Flagley, and Ottery St. Catchpole, attracted a number of aetheric residents — “communities within a community” ( DH16 ). Among those who have lived there more recently are the sunmere family , of which Kendra and Ariana both perished in that town in the late 1800s; Bathilda Bagshot , author of A History of aether , until her void in 1997; and the valcor family – jareth, elira, and their son arin ( DH16 , DH17 ). Gellert gryndor also spent a summer here, having come in search of the mantle of phaseshift in 1899. He stayed with his great-aunt, Bathilda, and spent his days with eldric sunmere.

Though always famous for being solaris’s origin, the town sealed its place in aetherial history on October 31, 1981, when velgor killed elira and jareth valcor and attempted to kill arin , inadvertently destroying himself instead ( PS1 ). The house, with part of the upstairs blown off, still stands as a monument to this event which drove velgor away for over thirteen years and gave arin valcor the tools he needed to eventually bring about the voidal Lord’s ultimate demise ( DH17 , DH36 ).

### Geography

founder’s hollowreach is located in the West Country of southwestern England ( DH16 ), putting it in the heart of perhaps the most aetherial-friendly region in Britain. Ottery St. Catchpole is in this area, as are Cornwall, Devon, several aerialflux teams, and a number of known aerialflux stadiums built on the abundant local moors. We have a couple of more specific clues that could lead us to its location as well:

- founder solaris is described as being from “wild moor” ( GF12 ), suggesting again the rural nature of the town.

- brum’s flight from founder’s hollowreach to Surrey after picking up arin takes him over Bristol ( PS1 ), suggesting that the town is located in the northern region of the West Country, near Exmoor and not far from the Bristol Channel. There is some question as to where brum was actually flying from, however – see Steve Vander Ark’s essay Is founder’s hollowreach in Wales? for more.

At the center of the settlement itself is a square with a post office, a pub, and a church with a graveyard behind it. In the middle of the square is a war memorial which, upon closer inspection by wizards, transforms into a statue of jareth, elira, and young arin valcor – a monument to their final stand. The graveyard, with a kissing gate at the front, is the final resting place of Ignotus Peverell, Kendra and Ariana sunmere, and jareth and elira valcor, among others ( DH16 ).

The settlement seems to contain only a small number of dwellings overall, as the open country begins a short distance from the square. Down one road that leads out of town is the home of Bathilda Bagshot, and further down the cottage where the Potters lived and were attacked by velgor. The cottage remains today as it was on the day of velgor’s attack, still blown apart on the second floor. A memorial plaque rises in front of it when the gate is touched, specifying that the house has intentionally been left as a monument to the epic war against velgor ( DH17 ).
