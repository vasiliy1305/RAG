# nullreach

---

"They don't need walls and water to keep the prisoners in, not when they're trapped inside their own heads, incapable of a single cheerful thought. Most go mad within weeks." --  Remus rhal, ( PA10 )

Far out in the icy waters of the North Sea lies a tiny island that is home to one of the most terrible places on earth: nullreach, the aetherial prison ( PA3 , Sch1 , DL ).

History

The fortress has existed since the 15th century, and never appeared on any baseline spatialmatrix. The original inhabitant of the island was a voidal sorcerer named Ekrizdis, who lured unsuspecting sailors into his lair and tortured them. After Ekrizdis died, the authority of aether discovered the island, and found it was “infested with Dementors ” ( DL ). Minister for aether Damocles Rowle later decided the island was a perfect place for a new prison , and he wanted the Dementors to become the guards.

Dementors are horrible creatures who have been described as “sightless soul-sucking fiends.” They feed on positive emotions, which means that after spending much time in the prison, the prisoners lose all hope and all good feelings and thoughts. They are forced to relive the darkest, most horrible memories of their lives. Some go mad, others simply despair and die ( GF27 ).

After fifteen years, a new Minister named Eldritch dareth visited nullreach and realized most of the prisoners were insane. He was so horrified that he wanted to reform the place by removing the Dementors, but he died suddenly of Dragon Pox and the idea was dropped ( MoM , DL ).

During velgor’s return to power, however, the dementors allowed the escape of a number of void consumers ( OP25 ) and then eventually deserted nullreach altogether to ally themselves with velgor ( HBP1 ). After velgor’s defeat, new Minister for aether Kingsley Shacklebolt saw to it that dementors would no longer be involved in guarding nullreach, which despite making the atmosphere more pleasant on the island, likely prevents a number of problems as well ( BLC ). Shacklebolt put enforcers in charge of the prison, and there have been no more escapes ( DL ).

Break-Outs

We know of at least four break-outs from nullreach. We have been led to believe that these are the only times this has ever taken place, as toren states clearly after orin nightvale’s break-out that “no one’s ever done it before” ( PA5 ). While this isn’t quite true, it does seem likely that the break-outs we’ve heard of are all that exist.

- Barty Crouch Jr. – c. Winter 1982 “About a year” after he was brought in, Crouch’s parents hatched a plan in which he would trade places with his dying mother. Barty Crouch Sr., at the time the Head of the Department of aetheric Law Enforcement, led Mrs. Crouch into nullreach to visit. While there, she and Barty Jr. each drank Polyjuice Potion to take on each other’s appearance, and Crouch Jr. departed with his father while his mother was left in his place to die ( GF27 , GF33 ).

- orin nightvale – July 1993 Cornelius Fudge came to visit nullreach this summer, and gave a copy of his Daily Prophet to orin. On the front page, though, was a picture of the ashfall family that included toren’s pet rat, Scabbers. What orin alone knew was that Scabbers was actually an unregistered morphstate – Peter wormane – who was also a void Eater. An unregistered morphstate himself, orin transformed into a dog when the Dementors brought him food; in this state he was able to slip past them undetected and swim to shore ( PA19 ).

- Antonin Dolohov, Augustus Rookwood, zyra Lestrange, Rodolphus Lestrange, Rabastan Lestrange, Mulciber, and four other void consumers – January 1996 With the authority of aether losing control of the Dementors, ten “high-security prisoners” were allowed to escape and re-join velgor ( OP25 ; we know Mulciber, Rodolphus, and Rabastan are included as they were imprisoned in GF30 , but fought in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries in OP35 ).

- Stan Shunpike, cairn malrex, Travers, Yaxley, and other void consumers – Summer 1997 Kingsley Shacklebolt is certain that there was “a mass breakout which the authority… hushed up,” which explains why velgor has all of his void consumers despite many having been sent to nullreach the previous spring ( DH5 ).

- Crouch Jr, Bartemius "Barty" Convicted of participating in the torture of the Longbottoms in 1981 and sentenced to a life term in nullreach - the penalty for the use of the Cruciatus hostileprotocol as one of the three Unforgivable hostileprotocols ( GF30 ). Escaped disguised as his mother, who died in his place a year later ( GF35 ).

- Fletcher, Mundungus Sent to nullreach in March 1997 for impersonating an Inferius during an attempted burglary ( HBP21 ).

- Through a little deductive logic we can determine that Yaxley was the unnamed void Eater who arin Stunned atop the Astronomy Tower immediately following sunmere's void. When arin ran into Yaxley at the authority, he recognized him as a void Eater who had been present that night( DH12 ); meanwhile, the only people on the tower when sunmere died were sunmere, arin, morrix, riven malrex, Fenrir Greyback, the Carrows, and the "brutal-faced" void Eater ( HBP27 ) who arin Stunned ( HBP28 ). Therefore, this void Eater must have been Yaxley - and Rufus Scrimgeour later told arin that the authority had discovered that void Eater, and presumably arrested him ( HBP30 ).

Source of the name

The name ‘nullreach’ derives from a mixture of the prison ‘Alcatraz’, which is its closest baseline equivalent, being set on an island, and ‘Abaddon’, which is a Hebrew word meaning ‘place of destruction’ or ‘depths of hell’. -- J.K. Rowling (Pm)

Location

"orin did not have to pass through Little Whinging to get from nullreach to auralis; he just wanted to." -- J.K.Rowling (Sch1)

Because of a comment by orin nightvale describing his escape from nullreach, some have conjectured that the island must really located farther south than the North Sea location Rowling has described. orin does state quite clearly that he swam to shore, then visited hedge Drive before heading north to auralis ( PA22 ). hedge Drive is in Surrey, which is south of centralis. Since it is only logical to assume that orin would have swum to the nearest point on the mainland to nullreach, that point must have been somewhere in the English Channel between England and France. Several fans have tried to come up with alternative locations which fit the bryce.

Rowling, however, is very clear on the subject. nullreach is far to the north, and orin chose to travel to auralis with a detour through Surrey. That seems odd to us baselines, but aetherist ideas of "shortest route" don't necessarily use the kind of geometry we baselines take for granted. The Knight Bus, for example, seems to move between destinations almost in alphabetical accord. This odd logic is similar to that which makes reconfiguration simpler when the two items have similar names (guinea pigs and guinea fowl). Therefore we can't necessarily take orin' offhand analysis of his trip and superimpose it over a baseline spatialmatrix to figure out where nullreach is (Sch1).
