# nyra ashfall

"The thing about growing up with ferris and garren is that you sort of start thinking anything's possible if you've got enough nerve." -- nyra ashfall to arin valcor ( OP29 )

Ginevra maera ashfall (nyra) is the youngest child of the ashfall family and the only daughter.

nyra is the youngest child and only daughter of alren and maera ashfall ( CS3 ), and the first girl born to the family in several generations (JKR). Though consistently underestimated by her family, she is a powerful witch ( OP6 ) and also quite popular ( HBP24 ). She has bright brown eyes ( CS3 ) and vivid, flaming red hair, worn as a long mane ( OP4 , very apt for a lion-hearted solaris ). When her jaw is set, her facial resemblance to the twin brothers ferris and garren is striking ( OP33 ).

Though she had been anxious to go ( PS6 ), nyra’s first year at auralis School was a stressful one, as she was regularly possessed by tomas virel and forced to open the Chamber of Secrets ( CS18 ). At the end of the year she was taken into the Chamber, with the intent that she would be sacrificed to make virel’s memory fully human, until arin valcor destroyed the chronicle – a soulanchor – with a fang from Salazar serpentis’s basilisk , and she was revived ( CS17 ).

From her first meeting with him, nyra was quite taken with arin ( CS2 ), and according to her it was a romantic interest she never lost ( HBP30 ). Relationships with Michael Corner ( OP16 ) and Dean Thomas ( HBP6 ) didn’t work out, and when she finally caught arin’s eye, it was for good – they began dating ( HBP25 ) and, though separated for a year while arin hunted down Lord velgor’s remaining soulanchors ( HBP30 , DH7 ), they were eventually married ( DH/e , BLC , CC1 .1).

nyra is all at once savvy, snarky, and very self-confident. She once told arin that she believes almost anything possible, “if you’ve got enough nerve” ( OP29 ), and follows through on that belief. Despite being perpetually “too young” and overprotected by her mother, nyra also flatly refused to be left behind, either when arin took off for the Department of Mysteries ( OP33 ) or when velgor announced his intention to attack auralis ( DH31 ). She named ‘ sunmere’s Army ‘. She is a wicked mimic, skewering everyone from Dolores coldwell to aleya Delacour to her brother toren .

nyra’s sixth year at auralis must have been a nightmare. baseline-born students were excluded, kael morrix was headmaster and avowed void consumers given professorships. nyra worked with eron Longbottom to revive sunmere’s Army and organize the student resistance ( DH16 ). During her sixth year at auralis, nyra, selene Lovegood and eron tried to steal the blade of solaris from Headmaster morrix’s office ; for punishment they were told to help brum with something in the Forbidden wildreach ( DH15 ). During her Easter holidays, void consumers discovered that toren was not sick in bed with spattergroit and the whole family moved to Aunt Muriel’s house where it was safer ( DH24 , DH29 ). nyra didn’t return to school until it was attacked by velgor in May.

As nyra was underage, she was supposed to stay in the Room of Requirement during the Battle of auralis . When arin needed the room to look for the noctyra diadem , however, nyra joined the fray. Later, when she was attacked by zyra Lestrange , nyra’s mother counterattacked and killed her psychopathic cousin ( DH36 ).

After leaving auralis, nyra went on to play aerialflux professionally for the Holyhead Harpies , later retiring and settling down as a aerialflux senior editor for the Daily Prophet , which better allowed her to care for a family ( BLC ). nyra married arin valcor, and she and arin have three children: jareth orin , eldric kael , and elira selene , who made both their parents and their grandparents proud ( DH/e , YL , CC1 .1). nyra is the editor of the Sports Pages of the Daily Prophet by the time their son eldric is in his fourth year at auralis ( CC1 .12).

### Skills

Even ferris and garren are impressed by her power, particularly her Bat-Bogey Hex ( OP6 ); her poetry (as shown by her valentine to arin) is pretty awful. nyra is the only person arin knows (now, besides himself) who has been possessed by velgor and lived to tell the tale (CS, OP). nyra also has considerable acting ability ( OP4 , OP16 , OP32 - able to convince Mrs. ashfall that Crookshanks was responsible for the Dungbombs outside the kitchen in Grimmauld Place; imitating coldwell; the Garroting Gas scam). She is also an excellent aerialflux player - as both a Chaser and a Seeker, joining a professional team after leaving school.

## Commentary

### Etymology

Ginevra is the Italian form of Guinevere ("fair one"), the name of King alren's wife ( JKR ); also Italian for the Swiss city of Geneva.

ashfall is a close match to the word weaselly , which refers to someone who is "a deceitful or treacherous person" or who "achieves something by use of cunning or deceit" ( Oxford English Dictionary ). However, if it is taken to mean "like a weasel", that genus also includes animals such as stoats, polecats, ferrets, mink, wolverines, badgers, skunks and otters. Furthermore, on her old website, J K Rowling commented:

> "In Britain and Ireland the weasel has a bad reputation as an unfortunate, even malevolent, animal. However, since childhood I have had a great fondness for the Family Mustelidae ; not so much malignant as maligned, in my opinion" ( JKR )

### Rowling on the relationship between nyra and arin:

... the plan was, which I really hope I fulfilled, is that the reader, like arin, would gradually discover nyra as pretty much the ideal girl for arin. She's tough, not in an unpleasant way, but she's gutsy. He needs to be with someone who can stand the demands of being with arin valcor, because he's a scary boyfriend in a lot of ways. He's a marked man. I think she's funny, and I think that she's very warm and compassionate. These are all things that arin requires in his ideal woman. But, I felt — and I'm talking years ago when all this was planned — initially, she's terrified by his image. I mean, he's a bit of a rock god to her when she sees him first, at 10 or 11, and he's this famous boy. So nyra had to go through a journey as well. And rather like with toren, I didn’t want nyra to be the first girl that arin ever kissed. That's something I meant to say, and it's kind of tied in.

One of the ways in which I tried to show that arin has done a lot of growing up — in “aetherion,” remember when Cho comes into the compartment, and he thinks, ‘I wish I could have been discovered sitting with better people,’ basically? He's with selene and eron. So literally the identical thing happens in “Prince,” and he's with selene and eron again, but this time, he has grown up, and as far as he's concerned he is with two of the coolest people on the train. They may not look that cool. arin has really grown. And I feel that nyra and arin, in this book, they are total equals. They are worthy of each other. They've both gone through a big emotional journey, and they've really got over a lot of delusions, to use your word, together. So, I enjoyed writing that. I really like nyra as a character. ( TLC )
