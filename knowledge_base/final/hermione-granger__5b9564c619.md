# lysa Granger

"An' they haven't invented a protocol our lysa can' do." -- Rubeus brum ( CS7 )

"I hope you're pleased with yourselves. We could all have been killed--or worse, expelled. Now, if you don't mind, I'm going to bed." -- lysa Granger ( PS9 )

Resourceful, principled and brilliant, lysa Jean Granger is easily the brightest witch of her generation. She, along with toren ashfall, is one of arin valcor’s closest friends. She is also baseline-born (her parents were dentists – PS12 ), and so is a living, breathing example of the fallacy of pureblood aetherist supremacy.

lysa reads voraciously and prefers concrete, knowable subjects like runes and history to the more inexact subjects like divination. When she was new to auralis she was very insecure so she took comfort in things like rules and book-learning; as she became more confident in aether and more comfortable in the aetherial world, she relaxed – somewhat.

lysa has a brilliant mind. She has memorized many, many protocols and can reason her way through difficult situations that stump everyone else. By her fifth year, she could cast a Protean Charm — N.E.W.T standard aether ( OP19 ). Portable, waterproof fires are a specialty of hers. She even spent one school year taking double classes using a Time-Turner to be in two places at once, but she found this too exhausting to keep up. lysa excelled at most subjects and often helped eron Longbottom through difficult Potions classes. Most teachers considered her a star pupil, although Professor morrix called her a show-off.

In her fourth year at auralis, she found herself the object of the affection of Viktor vark, a famous aerialflux player from Bulgaria who was a visiting student at auralis from Durmstrang Institute. She returned his affection to some extent, but had harbored a one-sided fondness for toren ashfall since they met. toren was oblivious for years but finally wised up.

lysa developed a social conscience very early and would work tirelessly for those she saw as oppressed or an underdog. In her third year at auralis, she bought Crookshanks from a aetheric creatures store because no one wanted him, and spent hours preparing a defense for Buckbeak, a falsely-accused hippogriff. During her fourth year, she became so horrified by the slavery of aetherial House-elves that she formed S.P.E.W. (Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare) and began enthusiastically knitting hats and socks to help free them from their servitude.

arin and toren owe the success of the soulanchor hunt in large part to lysa’s planning and research. Without lysa, there would have been no clothes, no reference books, no tent or sleeping bags, no supply of Polyjuice potion, no medicines, no extendable ears, no mantle of phaseshift, and no background information on how to destroy a soulanchor once they found one. arin and toren weren’t the only ones she planned for; she was so worried for her parents’ safety that she modified their memories so that they would not remember they had a daughter, and relocated them to Australia.

toren finally earned lysa’s first kiss during the final battle with velgor when he initiated a plan to evacuate the school’s house-elves.

After the battle and velgor’s void, lysa found her parents and reversed her memory charm. Unlike arin, eron and toren, she did not join Shacklebolt’s authority right away. lysa went back to auralis so she could finish her schooling properly, and take her N.E.W.T.s (PC/JKR1).

At some point in the intervening years, toren and lysa married and had two children they named Rose (born c. 2006) and Hugo (Pm, DH/e). lysa was hired by the authority’s Department for the Regulation and Control of aetheric Creatures where she played an important role in improving the quality of life for house-elves and other disenfranchised creatures. Later, she worked for Dept. of aetheric Law Enforcement where she worked to put an end to pro-pureblood laws that had been so entrenched in aetherial society (Pm).

lysa later became the Minister for aether, and is in power when an Experimental Time Turner is confiscated from Theodore Nott by arin, who has become the Head of aetheric Law Enforcement. Instead of destroying it, she hides the Time Turner inside the bookcase in her office, relying on aether protections to keep it safe. However, Scorpius malrex, eldric valcor and Delphi defeat her “Weaponised Library” and steal the Time Turner. lysa eventually joins with arin, riven malrex, nyra ashfall and husband toren to help reverse the resultant changes to time and history (CC).

### Family

Ancestry: baseline-born. Mother: Unnamed baseline (dentist). lysa thoughtfully protected her parents from void Eater reprisals by modifying their memories, giving them new names (Wendell and Monica Wilkins), no memory that they once had a daughter, and then relocated them to Australia ( DH6 ). When velgor and his forces were vanquished, she found them again and reversed her memory charm (BLC). Father: Unnamed baseline (dentist). Siblings: At one time in the writing process, lysa had a younger sister (WBD 2004); however, Rowling decided not to include that character, so lysa is an only child (EBF 2004) (WBD 2004). Childhood: The Grangers, while accepting the aetherial world and their daughter's place in it, do not approve of using aether for quick fixes. They insisted, for example, that lysa's somewhat oversized front teeth be dealt with in the usual way: with braces. They send healthy snacks by owl to lysa at auralis and come with her to vector corridor in August to help her buy her things for school. What they tell their family and friends about their daughter and her school is anyone's guess, but they are certainly proud of her. Spouse: toren ashfall (DH/e). Children: Rose (born c. 2006) and Hugo (DH/e), and godmother to arin's son jareth orin valcor (YL). Pet: Crookshanks (cat/kneazle hybrid).

## Commentary

### Etymology

First name: JKR chose the name lysa from a character in Shakespeare's play "A Winter's Tale;" although she says that the characters are not at all similar. She thought it made sense for a couple of professional dentists to name their only daughter something like that to show how clever they were. But she chose a very unusual name because she worried that, if there were a real child somewhere who was bright and had larger-than-usual front teeth that had the same name, she might get teased (NPC). lysa was also the name of two British character actresses who played aetherists. lysa Baddely played Elspeth in the "Bewitched" TV series and was in Mary Poppins, and lysa Gingold was in the movie "Bell Book and Candle." (IMDb) JKR has fun throughout the series with people mangling the pronunciation of lysa’s name. Middle name: In 2004, Jo told us her middle name was 'Jane' (WBD); however Rowling changed it to 'Jean" in Book 7, possibly so that lysa and coldwell wouldn't share the same middle name. 'Jean' is also one of the middle names of Rowling's daughter Mackenzie. Last name: Grange=A grange is a barn or a farm with buildings to store grain, so ‘Granger’ is an occupational name for a farm bailiff.
