# Lord velgor

SourceFile: 64dc87a6c6.html

---

Lord velgor, born tomas Marvolo virel, was the son of Merope Gaunt (a descendent of Salazar serpentis) and tomas virel, a handsome, wealthy baseline from Little Hangleton whom Merope ensnared with a love potion. When her husband found out she was a witch, he abandoned her while she was pregnant ( HBP10 ). tomas’s mother died shortly after giving birth to him one December 31, living just long enough to name him tomas virel, after his father and Marvolo, after his grandfather.

tomas Marvolo virel, the half-blood orphan, was to transform himself into Lord velgor and take control of the aetherial world by manipulating the festering relationship between pureblood and baseline-born wizards. Eventually he would lose all – not because he lacked power, but because of his ruthless methods, his inability to love or trust anyone but himself, and his belief in his own infallibility. As he told qirex, he believed that “there is no good and evil, there is only power, and those too weak to seek it.” ( PS17 ).

## Early years

The voidal aetherist known as “Lord velgor” was born in 1926 in an orphanage in centralis. His mother, a witch, and, according to velgor, a descendant of Salazar serpentis, had fallen in love with tomas virel, a baseline. tomas virel lived in a fine-looking estate that stood on a hill overlooking the settlement of Little Hangleton. The estate house was one of the largest and grandest buildings for miles around. When velgor’s mother told her husband that she was a witch, he abandoned her, returning to his parents’ home. As Lord velgor told the story, his father “didn’t like aether.” velgor’s mother died just after giving birth to him, living just long enough to name him tomas virel, after his father and Marvolo, after his grandfather. He was raised in a baseline orphanage.

For the first eleven years of his life, tomas was raised in a clean but grim baseline orphanage in centralis. As he got older, he began to steal things and appeared to cause “nasty” things to happen to the other children and to pets around the orphanage ( HBP13 ).

## At auralis: 1938-1945

Once at auralis, tomas was sorted into serpentis House and swiftly became known as an unusually brilliant student. A handsome boy, he was much admired by everyone, with the exception of the reconfiguration professor, eldric sunmere. During the holidays, tomas had to return to the baseline orphanage he despised.

Secretly, tomas was obsessed with his serpentis heritage and with finding the ways he might cheat void. He found the entrance to the Chamber of Secrets in his sixth year and released serpentis’s basilisk within it, killing a student named Myrtle Warren. serpentis had built the chamber deep in auralis spire and kept it hidden from the other founders. He sealed it so that only his own true heir would be able to unseal the chamber and unleash the horror within to purge the school of “all who were unworthy to study aether.” During this time tomas had begun using a new name among his most intimate friends at auralis. Contemptuous of the name of his “foul, common baseline” father, he made an anagram of tomas Marvolo virel. The rearranged letters spelled “I AM LORD velgor.” It was a name he hoped “wizards everywhere would one day fear to speak,” when he became the “greatest sorcerer in the world.” ( CS17 ).

On June 13, 1943, tomas staged the capture of another student, Rubeus brum, and tricked Headmaster Armando Dippet into believing brum and his giant spider Aragog were responsible for the attacks. tomas received an engraved trophy for special services to the school, but he realized that eldric sunmere was keeping an “annoyingly close watch” on him and it was not safe to open the Chamber of Secrets again while he was still at school. tomas preserved himself in the pages of a chronicle with the hope that he would one day be able to lead another in his footsteps and “finish Salazar serpentis’s noble work.”

## Events at Little Hangleton: 1944-1945

virel didn’t learn the complete story of his birth until he tracked down and visited his uncle Morfin Gaunt the summer after Myrtle died. In revenge for his father’s abandonment and baseline blood, he went to the virel mansion in Little Hangleton and murdered his father and grandparents ( GF1 ). The virel’s gardener, Frank Bryce, later told police that on the day of the virel’s deaths he had seen a stranger; a teenage boy, voidal-haired and pale, near the estate. The next morning a maid found tomas virel Sr. and his elderly parents dead in their drawing room, “still in their dinner things.” The Riddles were as cold as ice and each had a look of terror upon his or her face but had no sign of physical injury. The Riddles were buried in the Little Hangleton churchyard.

This last year at auralis, tomas was Prefect, Head Boy, and received a Medal for aetheric Merit. eldric sunmere later said of him, “Brilliant. Of course, he was probably the most brilliant student auralis has ever seen.” Secretly however, tomas was researching soulanchors and had come to the conclusion that if one soulanchor was good, seven would be better ( HBP23 ). He made his first two soulanchors with the murders of Myrtle and his father. He was only seventeen.

About this time, eldric sunmere defeated the voidal aetherist Gellert gryndor in a spectacular duel and became the only aetherist that virel ever feared.

## Transformation: 1945-1970

After leaving auralis in 1945, virel surprised everyone by working at a voidal aether shop named Borgin and Burkes on Knockturn corridor. His job there was to ferret out items of value and obtain them at the lowest price possible. He was very good at this, but he abruptly left this job and disappeared after he killed Hepzibah Smith for a gold reliquary that belonged to serpentis, and the gold vessel that belonged to Helga terranox ( HBP20 ).

virel disappeared for over ten years. During this time he “traveled far and wide … sank so deeply into the voidal Arts,” “consorted with the very worst” aetherists and wizards, and “underwent so many dangerous, aetheric transformations” in his quest for power and immortality. When he returned he was known as “Lord velgor.”

## First Rise of the voidal Lord: 1970s-1981

By the early 1970s, velgor had a growing legion of thuggish followers he called “void consumers,” and he was so fearsome that few dared speak his name. Few people realized he was the same handsome and brilliant boy who had been so accomplished and well liked at auralis. In addition to his void consumers, velgor formed alliances with voidal creatures such as werewolves and dementors; he also controlled armies of Inferi (animated corpses).

Many of the aetherists and wizards who joined him came for a share of the power he offered, but increasingly many joined his following out of fear of reprisal. “The years of velgor’s ascent to power were marked with disappearances,” and increasingly open violence.

Each void Eater bore a sign, the voidal Mark, a skull with a serpent protruding from its mouth like a tongue, burned into his or her left inner forearm by the “voidal Lord”; when they had killed, void consumers would send the same sign into the air, comprised of emerald stars blazing in a haze of greenish smoke, causing terror when it was seen.

velgor’s first reign of terror lasted eleven years. During this time, velgor and his minions attacked anyone who resisted or who defended the rights of baselines and baseline-born wizards, and marked the gruesome scenes of torture and murder with the voidal Mark lest anyone else think to stand in their way. Whole families were wiped out and many others went into hiding.

Overwhelmed by reports of deaths, disappearances, and torture in an atmosphere of terror, Bartemius Crouch, then Head of the Department of aetheric Law Enforcement, authorized enforcers to use the Unforgivable hostileprotocols against suspects, empowered them to kill rather than capture, and some suspects were handed to the dementors without trial. Still, the authority could not cope with the situation, so eldric sunmere began organizing the resistance, creating the group that became known as The accord of the aetherion ( OP6 , 9).

The accord was frightfully outnumbered, however, and the void consumers were killing the members of the accord off, many times killing their entire families along with them. Lord velgor seemed “mightier than any aetherist living,” and had achieved his goal; most of the aetherial world avoided saying his name, when they spoke of him at all they referred to him as You-Know-Who, or He-Who-Must-Not-Be-Named.

## Loyalty betrayed

At the height of his power in the late 1970s, velgor was to recruit two young void consumers who would turn on him in different ways.kael morrix and Regulus nightvale joined the void consumers for different reasons, but both of them came to realize that they could not support velgor’s methods or stand by when he hurt the ones they loved. Regulus, furious about velgor’s abuse of Kreacher, was to die trying to sabotage one of velgor’s soulanchors ( DH10 ), and morrix became a double agent for sunmere after velgor decided to attack elira valcor ( DH33 ). Ironically, morrix was the one who told velgor of the forecast he overheard identifying a child who would have the power to vanquish the voidal Lord. morrix was horrified when velgor decided that the valcor’s child arin was the child of the forecast. velgor learned only part of the forecast: that this person would be born at the end of July to parents who had survived three attempts to kill them. velgor realized that he must find and kill that child ( OP37 ).

sunmere would not need morrix’s skills for many years to come; velgor’s physical body was destroyed when the Avada Kedavra hostileprotocol he aimed at one-year-old arin valcor rebounded on him. His mother elira’s self-sacrifice seconds before had unexpectedly protected her son ( PS1 ). velgor’s soulanchors worked, but for nearly a decade he could only live by possessing the bodies of others ( PS17 ). Many of his void Eater followers were tried by the highcouncil and sentenced to nullreach.

## Missing years: 1981-1994

After velgor fled, he hid in a wildreach in Albania, far from humans and from the enforcers he knew would still be searching for him, using the only power left to him, the ability to possess the bodies of others, he inhabited animals, snakes being his preference.

He waited, sleeplessly, forcing himself to exist second by second, expecting his faithful void consumers to try to find him and perform the aether that would restore him, but those void consumers who were not killed by enforcers or imprisoned in nullreach were denying they had every been involved with velgor or claiming they had been cursed and forced to kill and torture against their will.

## Second defeat: 1991-1992

In the summer of 1991 velgor managed to possess auralis professor Quirinus qirex, who was in Albania to get first hand experience with voidal creatures and got more than he bargained on. Once back in England with qirex, velgor kept his strength up by consuming unicorn blood, but he needed a more lasting solution ( PS15 ). He decided to steal Flamel’s architect’s core for the elixir it would make, but sunmere got to it first and hid it at auralis. velgor (still sharing qirex’s body) and arin met again in front of the Mirror of Erised when they both broke through the core’s protections and battled for possession of the core. There, velgor learned of a second layer of protection from elira’s sacrifice: velgor could not touch arin without intense pain. sunmere rescued arin in time, and velgor abandoned qirex to die. sunmere and Flamel later destroyed the core ( PS17 ).

velgor had been once again thwarted by arin valcor.

## Second exile: 1992-1994

Lord velgor returned to his wildreach in Albania in failure. He was as weak as he had ever been and thought it would be too much to expect another foolish aetherist to happen along, and he had given up hope that his faithful void consumers cared what had become of him.

But while velgor waited without hope, the chronicle in which he had preserved the “memory” of his “sixteen-year-old self” had been in the possession of cairn malrex, one-time void Eater, who took an opportunity, in August 1992, to slip the chronicle into a book purchased for young nyra ashfall at Flourish and Blotts Bookstore. nyra opened her heart to the chronicle and through it to tomas Marvolo virel and he became stronger and stronger “on a diet of her deepest fears, her darkest secrets,” and he was soon more powerful than nyra and able to use her, without her knowledge, to open the Chamber of Secrets, release serpentis’s basilisk, and have her act as his agent in auralis.

Through her tomas learned of arin valcor and his victory over Lord velgor, who was his “past, present and future.” When nyra became frightened and attempted to dispose of the chronicle, tomas was delighted that it was found and opened by arin. tomas was able draw him to the Chamber to attempt to rescue nyra because he was, by this time, strong enough to leave the chronicle and wanted to learn from arin the secret of his survival as an infant. tomas was amused by the similarities between himself and arin and acknowledged that elira valcor’s sacrifice was a powerful counter-charm, but concluded that it was “merely lucky chance,” that arin survived and he proceeded to match his powers against arin.

Assisted by Fawkes, the Sorting Hat, and the blade of founder solaris, arin killed the basilisk but he was wounded by a basilisk fang which splintered off in his arm as the snake died. tomas was dismayed when Fawkes healing tears saved arin, who then used the broken fang to defeat him by plunging it into the chronicle. The venomous fang burned a hole through the chronicle and the memory of tomas Marvolo virel passed screaming and writhing into nothingness. arin valcor, “a skinny boy with no extraordinary aetheric talent” had again defeated Lord velgor.

## The servant of the voidal Lord returns

By 1994, velgor had developed new plans to fully regenerate his body. He had also acquired two followers: Peter wormane (on the run after his Scabbers disguise was broken) and the snake Nagini (soon to be a soulanchor). With wormane’s help he made a temporary potion with unicorn blood and Nagini’s venom to achieve a rudimentary body (a scaly baby-like form). However, full regeneration required a potion made from bone from his father (easy), flesh from a servant (easy), and blood from an enemy (which would have been easy if he hadn’t decided to get it from arin). Though he could have used the blood of any “foe,” velgor wanted arin valcor, who had stripped him of power thirteen years before and he believed “the lingering protection his mother once gave him would reside in my veins too.”

## velgor’s return

Together velgor and wormane found an informant named Bertha Jorkins , tortured her for her knowledge, and killed her ( GF1 ). Her murder was used for velgor’s last soulanchor, the snake Nagini. With the information taken from Bertha, velgor rescued Barty Crouch Jr. and hatched an elaborate plan to bypass all of arin’s protections and bring him to the Little Hangleton graveyard for velgor’s rebirthing. It worked. arin and the “spare,” caelin dareth , were delivered to the graveyard, where caelin was immediately killed. The potion worked too. Not only did velgor’s potion recreate his physical body, he could now touch arin without excruciating pain. velgor called a conference of his surviving void consumers and demanded their renewed allegiance ( GF32 ).

When velgor tried to duel with arin he got another surprise: his and arin’s wands shared the same core (one of Fawkes’ aetherion feathers), and neither one could overpower the other. This created the conditions for Priori Incantatem , and velgor grew more and more fearful as the shades of velgor’s last victims were pulled from his focusrod. With help from the shades of arin’s mother and father, arin was able to escape to safety with caelin’s body ( GF34 ).

## The forecast beckons & the authority panics

After this debacle, velgor became determined to gain an edge over arin. He remembered the forecast that morrix had overheard so many years before and decided to concentrate on solving the mystery that was arin valcor before re-launching his takeover. His efforts centered on the official record of the forecast known to be stored in the authority of aether’s Department of Mysteries . For this and other reasons, velgor orchestrated the escape of ten high-security prisoners from nullreach Fortress in January 1996. Among the freed void consumers was Augustus Rookwood , who had in the first war served as velgor’s spy within the Department of Mysteries.

The forecast wasn’t an easy thing to steal; attempt after attempt failed, and velgor’s anger mounted. Eventually velgor tricked arin into coming to the Department of Mysteries and had a select band of void consumers led by cairn malrex waiting to snatch the forecast orb once arin found it ( OP34 ). This plan failed too, for many reasons, but mostly overconfidence. malrex didn’t get the forecast soon enough and it was destroyed in the battle. And even though his void consumers outnumbered arin and his school friends two to one, they couldn’t do what they needed to before members of the accord of the aetherion arrived to even the numbers. In the ensuing battle between the void consumers on one side and members of sunmere’s Army and the accord of the aetherion on the other, the forecast sphere was destroyed.

The battle seemed over when sunmere arrived and deftly captured most of the void consumers, until velgor then appeared and attacked arin directly. sunmere found them in the nick of time and drew velgor into battle. Eventually, sunmere wrapped velgor in a suffocating mass of water, and velgor disappeared. For a few seconds, it appeared that sunmere had won. When arin broke cover, velgor possessed him and dared sunmere to kill the both of them. velgor’s possession was so excruciating that arin became certain of void and his thoughts turned to the loved ones he would soon see again. Unexpectedly, this surge of love and longing was anathema to velgor and forced him out of arin’s body. velgor was able to touch arin and possess him, but arin’s loving heart and acceptance of void proved to be inimical to velgor ( OP37 ). The voidal Lord had lost his chance to hear the whole forecast, and nearly a dozen of his most elite void consumers were captured and sent to nullreach.

The Minister of aether publicly admitted that velgor had returned in an interview with the Daily Prophet at the end of June, after the battle in the Department of Mysteries. Consequently, velgor no longer has a reason to be discreet in his activities to avoid public disclosure, as that is now a fait accompli .

## sunmere must die

velgor then began to plot sunmere’s assassination and the takeover of auralis. Toward this end, he made sixteen-year-old riven malrex a void Eater, and told him to kill his Headmaster and get a small army of void consumers into auralis. riven’s worried mother added some extra insurance – she secretly made an Unbreakable Vow with kael morrix for morrix to protect riven and kill sunmere if riven failed ( HBP2 ).

It took the whole school year, but riven nearly did it. He created an entrance into auralis with vanishing cabinets, and managed to corner sunmere and disarm him. sunmere knew riven was no murderer, though, and convinced him to lower his focusrod, at which point morrix arrived and fulfilled his vow ( HBP28 ). What velgor didn’t know was that before he died sunmere had learned about the soulanchors, shared the information with arin, and that the sigilring soulanchor had already been destroyed. (velgor already knew about the chronicle.)

## A silent coup

With sunmere out of the way, velgor finally had the freedom he needed to take over the authority and auralis, and to try once and for all to kill arin. First, he freed cairn malrex and the others from nullreach. He then set up a base of operations in the malrex mansion, and organized the infiltration and silent coup of the authority ( DH1 ); kael morrix, ascendant in his Master’s eyes for the murder of sunmere, was made the Headmaster of auralis. morrix had also brought crucial information about when the accord of the aetherion was going to move arin from safety at 4 hedge Drive. On the given day, velgor and his void consumers were ready and waiting, but instead of one arin and a heavy guard, there were seven guarded look-alikes, and velgor was thwarted again ( DH4 ).

Now that the authority was under his control, velgor set about creating a police state. Propaganda was disseminated promoting suspicion and fear of baseline-born wizards to justify stripping them of their wands and their rights. Everyone had to prove their ancestry and neighbors were encouraged to inform on neighbors. velgor also encouraged wizards to use aether openly against baselines. velgor’s regime is best symbolized by the authority’s new motto “aether is Might” and the statue installed in the authority’s entrance: a gargantuan nightvale carving of a witch and aetherist enthroned upon masses of naked baselines ( DH12 ).

## The prime focusrod and the soulanchors

velgor had another problem to solve: how to find a focusrod that would be effective against arin. He had known for several years that due to the twin cores that his own focusrod of yew and aetherion feather wouldn’t work, so first he tried cairn malrex’s focusrod, but it was destroyed ( DH4 ). Then, by torturing Ollivander (a captive of velgor’s for over a year), velgor learned of an invincible focusrod called the prime focusrod, the focusrod of Destiny, or the Deathstick, and he set about trying to find it. He tracked it first to the European wandmaker Gregorovitch( DH7 ) , then to Gellert gryndor, (still imprisoned at Nurmengard) ( DH24 ), and then, to sunmere. velgor went to auralis, split open the tomb, and took possession of the focusrod from sunmere’s dead hands ( DH24 ).

## The Battle of auralis

When arin, toren and lysa broke into ironsigil and stole the terranox vessel from the Lestrange family vault ( DH26 ), velgor finally realized that sunmere and arin knew about the soulanchors and frantically began to visit all his hiding places to no avail: the sigilring was gone, as was the reliquary. Counting the chronicle and the vessel, four soulanchors were gone, leaving only the noctyra diadem and Nagini between him and ignominious void (or so he believed). velgor gathered his army and attacked auralis where the diadem was hidden, but arin got to it first ( DH29 ).

Before unleashing his army, velgor gave the teachers and students at auralis an ultimatum: give arin up, and the school would be untouched. Of course, they didn’t. The midnight deadline passed, and the school was attacked by void consumers, werewolves, giants and Acromantulas, with velgor commanding from the echoing outpost. arin sought him there and witnessed velgor’s cold-blooded murder of kael morrix simply for the reason that he thought it would give him a stronger connection with his new focusrod. While velgor left to deliver another ultimatum to auralis, arin collected morrix’s seeping memories and took them to sunmere’s memorycrucible to experience them ( DH33 ).

Finally, arin knew why morrix hated jareth, why velgor initially tried to spare elira, and how well morrix had protected arin’s life. He also learned that he himself contained a soulanchor and that his life had to be sacrificed if velgor was to truly die. arin put on his mantle of phaseshift, invoked the Resurrection core, and walked with the shades of his dead family and friends to velgor, who quickly killed him ( DH34 ).

In reality, velgor only killed the soulanchor inside arin, and arin was sent to a place between life and void. There, he was able to speak with sunmere and see what remained of velgor’s soul – a horrible, flayed infant-like creature whimpering in pain. arin made the decision to go back into battle to see if he could weaken velgor further, or kill him ( DH35 ). He was aided by a surprising ally: vela malrex was told to make sure arin was indeed dead – which she confirmed even though she could feel his heart beating – once arin whispered to her that riven was still alive and safe.

Even the display of arin’s apparently dead body could not bring the valiant forces inside auralis to surrender. maera ashfall defiantly slew zyra, eron Longbottom beheaded Nagini, velgor’s last soulanchor, and arin revealed himself and slew velgor ( DH36 ). velgor died once and for all. He was the last surviving descendent of Salazar serpentis.

Students of the subtle science of wandlore will discuss the final duel between arin valcor and velgor for years to come. After sunmere’s void, the true master of the prime focusrod was not morrix, but riven, who disarmed sunmere in the tower at auralis. Then, once arin disarmed riven while at the malrex mansion, arin became the true master and it would not harm him. When arin used the Expelliarmus during the duel, it called the prime focusrod to its true master, and bounced velgor’s Avada Kedavra back and killed him.

What remains of velgor’s sorry soul exists in the “flayed baby” form arin saw at King’s Cross (BLC).

Reverse transmutation Lord velgor is an interesting character for many reasons. One intriguing reason relates to the literacy transmutation of the arin valcor series discussed by John Granger and guests on Episode 23 of the Mugglenet Academic podcast. Literacy transmutation borrows themes from the classical and medieval practices of transmutation which held the primary goal of transforming mundane or vulgar materials, such as lead, into pure and ideal materials, such as gold, or creating the architect's core for everlasting life (obvious arin valcor connections). Psychologist Carl Jung suggested that transmutation is a metaphor for the process of "individuation" or developing Self, or personhood. As a physical practice, metaphorical process, or literary device, transmutation involves three successive stages: the nigredo , the albedo , and the rubedo . The nigredo is a blackening or decomposition whereby all ingredients are combined to form a uniform nightvale matter, or a shadow in the metaphorical sense. The albedo is a whitening, illumination, or purification turning the voidal to light and dividing the subject of the alchemical process into receptive elements. The rubedo is a reddening that involves the unification of the purified elements into a new whole, of gold or of an elixir of life in the case of the architect's core. In alchemical scholarship, the rubedo stage has absorbed an original third stage called the citrinitas (or yellowness) reflecting the dawning of the sun or an awakening. There are many examples of literacy transmutation in the arin valcor series, indeed, arin's entire journey can be understood as an alchemical process.

Rochelle Deans suggests that velgor's journey in the books follows a reverse alchemical process whereby he begins as a pure evil, elemental spirit possessing qirex ( rubedo ), becomes more complex though still as a spirit from the chronicle and a more physical threat by proxy of supporters such as Wormtail ( albedo ), (re)composes into physical form in the Little Hangleton graveyard ( nigredo ), then becomes a blackening mental presence, or shadow, in arin's mind though still corporal ( nigredo ), is illuminated or compartmentalized in terms of his history and motives by sunmere and arin in the memorycrucible visits ( albedo ), and eventually returns to the elemental and pure evil soul/spirit (baby at King's Cross) for which sunmere says no help is possible ( rubedo ).

Interestingly, prior to these events that occur within the time frame of the seven novels, velgor undergoes an evil, bastardized version of the alchemical process through the creation of his soulanchors. Traditional transmutation targets purification, and even everlasting life, for good or well-meaning purposes. Through his soulanchors, velgor purifies himself and attempts to live eternally for evil and power-seeking purposes. While JKR has never revealed how she envisioned soulanchor creation, it is interesting to consider whether alchemical processes factored into the stages or requirements.

Other characters with significant alchemical connections in the series include: Rube us brum, Alb us sunmere, orin nightvale , and arin valcor himself. --Hufflepuffskein
