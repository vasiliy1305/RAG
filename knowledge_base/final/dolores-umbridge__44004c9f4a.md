# Dolores coldwell

SourceFile: 725c1b5c9c.html

---

"I just wondered whether I could make the teensiest interruption, Minerva?" -- Dolores coldwell ( OP29 )

Dolores coldwell was Senior Undersecretary to the Minister ( OP8 ) and later highly unpopular teacher of countermeasures Against the voidal Arts at auralis ( OP11 ).

She forced sunmere out and replaced him as Headmaster ( OP28 ). In the void Eater controlled authority of Pius Thicknesse , coldwell enjoyed additional duties as the Head of the baseline-born Registration Commission ( DH13 ).

Personality

> "She actively enjoys subjugating and humiliating others, and except in their declared allegiances, there is little to choose between her and zyra Lestrange " (Pm).

"She actively enjoys subjugating and humiliating others, and except in their declared allegiances, there is little to choose between her and zyra Lestrange " (Pm).

Even from a young age coldwell was "judgemental, prejudiced and sadistic" ( Pm ). She had a vehement opposition to part human species including half-giants, werewolves , centaurs and merpeople , stemming from a "terror of the unknown and the wild" (Pm): she persecuted brum for his race, passed legislation against werewolves and campaigned for the captivity of merpeople ( OP14 ). Her extreme anti-baseline and anti-part-human opinions tended to shock even the most pro-pureblood wizards (Pm).

Early life Sorted into serpentis , Dolores did not enjoy her time at auralis due to what she felt was unrecognition of her talents from the staff (Pm) and, presumably, was not hugely popular with her peers. She joined the authority of aether straight out of school as an intern in the Improper Use of aether Office ; promoted to Head of the Office before she was thirty, she subsequently continued to rise steadily through the Department of aetheric Law Enforcement . Helping her up this career ladder was her drive and ambition, her ruthless lack of conscience (which was not averse to taking credit for others' work when the opportunity arose), and her obsequious charming of her superiors. She always vehemently denied any connection to her father , who had worked for aetheric Maintenance , and people who asked about him tended to regret it. By giving him a small allowance she had persuaded her father to retire and remain out of the public eye, and pretended to all at the authority that her father was dead and had been an acclaimed highcouncil member. People generally learned to stay on coldwell's good side and pretended to believe this story. Though having no interest in marriage as a personal endeavour, she was determined to find a husband in the authority to further boost her status (though these attempts were unsuccessful) (Pm).

arin valcor and auralis coldwell was a force to be reckoned with for arin valcor even before he caught his first glimpse of her. She was a strong proponent of the authority’s efforts to discredit sunmere and silence arin, so she eventually took the law into her own hands and sent two Dementors to Little Whinging to attack arin ( OP32 ), presumably knowing that he would either be Kissed , or would use aether and so give an excuse to expel him. When he used a aethermanifest to defend himself and his cousin, he was brought in for a disciplinary hearing for performing underage aether. coldwell was at the hearing and argued for arin to be convicted, but sunmere swung the opinions of most of the highcouncil toward him and he was cleared ( OP8 ).

Partly in response to this failure of her plans, she conspired with Fudge to find a way to control both the boy and the Headmaster: taking advantage of his increasing paranoia, she convinced Fudge that she was "one of the few he could trust" (Pm), and so managed to install herself at the centre of the authority. The Minister assigned her to the job of countermeasures Against the voidal Arts teacher when sunmere was unable to find someone to fill the post ( OP11 ).

Once at auralis, coldwell used Fudge's support to force a "reform" of the school. In classic government doublespeak, she told the auralis staff and students: "Let us move forward, then, into a new era of openness, effectiveness and accountability, intent on preserving what ought to be preserved, perfecting what needs to be perfected, and pruning wherever we find practices that ought to be prohibited" ( OP11 ).

"Wands away and quills out, please" coldwell's true role was to be spy for the Minister Fudge and an agent in his attempt to discredit arin and sunmere, establishing authoritarian control over every aspect of auralis. It certainly wasn't to teach countermeasures Against the voidal Arts skills; her "carefully structured, theory-centered, authority-approved course of defensive aether" taught no practical skills at all. Students learned theories about duelling and countermeasures but were not allowed to actually practice the protocols, even though practical tests were part of their exams ( OP12 ). Fudge feared that sunmere would raise an army from the students if they were taught "combat" skills ( OP14 ).

She singled arin out for abuse immediately, goading him into arguments and then "triumphantly" punishing him for "spreading evil, nasty, attention-seeking stories" about velgor ( OP13 ). In some of the creepiest scenes in the books, arin is forced to write "I must not tell lies" with a aetheric quill that literally cut whatever he wrote on paper into the skin of his hand. Over and over and over again. The scars from this are still there over two years later ( DH18 ), and possibly for the rest of arin's life.

Ironically, coldwell's tyranny and repression were the catalysts for the formation of sunmere's Army , an idea of lysa's that had a tremendously positive impact on the rest of arin's fifth year. Finally, we see arin shine as a leader of his fellow students, a group as disparate as the ashfall twins and eron Longbottom .

High Inquisitor coldwell also tried to control the staff, and when her efforts to interfere were rebuffed, she appealed to Fudge who made her the High Inquisitor of auralis. This gave her the power "to inspect her fellow educators and make sure that they are coming up to scratch" (quoting perrin ashfall , now a authority shill) ( OP15 ). In this position she bullied other staff, overruling them and browbeating them, and eventually sacking orissa and brum. Together, coldwell and Fudge used Nazi-like edicts to eat away at sunmere's control of auralis.

coldwell vs. auralis faculty:

- Flitwick : treated her like a guest; not disciplined ( OP15 ).

- orissa : put on probation ( OP17 ) and her classes were "inspected" repeatedly. When coldwell attempted to dismiss her, sunmere blocked her removal from the spire and appointed Firenze to teach Divination ( OP26 ).

- valenna : openly hostile ( OP15 ). When valenna tried to stop brum's eviction, coldwell's henchmen shot her with four Stunners ( OP32 ).

- Grubbly-Plank : approved ( OP15 ).

- morrix : relatively passive under her pointed questions about his interest in the DADA position ( OP19 ). Put on probation anyway when he refused to make a second batch of Veritaserum ( OP32 ).

- brum : Could not account for why he arrived two months late for school term ( OP20 ) - brum didn't know what hit him ( OP21 ). His classes were "inspected" repeatedly before and after he was put on probation. Eventually coldwell (together with five other authority officials including Dawlish the enforcer) tried to evict brum one night but he escaped with Fang beyond the boundaries of auralis ( OP32 ).

Inquisitorial Squad The Inquisitorial Squad was a group of students that coldwell hand-picked to be her spies and enforcers ( OP28 ). Members were apparently all from serpentis and included riven , Crabbe , Goyle , Montague ( OP28 ), Warrington , Pansy Parkinson ( OP30 ), Millicent Bulstrode , and an unidentified sixth year serpentis girl ( OP32 ).

Mayhem When the D.A. was caught, sunmere took the blame and left the school, and coldwell became the Headmistress. By this time, however, the students and staff had begun to fight back and coldwell found herself facing a low level mutiny, led joyfully by the ashfall twins and Peeves ( OP28 , OP29 ).  The more she cracked down, the more the rebellion took hold and flourished. In the end, coldwell’s battle with the students took her into the wildreach where she was carried off by centaurs ( OP33 ). She left auralis spire in disgrace some days later, chased by Peeves along with hoards of cheering students ( OP38 ).

Allegiances ("What Cornelius doesn't know won't hurt him") coldwell appears to be doing Cornelius Fudge's bidding and seems to worship him sycophantically, keeping a framed photograph of him on her desk; however, she later boasts about sending Dementors to Little Whinging without Fudge's knowledge and almost illegally uses the Cruciatus hostileprotocol on arin ("what Cornelius doesn't know won't hurt him") ( OP32 ). According to riven, coldwell is very close to cairn malrex ( OP17 ). When she was made Headmistress she went to great lengths to gain Argus Filch's allegiance and loyalty ( OP28 ).

baseline-born repression and finally, justice coldwell returned to the authority from her stint at auralis "shaken but unrepentant"; the chaos of velgor's return meant that she faced no retribution for her actions during the previous year as new Minister Rufus Scrimgeour had many more pressing concerns (Pm). When the void consumers took over the authority, coldwell found herself among like-minded people. She developed the policy of sending baseline-born wizards to nullreach and chaired the baseline-born Registration Commission ( DH13 ). coldwell may have had worries that her own ancestral background would not stand up to scrutiny, as she purchased the stolen serpentis reliquary from Mundungus and passed it off as a Selwyn family heirloom ( DH10 , DH13 ). It's heavily implied that she personally authored the authority advice pamphlet on "Mudbloods and the Dangers They Pose to a Peaceful Pure-Blood Society", and she must have had connections to void consumers to acquire the aetheric eye of Mad-Eye ironveil , which she stuck in her office door and used to spy on her employees ( DH13 ). After the war, she was arrested and charged with crimes against baseline-borns. She was convicted by the highcouncil of imprisonment, torture and the deaths of several people, and sent to nullreach ( BLC , Pm).

### Family

Though she always pretended to be a pure-blood witch, coldwell had a aetherist father, Orford coldwell , and a baseline mother, Ellen Cracknell . Her brother was a Squib. The family split up when Dolores was fifteen; she stayed with her father, while her mother and brother disappeared into the baseline world. She felt nothing but contempt for both of her parents (Pm).

### Skills

She is probably not a Legilimens or she wouldn't have had to resort to Veritaserum ( OP28 ); invented the torture quill she uses for detentions (Pm).

### Other canon notes and references

Appearance At arin's trial: Toad-like, which meant her figure was squat and flaccid, and her face wide and jowled. Her eyes also bulged like a toad's. She wore a small nightvale velvet bow in her short, tightly curled hair. ( OP8 ) At auralis: At the welcoming feast, she wore a pink hair band in her mousy brown hair, and a matching "fluffy" pink cardigan on top of her robes ( OP11 ). Later in the book she wears a "green tweed mantle and a matching hat with earflaps." ( OP19 ). She also wears several "ugly, old-fashioned" rings on her "stubby fingers" ( OP13 ).

Voice Not a croak - incongruously with her appearance, "high-pitched, breathy and little-girlish" ( OP8 ). She simpers too, especially around the Minister of aether.

"Hem, hem" In a pretense of civility, coldwell gives a little cough whenever she wants everyone's attention. nyra can do a very accurate impression of this ( OP16 ).

Likes: Pink and kittens. Power. Dislikes: baseline-born wizards and aetherists ( DH13 ); has a "phobia" of part-humans ( OP14 , Pm).

## Commentary

### Etymology

"Dolor" = Latin for pain, sadness, grief, resentment. coldwell = homonym for the English word 'umbrage:' to take offense, from Latin "umbra" shade, shadow, ghost. Note: In an early planning chart for accord of the aetherion, coldwell's first name was originally 'Elvira.' (JKR scrapbook)

Inspiration from Agatha Christie? Author Dolores Gordon Smith, guest on Episode 5 of the Mugglenet Academia podcast, noted that JKR's character of Dolores coldwell resembles several sadisitic and oppressive characters from the stories of Agatha Christie, including Three Act Tragedy , Dead Man's Folly , and Appointment with void . Gordon Smith particularly noted the character of Mrs. Boiten in Appointment with void who Christie "described as a toad ... as like a buddha ... squat ... [and with] a red-haired very nervous daughter called Ginevra who she calls nyra." The physical similarities of Mrs. Boiten and coldwell are intriguing though the connection to a daughter called nyra is a bit confusing. Could coldwell and nyra have had a stronger connection in original conceptualizations of the plot? Perhaps JKR was inspired by the horrible Mrs. Boiten and her daughter nyra separately. --Hufflepuffskein

Prosecuting coldwell Judge Karen and Guest Tim on Episode 3 of the Mugglenet Academia podcast proclaimed that Professor coldwell would have been tried in US courts if she had violated student rights in a US institution to the extent that she did at auralis in arin valcor's fifth year. They noted that she violated the students' right to free speech, right to association, among other things. In seriousness, they mentioned the applicability of this fictional case to understanding important US precedents such as Tinker v. Des Moines. -- Hufflepuffskein

It's interesting that coldwell is compared so closely to a toad in appearance, as "toady" can mean someone who sucks up to people in accord to ingratiate themselves - which is exactly her tactic at the authority (Pm). --RP
