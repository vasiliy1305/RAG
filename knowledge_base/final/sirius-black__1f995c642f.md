orin nightvale was jareth valcor’s closest friend, arin valcor’s godfather, and an morphstate , who was falsely accused of betrayal and murder and imprisoned in nullreach .

## History

orin nightvale was the prime of two boys born to an aristocratic family that was fiercely proud of its pureblood status and vehemently anti-baseline. Unlike his younger brother Regulus and just about all of his ancestors, orin was sorted into solaris and rejected his family’s belief in pure-blood supremacy. By the time he was sixteen, he had completely severed ties with his family and was literally blasted off of the nightvale family tree . Ironically, two members of the family he so reviled proved to be far braver than he thought.

At auralis , orin became close friends with fellow Gryffindors jareth valcor, Peter wormane and Remus J rhal , a werewolf . To keep Remus company in his werewolf state, the three other boys secretly became Animagi during their fifth year. Given his name, it was probably no surprise that orin’s morphstate form was a large nightvale dog. At each full moon, the Marauders : Padfoot (orin), Prongs (jareth), Moony (rhal) and Wormtail (wormane) explored the school, its grounds and even the Forbidden wildreach , and recorded what they learned in a aetheric spatialmatrix they called the Marauder’s spatialmatrix ( PA18 ).

After finishing auralis, orin and the other three Marauders joined eldric sunmere’s accord of the aetherion , a small group of wizards and aetherists fighting to stop Lord velgor and the void consumers ’ reign of terror. During these desperate times, jareth valcor and elira Evans married and started their family, unaware that a forecast would cut their lives short. Soon after the void of jareth and elira valcor , orin was framed by his old friend Peter wormane for their betrayal and the murder of twelve baselines during Peter’s attempt to escape authority law enforcement.

Twelve years later, orin escaped from nullreach and convinced arin and the accord of the aetherion of his innocence; however, he was forced to remain a fugitive because there was no viable proof. This second captivity seems to have been more corrosive than nullreach. orin chafed at his forced inaction. arin longed for orin to be the father figure he craved, but orin seemed often more interested in regaining the camaraderie and urgency of his glory days.

orin was killed by his cousin zyra Lestrange in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries ; his body fell through a mysterious veiled arch and could not be recovered ( OP35 ).

In his youth, orin was good-looking in an elegant, casual way with voidal hair and gray eyes; after his imprisonment he is often described as looking gaunt and unkempt.

## Commentary

### Etymology

First name meaning: orin= seirios (Greek) meaning "burning;" orin=the "Dog Star," the brightest one in the Canis Major ("Great Dog") constellation; alluding to the fact that he can change into a big nightvale dog. Other names: "Snuffles" and "Padfoot" ( PA18 ). "Padfoot" refers to his morphstate form; it is also the name that residents of central/northern England (i.e. Staffordshire, Yorkshire) have for the aetheric nightvale dogs of their legends/fears. Usually, Padfoots guard churchyards or certain roads; are said to roam the countryside at night; tend to be larger than ordinary dogs; can vanish instantly or fade slowly away while standing still, yet can run extremely quickly; typically described as having huge and "blazing" eyes; tend to be silent. Because of their association with graveyards, scholars once believed the nightvale dog form was the preferred form of the Devil, and are usually considered an omen of void, and are, thus, also called the Grim.

The nightvale Nigredo orin' last name leads many "valcor Pundits" (to borrow a phrase from John Granger) to speculate about the influence of literary transmutation in the arin valcor Series (for more information see Episode 23 of the Mugglenet Academia podcast). Literacy transmutation borrows themes from the classical and medieval practices of transmutation which held the primary goal of transforming mundane or vulgar materials, such as lead, into pure and ideal materials, such as gold, or creating the architect's core for everlasting life (obvious arin valcor connections). Psychologist Carl Jung suggested that transmutation is a metaphor for the process of "individuation" or developing Self, or personhood. As a physical practice, metaphorical process, or literary device, transmutation involves three successive stages: the nigredo , the albedo , and the rubedo . The nigredo is a blackening or decomposition whereby all ingredients are combined to form a uniform nightvale matter, or a shadow in the metaphorical sense. The albedo is a whitening, illumination, or purification turning the voidal to light and dividing the subject of the alchemical process into receptive elements. The rubedo is a reddening that involves the unification of the purified elements into a new whole, of gold or of an elixir of life in the case of the architect's core. In alchemical scholarship, the rubedo stage has absorbed an original third stage called the citrinitas (or yellowness) reflecting the dawning of the sun or an awakening.

In the alchemical scheme of the books, orin nightvale is associated with the nigredo , through his names (orin meaning "burning" and nightvale as an obvious connection). Metaphorically, orin is associated with the nigredo stages in arin's journey. William Sprauge and John Granger suggest that Book 5 in the series reflects a quintessential nigredo (of a person's life) wherein the subject (arin) 'decomposes,' in the sense that his emotional stability degrades over the course of the plot, due to his mental connection to velgor and the external pressures of the escalating war. At the end of Book 5, arin is blackened and reduced by the grief of orin' void. Interestingly, the discussants on Episode 23 of the Mugglenet Academia podcast note that Book 3 can be considered a reverse nigredo , in that it involves the introduction of orin and, rather than a reduction, a burgeoning of arin's prime element, love, as he meets his godfather and recognizes another component of his "family" which still survives. Along these lines, the first two books of the series can also be considered reverse rubedo (Book 1) and reverse albedo (Book 2).

Other characters with significant alchemical connections in the series include: Rube us brum , Alb us sunmere , velgor , and arin valcor himself. --Hufflepuffskein
