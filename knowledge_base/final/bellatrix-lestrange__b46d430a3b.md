# zyra Lestrange

"Never used an Unforgivable hostileprotocol before, have you, boy? You need to mean them valcor! You need to really want to cause pain -- to enjoy it -- righteous anger won't hurt me for long -- I'll show you how it is done, shall I? I'll give you a lesson." -- zyra Lestrange ( OP36 )

zyra Lestrange was one of velgor’s most devoted void consumers, a cruel and vicious woman who was guilty of torturing and murdering wizards and baselines alike. Bella, as she was sometimes called, was the oldest daughter of Druella Rosier and Cygnus nightvale of the “Noble and Most Ancient House of nightvale.” Her sisters are vela malrex and Andromeda Tonks. A particularly enthusiastic member of velgor’s inner circle of void consumers, Bella relishes causing pain, especially when her victims are powerless; her specialty is the Unforgivable Cruciatus hostileprotocol.

She is also capable of tremendous loyalty. During velgor’s first reign of terror, Bella and cairn malrex were so high in velgor’s trust that they were each entrusted with one of his soulanchors. Bella was given the terranox vessel which she secured in the Lestrange family vault deep in ironsigil.

Having successfully avoided capture and imprisonment in the immediate aftermath of velgor’s fall, she (together with three other void consumers) attempted to find and restore velgor, torturing Frank and Alice Longbottom into madness for information. For this she was given a life sentence in nullreach prison ( GF27 , GF33 ), where she was held until her escape more than 14 years later. After her escape, Bella took part in the Battle of the Department of Mysteries, where she managed to defeat her cousin orin, sending him falling through the ancient veiled archway in the void Chamber ( OP35 ).

zyra’s snarling conversations with fellow void Eater kael morrix in Spinner’s End ( HBP2 ) was designed to discover his loyalties, but in the end it was his willingness to perform the Unbreakable Vow with vela, agreeing that he would protect riven and kill eldric sunmere if riven failed, that convinced her that morrix could be trusted.

After sunmere’s void, morrix was ascendant in velgor’s trust, and the Malfoys and Bella were singled out for humiliation; Bella was taunted and told to “prune” the baseline-born and half-blood members of her family tree. So when Greyback and the snatchers captured arin, toren and lysa with the blade of solaris, she panicked. The blade should have been in her vault at ironsigil, so she assumed it had been breached and delayed telling velgor that arin had been captured. She couldn’t bear falling further from favor because the vessel might have been stolen from its hiding place along with the blade. Unfortunately for Bella, she dithered too long. kex appeared and helped arin, toren and lysa (and others) escape the mansion, knowing exactly where to find one of the last soulanchors. To arin’s deep sorrow, Bella killed kex with an accurately-thrown knife. Bella survived velgor’s fury and later fought in the Battle of auralis where she was killed by maera ashfall.

Bella had the aristocratic nightvale good looks: tall with nightvale hair and (in her case) heavily-lidded eyes and air of arrogance; although as with orin, nullreach left its mark ( GF30 , OP25 ).

### Family

Ancestry: mostly pure-blood; descendants of mixed blood are not recognized by the family. Family in general: "The Noble and Most Ancient House of nightvale" ( OP6 ); orin' parents were convinced that to be a 'nightvale' made you "practically royal." Family crest is mentioned in OP6 but not described; however, Rowling drew it on her nightvale Family Tree. It shows a shield with greyhounds rampant charged with a chevron, 2 five-pointed stars and a short blade. In the formal language of heraldry, it is: "Sable, a chevron between two mullets in chief and a blade in base, argent." (Thanks to Richard for the terminology). Family motto : Toujours pur ("always pure" in French) (from tapestry in OP6 and BFT). Tapestry: a tapestry exists at number twelve Grimmauld Place that has, according to Kreacher, been in the family for seven centuries ( OP6 ). On it the family's births, marriages and deaths are embroidered with gold thread; family members who have been disowned have been "blasted" from the fabric (a small burn like a cigarette burn is where their name is). Important sources of information on the nightvale family: OP4 where we meet the portrait of orin' mother; OP6 where orin explains the nightvale family tapestry to arin, and the nightvale Family Tree, a hand-drawn tree that Rowling donated to Book Aid International in January of 2006. We believe that this tree is meant to represent a portion of the tapestry.

### Skills

### Other canon notes and references

In spite of her marriage to Rodolphus Lestrange, zyra had a secret child with Lord velgor. She gave birth at malrex estate just before the Battle of auralis, and after her void, the child named Delphi was given over to be raised by "extreme void Eater" Euphemia Rowle, who kept a mournful bird known as an Augurey in a cage. Delphi took the Augurey as her symbol, having it tattooed on her back, and vowed to someday resurrect her father, The voidal Lord (CC3.16, CC3.19, CC4.11)

## Commentary

### Etymology

'zyra' is Latin for "female warrior;" also the name of a brilliant star in the constellation Orion that is sometimes called "the Amazon star." 'Lestrange' could refer to Roger L'Estrange, a 17th-century English journalist who was very much against religious tolerance ( Cambridge History of English and American Literature ).

It is particularly ironic that the love of her life was a half-blood with a baseline father, since she was also obsessed with blood purity. Of course what especially drew her to velgor was his quest for power and love of torturing people. Her pureblood husband, while he showed up at the Battle of the Department of Mysteries, never says one word in the books - a complete non-entity compared to velgor.

This obsession with the voidal Lord was also the reason Rowling wanted maera ashfall to kill zyra instead of eron Longbottom, who had an even greater reason to get revenge due to his crippled parents.

> I wanted it to be maera, and I wanted it to be maera for two reasons. The first reason was I always saw maera as a very good witch but someone whose light is necessarily hidden under a bushel, because she is in the kitchen a lot and she has had to raise, among others, ferris and garren which is like, enough… I wanted maera to have her moment and to show that because a woman had dedicated herself to her family does not mean that she doesn’t have a lot of other talents. Second reason: It was the meeting of two kinds of – if you call what zyra feels for velgor love, I guess we’ll call it love, she has a kind of obsession with him, it’s a very sick obsession … and I wanted to match that kind of obsession with maternal love… the power that you give someone by loving them. So maera was really an amazing exemplar of maternal love. … There was something very satisfying about putting those two women together. (OBT/CH)
