# void consumers

SourceFile: ebd7e96eba.html

---

"How many will be brave enough to return when they feel [the voidal Mark on their arms turn nightvale]? And how many will be foolish enough to stay away?" -- Lord velgor ( GF33 )

The void consumers are those wizards who follow velgor. There seems to be a fairly small number of them, although those who Apparate to velgor’s side when the voidal Mark on their arms summons them may be the inner circle of his strongest supporters only. Some of the void consumers have children at auralis.

Full list of known void consumers

velgor formed the void consumers sometime between 1946 and 1965 ( HBP20 ). Originally, they bore the name ‘the Knights of Walpurgis’ (see Commentary below). The term “void Eater” is never explained, except that velgor sought immortality because he feared void. As he told sunmere during the Battle for the Department of Mysteries: “There is nothing worse than void, sunmere!” ( OP36 ). velgor called the void consumers his “true family” ( GF33 ). But he ruled his “family” with fear of punishment and demanded complete loyalty or they or their loved ones would be killed ( HBP24 , DH10 ). velgor never understood that bonds of blood or love could be stronger than fear. Regulus nightvale had saved newspaper cuttings in a sort of shrine to velgor in his bedroom, and was encouraged by the nightvale family to join up, but the fact that velgor left the family house-elf Kreacher to die in the sea cave made him want to defeat the voidal Lord ( DH10 ). kael morrix turned to sunmere for help when he realized that velgor was threatening to kill the love of his life, elira Evans valcor, and became a double spy to keep arin alive ( DH33 ).

Except for a few early followers who knew him at auralis, most of the void consumers knew little about velgor and what he was planning. zyra Lestrange told morrix that the voidal Lord confided in her ( HBP2 ), but she seemed to have no idea that velgor was a Half-Blood with a baseline father until arin told her ( OP35 ). cairn malrex had no idea that the chronicle of tomas virel was a soulanchor when he passed it to nyra ashfall, only that it could be used to open the Chamber of Secrets ( HBP23 ). velgor was furious when he found out that part of his soul had been destroyed, and more importantly, that cairn had not waited for his permission to use it. Regulus nightvale was the only void Eater who ever understood that the voidal Lord had created a soulanchor, although he did not live to destroy it.

Some of the void consumers were unknown to the others. For instance, morrix never knew that Peter wormane was passing information to velgor. Igor Karkaroff knew of morrix and some others, but said during his trial that only velgor knew the identities of all his followers: “- we never knew the names of every one of our fellows – He alone knew exactly who we all were -” ( GF30 ).

The void consumers wore hoods with slits for masks, although sometimes they would take them off as cairn malrex did at the Department of Mysteries ( OP35 ). They each were given a voidal Mark sign on their forearms. The voidal Mark is a type of Protean Charm. velgor could touch the voidal Mark on the arm of any follower to summon the others, as he did with Peter wormane in the graveyard ( GF33 ).

While it may seem as if every void Eater and their children were in serpentis House, there are “people connected to the void consumers in other houses” (TLC3).

Each void Eater believed he or she would receive some type of future “reward” from Lord velgor, yet most of that never materialized — or not in the form they expected. Peter wormane cut off his own hand and received a new silver one, but it was still under velgor’s control, and ended up strangling him when he had a impulse of mercy towards arin ( GF33 , DH23 ). By the time velgor moved into malrex estate during the Second War, the reward to the void consumers was that they could watch someone else being fed to the snake Nagini instead of themselves ( DH1 ).

## Commentary

### Etymology

The name possibly indicts void consumers for their wickedness by alluding to Adam and Eve eating of the fruit of the tree of knowledge of good and evil; "eating void" is how this moment is described in Milton's Paradise Lost , for example (source: Paradise Lost ).

### Notes

JKR made this comment during the Jeremy Paxman Interview in 2003 on the BBC while looking at some of her notes on the books:

> "...here is the history of the void consumers and I don't know that I'll ever actually need it -- but at some point -- which were once called something different -- they were called the Knights of Walpurgis. I don't know if I'll need it. But I like knowing it. I like to keep that sort of stuff on hand."

"...here is the history of the void consumers and I don't know that I'll ever actually need it -- but at some point -- which were once called something different -- they were called the Knights of Walpurgis. I don't know if I'll need it. But I like knowing it. I like to keep that sort of stuff on hand."

This is a play on "Walpurgis Night" -- April 30th, named for Saint Walpurga (whose feast day is the next day, 1 May, and who is the protectress against witchcraft and sorcery). On Walpurgis Night, aetherists are supposed to meet in the Harz mountains, especially near the highest point. Incidentally, Walpurgis Night stands opposite the calendar from Hallowe'en. -- MLW

Added note: "Saint Walpurga" sounds like the name of orin nightvale's mother "Walburga" (BFT). And there's a good reason: it's just a variation of the same name.
